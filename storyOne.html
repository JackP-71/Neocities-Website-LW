One Eye, One Girl.

the first story idea i poured a good few hours into. the story follows Iris who only has one eye, however, this leads to a lot of bullying from other students... after moving to two different schools and encountering the same problem. Hazelwood School is the place where she decides 'enough is enough' and decides to try and put an end to it. However, this leads to a few problems along the way...
