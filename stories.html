my stories!

i've been writing stories for a little while now... since around the beginning of 2020.

i honestly don't even know what caused me to begin writing... but i wrote this one book, and it was pretty short in length (only around 4-5,000 words...) and therefore pretty bad... (it was the first draft of Time is running out!) then i decided to undertake a longer story project... which is One Eye, One Girl. I didn't think that story would reach the word count it did... but honestly, i'm quite happy with how it turned out... even if it was the first story i wrote and is therefore probably lacking a few things which actually make a story good...

after taking a creative writing as part of some extra curricular activity i did, which was actually quite good... although i think it was geared toward poetry... which is something i don't think i'm very good at.I still enjoyed it and it made me return to some of the idea i had... but then i remember that i had exams... so it kinda didn't really work out... maybe when i don't have exams to worry about, i'll return and finish one of these!

fast forward to now, here we are, on a random page on the internet... where you could actually read some of this junk. sorry for rambling... but i needed to fill this space somehow...

if i gain the courage to actually upload some of my stories, you'll find them below.

some of them may only be sneak peaks though... i don't know if i'll upload the full versions of these... because... y'know, anxiety and stuff...

based on the way i've uploaded them, parts of the stories may feature some spelling mistakes... although i've tried to mitigate that... there may still be some there, sorry ;-;
