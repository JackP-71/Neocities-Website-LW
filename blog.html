(05/03/2024) - (Trying) Online Gaming?

Ok, usually I would give online gaming a miss, but now I see why it's the norm for some people.

After adding a few friends on Xbox, I am slowly beginning to see the appeal of online gaming. Usually, when online games are mentioned, I tend to stay away, because usually, they aren't the greatest... But after giving it a go, I can kind of see the appeal of it, because it allows people to make new friends, or continue to stay in touch with people they may not have seen in a while. But in this case, having played Forza Horizon 4 (the best one!) with a friend of mine, I can say that online gaming can create new memories in a game you may be beginning to drive away from.
