One Eye, One Girl

Currently reading: Chapter 19

Previous Happenings:

well... it was unexpected what just happened... hopefully everyone's ok...

I regained some consciousness because I stumbled back on my feet “What… What j… just happened” I managed to stutter, my eyes were barely open and when my eye did eventually fully open, I saw both my friends lying on the floor, both against the walls of the room, it was weird though, my eye felt more tired than usual, it felt weird though. I couldn’t really place my finger on it though (otherwise I would poke my eye, and that would hurt very much). It must have been at this point that I must have fully regained my senses, because I was bought back to reality. Both my friends were lying on the floor. Either one of them or both of them could be dead.

“Beatrice?” I asked, walking over to her, when she didn’t wake up, I tried again, but slightly louder “Beatrice!” She stretched her arms, but that was the only sign of life I was seeing at the moment.

I walked over to Willow, and called her name, of course, it was just like every morning at Hazelwood, she wouldn’t awaken, I tried shouting her name just like I had with Beatrice but that didn’t seem to work. A tear starts to form in my eye as I realise that both of them could be dead.

Archie must have heard me shout his sister’s name because he rushes into the room and he asks in a rather loud voice “Is everything alright?” I don’t want to give him the answer I am about to give him, but it is the right answer. Whilst repressing the thoughts that my friends could both be dead, I manage to stutter a reply

“No… I… No. I don’t think so…” I look over to him from where I am, crouched next to Willow and trying to wake her, to see if he has any sort of plan to try and resurrect my friends from what could potentially be death. His eyes widen as he looks at me, but all he says is

“I… This… wasn’t part of the research…” After he had said this, he must have broke free of the thoughts that his younger sister, whilst seemingly annoying to him, could be dead. “Ok… I don’t see how we are supposed to reverse the spell now… Both of your friends could be… could be… could be dead.” I stand up and I realise that standing here, mourning our friends who may or may not be dead, isn’t actually going to do anything…

“Surely there’s something we can do” I say “Can we not do it, two of us, what’s the difference?”

“I guess you’re right…” As soon as he had said this, my spirits were lifted, however, they came right back down as he added “but I have a slight feeling that we need three people to bring it back”

“What do you mean?” I ask

“Well… you three had reversed the spell, which you have obviously done” he says as he points to Beatrice who is lying on the floor still, powerless and helpless

“Wait…” I interrupt “You knew this was going to happen” my anger starting to rise ever so slightly as I point to both my friends who are both collapsed on the floor “and you didn’t even tell us?” I ask, because I remember him leaving the room without warning just as we had started to reverse the spell

“Well… Yes… But…” He starts “But I just remembered it at the last minute”

“Of course you did…” I say, quite annoyed, he, most certainly, could have told us that we were going to be thrown at the walls by a shockwave way before we had started to reverse the spell.

“Anyway…” Archie continues “Because three of you reversed the spell, I think that three of you may need to bring it back”

“Great…” I say, we were defeated. Defeated by some stupid mystery of history from like 70 years ago, what were we supposed to do now, I would have to return to school, with no friends, because they could very well be dead. Killed by me for making them both come with me on the stupid little quest, which in the end didn’t work. In fact, it probably did the opposite of work. It failed. Completely.

I couldn’t be defeated though… There was one small thought in my head, which seemed to be lost in the sea of doubt flowing through my brain at the moment, a small island in the middle of the sea called hope. Doubt was quite a choppy sea, filled with waves and the weather wasn’t helping much either. It was cold, dark, and extremely rainy. No sun in sight. I tried to sail towards it, but the doubtful thoughts held me back, no matter how hard I tried to sail, It wouldn’t work. I couldn’t do it. I couldn’t save my friends. I did keep trying, however, a very miniscule amount of progress was actually being made, you could probably use a microscope to measure how far I had managed to traverse through the sea.

I continued to wade through the waves and combat the vicious weather, however as I sat down on the cold stone floor, day dreaming, about school, what school would be like without my two friends, much more horrid, that was for sure. I wasn’t able to look into the future, but I could probably already tell that for the rest of my time at Hazelwood School, it would just be bullying. That was all I could see. It was this thought of bullying that brought me closer and closer to the island of hope, just as I was about to reach it, Archie interrupted my thoughts by saying

“You know what? It might actually be possible to bring back the spell with just the two of us”

And just like that, Archie had practically pushed my metaphorical boat to the island of hope. I had conquered the Sea of Doubt.

To reverse the spell, Archie had told me that we both needed to drink 3 drops of the mysterious purple liquid and then chant out the words he had shown us previously and we didn’t need to join together or anything like that (unlike the part where we had to reverse the spell), and I was quite glad, if we had to do that, it would have been quite weird.

So first, I drank three drops of the liquid, it didn’t seem to have a flavour to it, and yet, it tasted sour, thankfully it didn’t burn my insides out. After Archie had drank three drops of the liquid, he then nodded to me and we begun the process to bring the spell back, hopefully this time it wouldn’t end in potential death

We both chanted the words that were written on the piece of paper and when it was done, I stood anxiously and waited for something terrible to happen.

I waited for what felt like minutes, although I was sure it was just a few seconds. I eventually figured that nothing bad was going to happen, so I decided to ask Archie one question, one question which would decided if we had done it properly, and potentially changed the school.

“Have we done it? Did we bring the spell back?”

“Well… I’m not entirely sure, nothing seems to have happened…”Archie started to say

“Why… Should it of?” I was instantly nervous, if something was to happen, I certainly didn’t want to be thrown back to the wall, where I could have been knocked out or killed.

“No… or at least, that’s what it says in my research, I was hoping these two would have at least showed some signs of life by now… But I guess they must have been thrown back at the wall at some speed…”

Nearly as soon as Archie had said this, I heard a breathe coming from my left, I looked over and saw Beatrice stretching her arms and then her legs, her eyes opened and I noticed something slightly strange.

Archie then asks his sister whether or not she is ok, Beatrice manages to stutter a response, at least she was still alive, I couldn’t say the same for Willow yet (hopefully it was yet, it couldn’t be certain, it can’t be, not yet anyway).

=====

It takes a few minutes before Beatrice eventually stands up and tells us both confidently that she is alright and still alive. However all eyes were now on Willow’s body, which was still lying there, what were we going to do, we couldn’t leave it here. Beatrice walks over to her and, obviously not realising that I had already tried, tries to call her name, her voice gets progressively louder with every call, however she eventually realises that it’s no use, a tear forms in her eye, as she says “No… She… She can’t be dead…”
