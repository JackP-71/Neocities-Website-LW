One Eye, One Girl

When I asked Beatrice about how I could help with the project, she told me a few things that could be done, since the end of the lesson was only about ten minutes away, I decide to spend the rest of the lesson trying to read up on some of the things we may be able to add to the piece of paper we had been given.

Doing this had actually distracted me from what had happened at break time, well… until I just mentioned it, the ten minutes quickly turned to five because we were so focussed on the project. And before we knew it, the five minutes quickly turned to two and the teacher was telling the class that it was time to tidy the classroom. Before I began to help with the tidying, I looked down at the piece of paper, about a quarter of the page was filled with lots of information.

I then stood up from the desk and began to tidy some of the stationery away, being careful to avoid going near any of the school bullies, and even if I had no idea where some of the pencils or pens had come from, I still did the job, otherwise I knew we wouldn’t be allowed to leave the lesson. Eventually, when all the stationary was tidied away, the teacher told us all to “Stand behind your chair and if you want to be dismissed, be quiet” I quickly checked my timetable and discovered that I had science next, which was then followed by break time. Me, Beatrice and Willow (and a few others) were silently waiting to be dismissed, but a couple of students still seemed to be talking (would you believe that it was mainly the bullies?), I suppose some people hadn’t quite grasped the concept of silence or just being quiet.

Finally, when everyone did fall silent, we were dismissed from the classroom. I stepped into the corridors and was soon joined by both my friends, then we all walked to science. As we were doing so, I decided to ask a few questions about the project “Why are we doing a project in history anyway… Shouldn’t we be doing some actual work?”

Beatrice responds with “In a way, this is, technically, actual work…”

“What do you mean?” I curiously ask

“Well… It goes towards out final grade,” I then began to wonder why we would be doing a project which impacted our final grade, and why the teacher gave us some rather unusual things to research… Then another question entered my mind,

However, before I have time to ask, this question, Willow asks me what happened during my visit to the head master’s office, “You said, you would tell us after the lesson,” she said, and I suppose I did. So I decide to tell them everything that I could remember.

After I finish speaking, I asks the question that had entered my mind a few minutes previously “Because this project goes towards our final grade, does that mean that it will have a due date?” Beatrice then quickly answers my question by telling me we would have until the end of the month, which was about twenty days.

Once that question had been answered, we continued in silence towards the science classroom.

=====

I’m probably not the first one to notice how the science lessons are quite different to the typical science lessons you are probably used to. This is what played a part in choosing which school I went to next, because I thought it would be interesting to learn about the things which other people may not be aware of. The topics we learn in science are quite interesting and even if they seem otherworld – like, they are supposedly real. I would love to try to figure out whether one of the things we learn, but since we are barely allowed to leave the school. I don’t think that will happen any time soon.

=====

We are the first ones to walk into the science classroom, and our science teacher: Mrs Campfield says “Wait a second, you three” She then picks up a pile of sheets and then hobbled over to where we were waiting and hands each one of us a sheet of paper, she tells us to “Read the piece of paper and then answer the questions on the back of the paper” We then all begin to head to our seats, when I sit down I begin to study the sheet.

Last week, I remember the teacher telling the class that we would begin to learn about a new topic, I think I remember her saying that this topic would be about the differences within the human population, or something similar to that, and looking at the sheet of paper, it seemed that today would be the day that we would be learning about that new topic.

This was because when I read the information on the piece of paper, it said something about how the some of the personality traits you displayed depended on your eye colour. I found this rather peculiar because, up to this point, there hadn’t really been anyone who displayed exactly the same characteristics. Although, reading into it further, I realise that there are only certain personality traits were seemingly common between eye colours.

After I had read over the piece of paper a few times, I look around the classroom and it’s rather quiet for the amount of students here, but that seemed to always be the case for science lessons.

I look back down at the paper and begin to answer the questions, as I did this, I heard a familiar voice ask a question “What’s written on the piece of paper, is it true?” It was Beatrice. The teacher then responds with

“We shall learn more about this in today’s lesson” So this might be quite an intriguing lesson.

Once I had finished answering the questions, I notice just how much information was on this small sheet. And I also figured that we might be able to add some of this to our history project, considering we ran out of ideas for anything else to add, this could be quite good in terms of information. I then discovered that whilst this piece of paper seemed to have every bit of information typed onto it, it didn’t seem to have everything… for instance, this piece of paper told me what would be the case if you had a certain eye colour (and even differently coloured eyes!), and what your personality traits would be. However reason why I say this piece of paper ‘seemed to have every bit of information’ was because it failed to mention anything on what sort of personality traits you would have if you only had one eye. I look up from the piece of paper and raise my hand, the teacher looks over and asks “What is it, Iris?” I was about to ask my question. Then all of a sudden, just as usual, the classroom becomes louder when the school bullies all walk into the classroom, “Sorry we’re late, Miss” Evie says, but I know she doesn’t actually mean it, considering she is late to almost nearly every lesson.

“What’s your excuse this time?” Mrs Campfield says

“We all thought it was lunch time” The red-haired bully replies, even I’ve been at the school for a shorter amount of time, and even I knew lunch time wasn’t for another hour. The teacher sighs and quietly says

“I’ve heard worse excuses…” Then she tells them to “Go and take a seat,” she also adds “and you can all stay behind ten minutes” The teacher turns back to me and asks what my question was, but I decide to forget it and say:

“Nothing miss…” I knew that if I was to ask what sort of personality traits you would have if you only had one eye, one of the bullies would make some horrid comment, I didn’t really want to listen to that so I just decided it best to not ask my question.

=====

Usually, at the beginning of lessons, there isn’t really anything we learn because we are recapping what we learnt last time. So, whilst this time wasn’t any different, I was sure I began writing sooner this lesson.

This writing was soon interrupted when the teacher told everyone to put down their pens, and then she began to ask everyone what eye colours they had. She first asked if anyone had blue eyes, a few people raised their hand for this, our teacher then went onto to mention how if you had blue eyes you would show signs of sorrow more frequently than any other eye colour.

She then said “Raise your hand if you have green eyes” A few people, including me, raised their hand for this, she explained that you would appear slightly more jealous than people with other eye colours. She did the same for people with brown eyes, quite a few people raised their hand for this as well, in fact, I think there were slightly more people who had brown eyes than people who had green eyes or blue eyes. and told us that you would generally be more kind and generous.

I was about to begin writing down some of what the teacher had described, but then she asked “I’m curious… does anyone here have different coloured eyes?” As soon as she had asked this, most people began looking around the room, and I saw that one person had raised that hand to this question, that person being Beatrice. The teacher asked her what colour eyes she had,

To which, Beatrice responded with “I have a brown and a blue eye” Yet, I had never noticed that… I pick up my pen and begin writing but from the corner of my eye, I see Mrs Campfield looking around the room.

She looks in my direction for a while before she asks “Raise your hand if you only have one eye” A nervous feeling washes over me. I drop my pen. I look down whilst simultaneously looking around the classroom. Most people were looking in my direction. I raise my shaking hand.

I didn’t have to see it, but I knew that Evie would definitely be looking my way, probably staring daggers at me.

Ironically, the teacher then said how having one eye was seen as unlucky, and to be honest, I felt that was quite accurate. I was thinking about how there were seemingly a lot of bullies and not many friends. Although, I end on a somewhat positive thought to myself, I’m not bothered by what other people think because I can’t change who I am, I am who I am and that is fine.

By the end of the lesson, I had written the quite a few notes, but here were just some of them:

  • If you had blue eyes, you had a negative view on life, people with blue eyes tend to lead a life of sorrow.
  • If you had green eyes, it was a sign of jealousy and greed, you would often go out of your way to try and get things other people have.
  • If you had brown eyes, you would be a kind and generous person, you would generally be kind to others and treat others with respect and generosity.
  • If you had different coloured eyes, it was seen as a sign of good luck and our teacher mentioned how it had the potential to save you from death. If you have two differently coloured eyes, sometimes the eye colour traits will clash and you may end up with two different personality traits.
  • If you have one eye, it is seen as a sign of bad luck, our teacher mentioned how people with one eye don’t last very long.

 

Overall, it was quite an interesting lessons, it was quite strange to discover how the eye colour you have (and the misfortune of having one eye, or the luckiness of having two different coloured eyes) would impact what sort of person you were.

This wasn’t all the teacher had told us though, she had also told us how this had come to be. As it turns out, there was a group of sorcerers and sorceresses who had joined together in an effort to create peace in the world. She explained how the magical beings had managed to find out the eye colour of the people who lived in a certain radius of where they did the spell. Mrs Campfield then went on to say that they did two spells, one to first find out everyone’s eye colour and another one to change everyone’s personality based on their eye colour.

Our science teacher hinted at how there was some speculation as to why they did this, but she didn’t seem to mention much about it. The teacher also said about how people had been researching this strange mystery for some time, these researchers had managed to find out which area had been affected by the spell, (because as it turns out, the radius in which the spell affected was quite huge). They had managed to narrow it down to two places, these two places were a coastal town named Rose Cove and a village, which is actually quite near to Hazelwood School, called Hazelford. The teacher showed us a map and I realised Hazelford is also located near a forest, which some of the students in our school call the Forgotten Forest, whether or not that is its actual name, I’m not too sure.

So, to summarise, it turns out that this group of sorcerers and sorceresses were responsible for changing everyone’s personality traits. I didn’t think it was even possible for that to happen, but I guessed anything is possible when you set your mind to it.
