One Eye, One Girl

Currently reading: Chapter 13

Previous Happenings:

Our protagonist had yet another run in the the school bully... which, thankfully it wasn't as bad as the first two encounters. Then, Iris's free time before lunch time was spent reading some more of the book she had found previously.

The school bell rings shortly after, I was about to head down to the main hall but then I remembered the reading I had done and the notes I had managed to make. So I decide to take my bookmark (but not before taking a note of which page I was on), and say to Willow “Come on, let’s go downstairs”

She doesn’t reply but follows me out of the dormitory anyway.

When I arrive at the main hall, I see Amber and Beatrice already sat at one of the tables positioned towards the centre of the main hall. I tell Willow and we go and join them.

Slightly enthusiastically, Beatrice says “Hello!” Once we arrive at the table, I can tell the fact that she doesn’t have to worry about the homework right now had made her slightly happier than before, unless she found it.

“Hi…” I say as I take a seat, realising I was quite worn out after reading for nearly an hour. I sit down and then ask my friends “What have you all been doing?”

Beatrice ponders upon this for a moment before replying with “I managed to find my geography homework, it was in my dormitory after all that. So I just completed that and then did some revision” She then reassures us “I’ll try not to lose this piece of homework though.” Beatrice then turns to Amber who tells us she had been doing some revision as well of English Literature. Willow said she was doing some reading, which I hadn’t realised, I guess I was focussed on my own reading instead.

When it was my turn to say what I had been doing, I told them that I had done some more reading of the book, which gives me a chance to show the notes I had made. So I take the piece of paper out of my blazer pocket and place it on the table.

Everyone (well, everyone who is sat at the table) looks down at the piece of paper, I then decide to look at it myself. When I do, I notice that seven more notes had been added to the piece of paper, however, it was really only two notes as some of them had been learnt in the science lesson on Monday. However for 90 pages read, I really thought I would have found out more. Maybe I wasn’t writing enough? Or maybe the book really did lack the information we needed? Who knows…

I suppose it was worth realising that some of the information I’d found could be the difference between finding out how they had managed to change everyone’s personality traits or completely ruining the plan I had thought of earlier in the day.

Looking at their faces, I could tell Beatrice was concentrating on deciphering my scruffy handwriting and Amber’s seemed to display a slightly shocked expression. I wondered why…

So far, I have written down the following things on the piece of paper:

  • Blue eyes mean that you lead a life of sorrow
  • Green eyes mean that you are jealous and greedy
  • Brown eyes mean that you are a kind and generous person
  • Different coloured eyes mean that you have good luck and the different eye colours traits may clash and you may end up with two personalities
  • If you only have one eye then it is seen as a sign of extreme bad luck
  • A group of sorcerers and sorceresses had joined together in the late 1800s and created some sort of spell to find out what eye colour everyone had
  • They then created some sort of spell to give everyone different personality traits
  • The group of sorcerers and sorceresses were either formed in a village called Hazelford, or a coastal town called Rose Cove.
  • In the book it says that it is widely believed that the sorcerers and sorceresses were formed in Hazelford, so this could mean that they did their spell in Hazelford, rather than Rose Cove
  • They had to complete two spells to change everyone’s personality traits, one to find out everyone’s eye colour and then one to change everyone’s personality traits according to what eye colour they had
  • In the book it says that researchers have found that they have lived in a slightly small stone building, this is slightly confusing because as far as I am aware, there are no stone buildings in Hazelford, so my only guess is that they must have lived somewhere in the forest which is located next to Hazelford,
  • When the book starts to talk in more detail about the people who started the entire mystery of history, it talks about someone called Zachary Grimstock and it looks like all evidence points to him being the one to make the plan, the plan to change everyone’s personality traits

 

Upon reading through my notes, I was left with a few questions, so I waited a few minutes before assuming everyone had read it and took the piece of paper and found a pencil in my blazer pocket. I then began writing these questions on the same piece of paper:

  • What if this is all fictional and it never actually happened? (Willow originally thought this – but I wonder if it’s actually the case)
  • Was it really Hazelford where they had done the spell to change everyone’s personality traits, or was it somewhere else?
  • How did they manage to find out everyone’s eye colour? I assume it was some sort of spell, but how did they manage it?
  • Why would they have chosen those personalities for everyone? Was there someone the leader of the group of sorcerers and sorceresses really hated?
  • Is it even possible to reverse the spell which they have managed to do? or is it simply impossible.

I had assumed everyone had finished reading the piece of paper, but I was surprised that Willow was looking over my shoulder at it. I put it in front of her place so she could continue reading it.

When Willow passes me the piece of paper, I think to myself, if I can find out the answers to all of the questions that are written on the piece of paper maybe we will have all the information we need about the group of sorcerers and sorceresses and how they managed to change everyone’s personality traits.

I had learnt a bit more from the book which made me remember about how there was a leader to this operation. I don’t know if the book mentions where the group was formed. However, there was one thing I’ve now realised, if it is true.

I had recently read a bit in the book which didn’t realty align with what I had been told, at least, in my head it didn’t really make much sense. One of the things was the fact that the leaders of the group had supposedly changed everyone’s personality traits because he hated someone. But these personality traits don’t really align with that in my head. They don’t really seem like the traits you would want to give to someone you hate. Maybe the sorrow one though…

I look up from the piece of paper, I was too distracted by the research I had done that when I look up, I see that Amber and Beatrice had disappeared. I asked where they were and Willow told me that they had already said they were going to get some dinner. I must not have been listening. Before, I decided to do the same, I add one more question to the piece of paper.

  • Why was one of my friends seemingly shocked at one of the pieces of information I had written down?

Because, surely there had to have been another reason as to why Amber had shown a shocked reaction to the name of the leader of the sorcerers and sorceresses.

Despite there being so many questions that still (kind of) required answers, the temptation to try and fix everyone’s personality traits was still there in my brain. However, first, I head to the canteen to get some dinner.

=====

In fact, for the rest of the week, that was all I could think about. Over the course of the week, I attempted to think of answers to the questions I had written earlier, however, quite a few of these went unanswered.

I wanted to attempt to try and get my friends to help me with the idea of changing the personality traits. Even I, the one who actually wanted to ty and solve this mystery, still wondered whether or not all this actually happened, especially after what Willow had said to me a few days earlier.

I suppose, reading that book gave me the hope I needed that it was real, but I had no idea if this book was non fiction or fiction (because it could have been miscategorised). I checked the library yesterday to see if I could find the village at which the spell to change the traits had happened. Already, progress had been made in this aspect of the search. Reading the book further, I had discovered that it had taken place in a village called Hazelford, previously it had seemed as though it couldn’t be possible, as I didn’t see any stone buildings which was where the book I was reading had mentioned.

The question of where the stone building was located was answered yesterday, when I found another book in the geography section. It was a map of the area which Hazelwood School was located in. The main reason why I picked this book was because the map also featured a map of Hazelford. When I studied it I found that there was quite a large forest separating Hazelwood and Hazelford.

It seemed likely that if there was going to be the stone building, it might potentially be in that forest. Even if I wasn’t sure, it sparked some hope within me because, if this was the case, then it meant that it might be closer than we think. Which would definitely make it easier if we decided to do it.

Upon discovering this, I couldn’t help but realise that it seemed strange… There was just too many pieces of information which linked it to real life… I was beginning to shy away from the potential fact that it could all be a lie… I suppose, there was only one way to find out. It just depends whether my friends want to help me find out…
