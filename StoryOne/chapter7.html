One Eye, One Girl

Currently reading: Chapter 7

Previous Happenings:

After another run-in with Evie, Iris decides to try and learn more about the mystery which she had been taught about in science.

Science wasn’t quite what I expected today either, because we had, once again, moved onto learning about something else. Which wasn’t really what I wanted because I was hoping to learn more about how the group of sorcerers and sorceresses had managed to change everyone’s personality traits.

Instead, we had moved onto genetics, which was probably something else we would be able to add to the project, but it just wasn’t as interesting as the previous lesson. As much as I did try to listen to what our science teacher said, I just couldn’t. So I pulled out the piece of paper in my blazer pocket.

Even though I keep the piece of paper underneath the table, the student, who I think is called Sophie, sat next to me whispers “What’s that?”

“Nothing,” I quickly whisper back, a lie, but it would be too confusing to explain.

I take a quick look around the classroom and see Beatrice also looking around, she sees me and I see her mouth “Are you alright?” to which I nod my head.

After I stop surveying my surroundings (I had seen it plenty of times before anyway), I pay attention to the list in my hand. I spend some time looking at the list I had made, and from the crossed out items of the list, I decide I need to check the ‘Non-Fiction’ section and the ‘Mysteries of History’ section. I’m prioritising those bookcases first because I think I am most likely to find some information in those sections. I didn’t think it would be any use checking the fiction sections because it actually happened, well, at least, that’s what our teacher says.

=====

By the end of the lesson, I hadn’t made many notes, (not when compared to last lesson), I didn’t really view that lesson as the most interesting one we’ve had so far, yesterday’s lesson was much more intriguing than today’s. I look to see what lesson I have before lunch, history.

I decide to bring my science book with me as I would be able to write down the things we had learnt and written down yesterday and the few things I had written down today.

When I arrive at the history classroom, I look around before opening the door, and whilst looking around, I notice that no one appears to be there. I was at least expecting the teacher to be there, but there was no one. I walk into the classroom and hesitantly stand at the back of the classroom for a few moments. I begin to wonder whether I head lunch time or not, these thoughts were put to rest when a few more students enter the classroom, so I go to sit down.

I place my science book on the table and flick to the page I had written all the notes on, ready to begin. It doesn’t take long before Beatrice and Willow arrive and sit down on the row I’m sat on.

“Where’s the teacher” Beatrice whispers, since no one else was talking.

I am about to reply “I’m not sure”, but then the teacher opens the door and apologises for being late. She’s holding a pile of books, which I guessed were history books. She places them on the back table before she opens the cupboard and takes out the projects. When she begins to hand them out, I remember that ours is still left in mine and Willow’s dormitory.

When the teacher returns to her desk, after handing out all of the projects, I tell her “I left our project in my dormitory, should I go and get it?”

Miss Seddon sighs before saying “Well… You’ll have to, because it’s what you need for today’s lesson. And it’s not just yours, it’s also Beatrice and Willow’s,” She tells me to go to my dormitory, whilst trying not to be seen by any of the teachers. I agree and think about all the other times I had to leave a lesson because I had been bullied or some other reason, I walk out of the classroom and set myself two mental goals, firstly, don’t get caught and, secondly, retrieve the history project from my dormitory. It seemed easy in my head, considering when I scanned the corridors when I left the history classroom, there was no teachers there.

I did have a vague idea of which teachers might be roaming the corridors.

This was because teachers who weren’t teaching a lesson during a time where students were being taught, they would have to check the corridors to see if any student was trying to skip their lesson. I only knew this because it was one of the things I had been told on my first day at Hazelwood school.

I began to walk along the corridor, and despite my check of the corridor, it didn’t talk too long before I saw one of the teachers. I don’t know which teacher it was, although, she did have to do a geography cover lesson, and from that, I could tell she was quite strict. So, it wasn’t the person I necessarily wanted to see, especially when I was technically trespassing on the school corridors. I could have tried to hide, but I didn’t see the point in that.

Since the teacher was walking in front of me, I tried to walk quietly behind her, although, she must have heard the sound of my footsteps behind her, because she turned around and saw me. As soon as she had seen me, she asked in a loud, stern voice “Which classroom should you be in?”

I pointed to the classroom, which I had just left a few moments ago, “That history classroom…” I say in a slightly quieter voice than usual (worried or not, I don’t know).

“Ok… Well, go back to the classroom then,” I dare not disagree with this teacher, but I was allowed to leave the class, which gave me the perfect excuse.

“I am going to get some work from my dormitory”

“For that lesson?” She asks.

“Yes.” I answer, my confidence building up again.

“Well then, hurry along because you’re not going to pass your final exams standing in the corridor” Which did make me realise that they may be closer than I initially thought.

“Yes miss” I say, quickly heading down the corridor. I decided not the mention the fact that she was the one who made me stop in the corridor in the first place.

It doesn’t take me long to arrive at dormitory number four, when I do, I quickly go in and grab the project from where I left it last. I don’t spend that much time looking around, because I know Beatrice and Willow would be waiting for me, so I close the dormitory door and quickly head back to the history classroom.

On my way back, I almost bump into the group of bullies, who I guess are trying to look cool by skipping lessons, but, I suppose, that also means that they have a higher chance of failing their final exams, just like that teacher said! So, out of those two options: looking cool or passing my exams, I would have definitely picked passing my exams. I decided to walk another way to avoid them, even if it was the longer way. I suppose it meant that I would most likely avoid being involved in a fight, which was drama I wanted to avoid today (especially after yesterday).

It didn’t take long before I arrived back at the classroom. I walked over to the table and triumphantly placed the project on the table and said “I have the project!”

“You did take a little while,” Miss Seddon says from her desk, “but don’t worry, there is still plenty of time left”

I then apologise for taking so long to get it. Beatrice gives me a comforting smile before she says

“Don’t worry about it, we all make mistakes sometimes” whilst it may be true, it made me wonder what mistakes I was making that led to all the bullying.

=====

We continue working on the history project all lesson, I manage to write down everything from the previous science lesson. I then begin looking through some of the books in the classroom in search of anything I may be able to add to the project. Although, it didn’t take all that long before the feeling of boredom hit me as I ran out of things to write about.

I place my pen on the side of the desk and start to think about lunch time, I think about how I needed to try and search some of the bookcases, I remember saying to myself earlier that I needed to check the ‘Non fiction’ and ‘Mysteries of History’ sections first, as that would be where I would have the highest chance of find the information I was looking for.

Besides, if I failed to find anything relating to the science lesson in those two bookcases, then I would have to probably check the fiction sections as a last resort (That would probably mean looking through, like, two thousand more books). Looking around the classroom again, it appeared that there were a few more pens on the various desks, probably because quite a few people had become bored. Or, potentially, it’s due to the temperature, which seemed to be getting warmer as the day moved on. It hadn’t been too bad during the day, however, it seemed like it had increased in temperature quite drastically in this lesson. Since there was no fan in the classroom, or anything of the sort, it felt as though we were sat in some kind of oven.

It became quite annoying to the point where I couldn’t really focus anymore, I wanted to leave the classroom and stand outside, to stand any chance of cooling down. I looked around the classroom, there was barely anyone working. One of the few people still working was Willow, she looked to be writing some notes from a book she had found, I tried to read what she was writing, but it was hard to read something upside down. Beatrice had also given up, she still had her pen in her hand, ready to write something, but it didn’t look as though she was going to write anything else…

I try to read some more of the books in the classroom, but it’s no use. Miss Seddon must have acknowledged that most people weren’t actually getting any work done, because she says “Right, everyone listen, I can understand as it is quite warm outside” She then checks the time “But, since we still have ten minutes left, I’m going to give you some options, you can either stay where you are and continue working, or you can start to tidy up the classroom.” When I hear the chairs scraping on the floor, it’s clear the second option had been chosen.

So, we begin to tidy away all our belongings and all the resources that had been used. I stand up and start to put some books that we had used, and some which we hadn’t, back on the bookshelf. Beatrice tidies away some of the stationery, and I walk back to the table to tidy two of the books which I had left, because I couldn’t really carry five different books at the same time, or else I’d have risked dropping them.

I’m about to take the book off the table when Willow places her hand on it, I decide to tell her “You don’t have to continue working on the project”

All Willow does is look up at me and says “It has to be done by someone.” I couldn’t really argue with this, so I just decide to leave her to it, I sat back down at the table and waited for the lesson to end.

It took a few minutes, but the bell sounded and we were dismissed. I took my science book and left the classroom and, since it was lunch time, headed to the library. It was finally time to put a plan (long awaited) into action.
