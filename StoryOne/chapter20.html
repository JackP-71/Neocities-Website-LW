One Eye, One Girl

Currently reading: Chapter 20

Previous Happenings:

Upon regaining consciousness, Beatrice and Iris wonder what just happened... however, Willow hadn't woken up yet... had she survied the shockwave resulting from the spell?

Before Beatrice starts to cry, a quiet breathe could be heard, and it didn’t belong to any of us. I looked to see whether or not Willow had regained consciousness, but Willow still seemed dead, she wasn’t breathing.

“No one make a sound” Archie hisses to us, had the breath come from outside? Surely no one else was around at about 2 o’ clock in the morning. No one else should be in the forest. Not at this time. We stay silent for as long as we could, we hear another breath, however this one felt closer, much closer. I turned and saw Willow starting to breathe. I was happy to see that Willow was finally alive, however she had chosen now to regain consciousness. Great timing. The quiet breathe could still be heard outside, we all stayed silent, apart from Willow who was breathing, and then she coughed. I thought it was game over, if we were killed by some random person because of a cough, then great (obviously that was sarcastic, as if I would have wanted that to happen).

Everything in my body began to tense, if someone really was outside, then it could be over at any second. Just as I thought my life would end in some random house in the middle of nowhere, I heard footsteps stepping over a few leaves, they were going away, whatever was outside, my curiosity got the better of me, I quietly dashed to the front door and took a look at what had just walked past, it was a wolf. A full moon in the sky, I guess that made sense now.

I then rushed back to the other room and saw Beatrice with Willow calling her name, asking if she was ok, she didn’t seem to reply, although at least we had seen some sign of life from Willow. She wasn’t dead after all.

When she awoke, her eyes opened, unlike Beatrice, her eye colours appeared to still be the same, however, when she looked up at me, she gave quite a loud scream, “Oh my goodness!”

“What?” Beatrice asks, looking at Willow and then looking at me “Oh… Oh my goodness!”

“What?” I ask them both,

“Do you not know?” Beatrice asks

“Well… Clearly not…” I say

“Well… You… It looks like you have another eye…” Beatrice says, quite shocked

“Seriously?” I ask

“Seriously.” Beatrice confirms “Although, maybe my minds just playing tricks on me”

“I thought my eye felt weird… I guess now I know why” My eye did feel quite tired, although maybe it wasn’t just my left eye, I hadn’t thought of my right eye, I had never seen through it. The only thing I could now see, was that there was quite a lot more to see, now I knew what it was like to have two eyes, and not to have just one eye, at least I could be seen as the girl with two eyes and not just one.

=====

“We should probably head back to school now” I say

Beatrice says, and she wasn’t wrong, we didn’t want it to be sunrise and people start to notice we were gone.

“Yes… You should probably do that…” Archie agrees “Unless you want to try and see if we have managed to get it to work”

“Iris is right… We should probably head back to school” “if we don’t get back to school, then the teachers will keep start looking for us”

“Yeah… Because we can’t leave school grounds without permission” Willow adds

“Ok… Yeah, we should probably start heading back now” Archie said

We step out of the room, I still had no idea whether or not we had actually managed to do it correct, when I had asked Archie, he told me that he didn’t think anything would happen, so there was no physical indication that anything had changed, maybe I would see the change when I arrive back at Hazelwood, or maybe we just have to wait until the morning.

As we headed back through the first room, I was surprised to hear Beatrice say “I’m going to miss you, Archie”

“Well… I don’t think I’ll miss you” He teases, and then after a moment of silence, he says “I’ll miss you too, Bea”

“Hey! Don’t call me that!” Beatrice says “You know I don’t like it!”

“I know… That’s why I said it” Archie replies

We walk through the forest, for what is probably going to be the last time. I was hoping I wouldn’t have to see this path again, it nearly killed us.

=====

When we reach the sign which welcomes us to Hazelford, or in this case, thanks us for driving carefully, even though we weren’t driving, just walking through. Archie tells us “All right… Well… I guess this is where I say goodbye… I’ll probably only ever see one of you again” I could tell he was talking about Beatrice, then he continues by saying “Although… Who knows, maybe I will, maybe I won’t” He was right, the future was an unusual and scary place, you never knew what was coming. He crosses the road and as he starts to walk the other way, back into Hazelford, he rather loudly says goodbye, we all say goodbye back, I wasn’t expecting Willow to say it, but I guess maybe the spell was working.

On our walk back to Hazelwood, Beatrice says “I didn’t think I would see my brother today…” and then she tells us “I also didn’t think magic existed until now…” I guess this whole thing was kind of magic, although I always thought magic didn’t really make much sense, although, now that I think about it, this whole mystery of history didn’t really make much sense.

 

The walk back didn’t take as long as I thought it did, however before I knew it, we arrived back at a familiar sight: Hazelwood School, it felt good to be back, it felt as thought we had been gone forever, even if it was just one night, although, I was certainly not going to miss those boring maths lessons, but at least we were back to some form of safety, I certainly wasn’t a big fan of the unpredictability of the forest. However, I wasn’t too sure about my friends, but all I needed was some rest, I was so tired, I really needed to get some sleep.

When we walked closer, we saw someone, a silhouetted figure walking about on the stone paving leading up to the school. When Willow gasped and told us who it was, I could recognise him instantly

“I think that’s the school gardener”

“I think you’re right” Beatrice says and I nodded in agreement, the gardener must have been checking the plants to make sure they were in their best shape before people arrived (and ruined them).

Since it was just the gardener and not a teacher, we figured that we would have been able to just head back into school without being noticed, or at least attempt to.

So that’s exactly what we did, we continued to traipse towards the school. Soon after we had made it, I had a sudden realisation, we still had the keys, we would have to give them to the gardener without him thinking we took them, I wasn’t the one with the key though, I had a feeling Beatrice had the key, or was it Willow?

“We still have the key, don’t we?” I ask

“Oh… Yes I think it’s in my bag…” Beatrice says, getting her backpack off her shoulders, and starts to look through it, I started to wonder what would happen if she didn’t actually have the key, however, she did eventually find it. I told her that we should probably drop it, when we get near, we hoped we would be able to trick the gardener into thinking he had dropped them.

We started to walk down the stone paving, all the time and effort of not being caught by one of the teachers could be wasted if we mess it up now. We walk towards the dark wooden doors which lead to Hazelwood’s reception area. We had to try to get the gardener to pick up the keys, so we had to hope he would notice us. As we were about to walk past him, he stood up and turned to us and said

“Where do you all think you’re heading too?” He looks at us, obviously expecting an answer, so I quickly think of an excuse

“We couldn’t get to sleep… So we decided to go for a stroll around the village” This was sort of true, but the sleeping part was most certainly incorrect, we never actually tried to go to sleep.

“Wait… So, all three of yous decided to leave the school because you couldn’t sleep.”

“Yes…” I say, not entirely sure which direction this conversation was heading because he either knows we were up to something or he doesn’t.

“Ok… I have a feeling I knew what you three were up to, but I can’t be bothered to take you to the head teacher right not, so if you get caught, don’t mention me.” Whilst he was saying this, Beatrice dropped the keys to the floor, thankfully, they didn’t make that much of a sound.

“Oh wait…” The gardener suddenly says and we all stop “That door needs unlocking, lucky for you, I have the key” and he starts to walk over to the door, we follow him as he checks his pockets for the keys which Beatrice has just dropped, “I can’t seem to find my keys” He says, now slightly flustered, “You haven’t seen them have you?” He says rather quickly

I then look over to where the keys were, and point to them as I say “Are those the keys you’re looking for?”

“Oh… Yes. Precisely.” He quickly walks over to pick them up and he says “Thank you” as he turns the key in the lock, whilst he opens the doors, he mutters “Honestly, these students are going to get into some right trouble one day” I don’t think he realised we had heard because he thought the creak of the door must have drowned out his voice, however, I just decide to ignore this as me and my friends say our final thanks before we head into the reception area.

 

All that was left to do was to head up to my dormitory, which only takes a matter of moments, up the stairs and along the corridor, we stayed quiet and that seemed to work, as we walked down the corridor, I noticed that one of the teacher’s offices surprisingly had the light on, we had all decided to head back to mine and Willow’s dormitory as that was the one which was closest to the entrance.
