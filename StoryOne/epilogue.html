One Eye, One Girl

Currently reading: Epilogue

Previous Happenings:

About a month after the events of the story, the cross country race has finally come around.

Today was the day of the cross-country race! Since Evie was better, the race ended up being moved to another day instead of being cancelled, I guess they mustn’t have expected Evie to make some kind of miraculous recovery. In the weeks coming up to this, we had been practising for a few weeks leading up to it. We even missed a few lessons for it, which was ok… but I wondered whether we were missing anything worthwhile… maybe it would be more on what we investigated about a month ago. Maybe we already knew whatever was being taught if that was what was being taught, well, that’s in terms of science, I have no idea about the other subjects.

Anyway, we spent about two weeks practising for what would take place in the afternoon. We changed into our sports kit and were ready to go. The race would be taking place near our school, so the other schools would be travelling here. Since they weren’t here yet, we decided to get some more practice.

We were only practising on the school field; we would head out to where the actual course was later. I think the route actually passed the small stone building we had to go to earlier, but I wasn’t too sure on that. We would be told later during a briefing with Miss Crossley, our Physical Education teacher.

There was only three of us on the team, I think there was another team for the younger years if they wanted to do it. It was just me, Beatrice and Evie on this team, though.

As we headed into the forested part of the field, Beatrice slowed down to where I was, she was much quicker than both me and Evie, she said “After everything we’ve done. It’s nice to get a breath of fresh air, don’t you think?” I had to agree, especially now we didn’t have anything to worry about anymore. No strange curses or anything like that.

After running for a while in the forest, I begin to wonder where Evie is, then a few seconds later, she passes both me and Beatrice and calls back (in a somewhat mocking tone) “Come on you two! Don’t you want to win? Or are you letting me win?” We decided to quicken our pace after that… as much as I didn’t want to admit it, she was right… The new Evie was definitely better than the old Evie.

=====

About 2 hours after we had finished the last practice session, the other schools coaches pulled up to Hazelwood’s entrance. Before this though, we had the briefing from Miss Crossley about the route we would be taking and I was right, it was through the forest we had ventured through before, although now we’ll be able to see it in the daylight. We would be going past the stone building and further into the forest, doing a loop around and then turning back to head to the entrance. She also told us about strategy, telling us “It’s not just about winning, it’s also a test of determination and strategy” However, I began zoning out at this point. I realize that even though I was racing with one eye still, before I would have been a little self-conscious the other teams were making fun of me. Now, however, I didn’t really care and it felt good. It felt how it should.

There was also another part of the briefing about the scoring, but we already knew that. It was on a points-based system, where 1st place got the highest number of points and then 2nd place got a reduced number of points. I wasn’t really listening because I think we all knew and remembered that part of it.

We met the schools outside the entrance, we said our ‘hello’s. Before I would’ve felt a little nervous about this, but now, it didn’t feel too bad. I even had a little conversation with someone called Penelope from Misthaven Academy. We both wished each other well, but secretly, I hoped I would do a bit better.

It wasn’t too long before we were told to head to the beginning of the track. We walked there in teams, Beatrice and Evie walked on either side of me.

“Do you think we’ll do well?” I decide to ask in an attempt to break the silence between us.

“Well… we spent a lot of time practising…” Evie says “So, I’d be a little annoyed if we didn’t.”

“I think I’m a bit the same…” Beatrice admits.

Looking behind us, I see that there were a few other students alongside the runners for the other schools, usually, when we went to other schools, or the one other time I’ve competed at Hazelwood, you were usually allowed to bring a few friends to watch the race. Last time this happened, me and Beatrice bought Amber and Willow along, and Evie bought Phoebe along with her.

However, as the race was taking place at our school this time, I think the whole year group and maybe some of the younger years were watching us. So… no pressure, then…

It did feel a little weird to walk along this path, it was a little weird to think that Evie had probably hadn’t walked down this path recently, whereas, Beatrice and I had.

=====

It didn’t take too long to arrive at Hazelford, but when we did, there was a bit of a crowd around where the entrance to the forest was. It was mostly students from our school. When we reached the crowd, I looked around, some students were wearing the school blazers over their shirts still and others must have left them back at the school. The weather wasn’t too bad, although it had rained the day prior.

Miss Crossley said the race would begin in 10 minutes, so, I look around and find Willow and Amber talking to each other, a bit away from the crowd. I walked over to where they were standing.

"Iris! Good luck!" Amber says as I walk over "Tell Beatrice I said it!"

"Don't worry, I will!" I say.

"Hopefully you'll be able to run quicker now that all those things have been taken off your mind" Willow says, and I guess it was true. I think in practice I was quicker as well... Beatrice didn't say anything, but I wonder if she noticed...

Then Beatrice wanders over and Amber says "Good luck, Beatrice!" so I guess I wouldn't have to tell her now.

Beatrice says "Thanks!" she must have been running on the spot a little to get ready, she was a little out of breath. 

=====

We had a little conversation before a teacher says "The race is going to begin shortly!"

Amber says "Good luck" once more and Willow says it too, only a little quieter. We begin to walk to the start line. Evie begins to walk away from Phoebe and Maisie are standing, Phoebe would have usually been someone I wouldn’t have been too happy to see normally, however, I was glad she was here although, as for Maisie, I still wasn’t too sure… Anyway, I’m sure that both of them would definitely be rooting for Evie and hoping she would win, especially after the illness that ridiculed her which we had helped to cure.

Everyone's now crowded around the start line... or where it would be... instead it was just a signpost... but it was good enough for a small competition. Each of us gave each other good luck and then the time came.

I looked back at the crowd and then in the direction of the school building, everything bad was gone now... it would be good if I won the race, but I had kind of already won, what with no more bullying even if it did feel a little weird... anyway, enough about that, it was time for the race to begin.

We lined up. Ready to go. There was only about 9 of us (3 of us from each school). but it was still going to be a good race regardless, no matter the outcome.

Having never seen the course in daylight, I wasn’t too sure what to expect, but I had a rough idea because we had seen it on a map during the briefing.

The teacher counted us down.

3.

2.

1.

Go!

Off we went, into the forest.

Evie, me and a few other runners stayed towards the back and few others, including Beatrice, set off at a much quicker pace.

Now, I knew this was going to be one of the longer courses we would be racing this year, it was also forested all the way, which made things a little more difficult, especially with the path being so narrow. Although, we had had some practising on the forested part of the field earlier, which had a fairly narrow trail. Then again, there was only 3 of us instead of 9 of us now. Right now, the path may as well have had 20 of us running on it, because that’s what it felt like.

It seemed the rest of the competition was a little fierce because the most of the runners from other schools seemed to be further ahead. Beatrice seemed to be doing a good job at fending them off, but she, along with me and Evie were told to conserve our energy at the beginning at once we reached the clearing in the forest to turn around, that’s when we were told to speed up. I wonder whether Beatrice actually remembered that’s what we were supposed to be doing.

=====

It didn’t take too long before we reached our first ‘obstacle’: a small mud bog. It was probably to be expected because of the weather yesterday, but it was still annoying to see. Even though we had to slow down slightly to cross the mud bog, the runners in front still managed to spray me with some mud… which was a bit irritating... Evie gave a little groan as she was also sprayed with the kicked-up mud. Other than that, it didn’t take too long to trudge through it due to the bog being fairly shallow.

A few minutes later, we passed the stone building, which somehow still didn’t have a name in my head. Just as I remembered, along the path were a plethora of roots, threatening to trip us up. I wondered whether anyone else had discovered the building in our absence. I wonder whether Archie had returned there.

=====

About 3 or 5 minutes later, I begin to lose track of time when I’m running, we arrived at the clearing in the forest. Some of the people ran past us to head back. Me and Evie arrived at the same time, under my breath I told her “Come on, last stretch now!”

“We’ve got to pick up the pace now!” Agreed Evie as we turned around to begin the race back.

Picking up the pace wasn’t too bad, although I could see up ahead, Beatrice was beginning to falter behind. I knew I had to try and get some points for our team, so I continued onwards.

I began passing some of the other runners, the girl I met earlier seemed to be quite far ahead… Maybe she was our real enemy in this race. Although there seemed to be quite a few grey aertex t-shirts ahead… Maybe everyone from Misthaven Academy proved a threat to our points.

Upon catching up with Beatrice, she told me, through her heavy breathing “Maybe I should’ve listened to the teacher…” She was right… she seemed to be really tired. However, it was what she said next that baffled me a little. “Iris,” I turn around to make sure she was the one who said my name. I slow down a little as my friend continues to speak “Maybe, le… let Evie win?” at first, I was wondering why. After considering, all that had happened in those past few weeks, I guess it made sense. She had basically been bed-ridden for about a week, so it would have been good for her to win the race out of all of us. However, looking behind, she seemed to still be a little far behind. Maybe she couldn’t do it?

I couldn’t slow down now though… so I pushed on. I couldn’t really look back anymore now, the roots had returned for a vengeance and they almost caught me out, thankfully, I didn’t trip over any of them.

Upon passing the stone building though… I looked back, Evie was beginning to pick up the pace now, she had just passed Beatrice. Should I let her win? That was the pressing question as I continued to run.

I had made it to about fifth place and was now fighting for fourth with Penelope. There seemed to be a blonde-haired runner wearing a light blue sports t-shirt in front, but I wasn’t sure which school that was. Whoever it was, they seemed to be quite far in front.

Through her fast breathing, she said “I’m sticking by what I said earlier… I’m going to beat you.” She previously told me that she was at least going to try and get more points than me on the scoreboard this year. I knew it was a small threat, done as a joke. But now I was slightly worried it was actually going to happen.

In our small competition, we managed to pass someone else, now the stakes were raised. We were now racing for the podium… or metaphorical podium… our school didn’t have one. Well… I should say we were fighting for the podium, because a red-haired girl in an orange sports t-shirt passed us. Evie had now made it into the top 3. I was happy to see it, but if I was fighting for fourth place, where was Beatrice? She was the fastest of us, so she should’ve been further in front… Maybe that’s what happens when you don’t listen to the teacher.

Passing through the mud bog, both me and Penelope got sprayed with kicked-up mud from Evie. Thanks for that one, Evie…

Although… now we had passed the mud bog, that meant that we were quite close to the final stretch, so I willed my already tired legs to continue running as quickly as they could.

The sound of cheering became closer.

This was going to be close.

Penelope was by my side.

Evie was in front.

I don’t know who was behind… I hoped it was Beatrice.

Then, the sound of cheering increased in volume once more as I passed the finish line… or the sign saying ‘finish’ on it, the question was, was I fourth or fifth?

I came to a stop at the side of the entrance and fell to the pavement. My breathing was going at an incredible rate and the blood was singing in my ears. I couldn’t really hear what the teachers were saying. Although at the moment, it didn’t matter. All I knew was I had tried my best. That’s what mattered most.

Beatrice sat by my side once she had left the forest. I had no idea where she had placed, but hopefully it would be good for the team.

“I… I probably… should have… listened to Miss Crossley…” Beatrice says through her breaths. The strategy she suggested did seem to work for me and Evie, maybe if Beatrice had listened she might have done a bit better.

After a while, I regain my breath and ask Beatrice what’s happening now. The teacher all seemed to be talking to each other talking whilst all the runners sat on the pavement, some were asking for water from the classmates. I, too, was quite thirsty, but it didn’t matter to me at the moment. The main thing I wanted to know was how our team did. Beatrice just said “I think they’re calculating points or something…”

“Maybe… Although, me and this other girl did come quite close… I think it was probably a photo finish for us two” I say.

“Evie did quite well I saw”

“Well, it took me a little bit of time to process what you said… but I wasn’t even thinking about it until she passed me.”

“If she was still a bully, you’d never hear the end of it now!” Beatrice jokes. It was like that at the beginning, at a previous race, I was quite nervous of where I needed to place in relation to Evie, because I knew she’d mock me for wherever I positioned in that race, whether it was in front of behind, she would have mocked me either way. Anyway, I heard “Ha, you came last!” for about a week after that race happened. Even if I didn’t come last, I was literally two places behind her, and there were another two girls behind me. So, her logic was a little flawed there.

=====

It took a little while, but the results of the race were in.

“It was quite close this term!” one of the other teachers said, looking at their school coat, it was the physical education teacher for Misthaven Academy.

“Indeed, it was, and now it’s time for the results!” the other teacher said, from the school I have no idea of the name. “As is tradition, the teacher whose school is nearest shall be reading out the results, which in this case is Cass… I mean, Miss Crossley” Maybe some schools referred to their teachers by first name, we did it by surname, so it was a little odd to hear our teacher’s first name.

Miss Crossley takes a piece of paper, “Ok… So, it was quite a close race this year, we even thought we had a draw at one point in our discussion, however it was proved this was not the case.” I wondered whether she was referring to me and Penelope, unless there was another draw. “Firstly, I will apologise to everyone for the later date of this race… but we had to postpone it due to unforeseen circumstances” she looks over at Evie when she says that and Evie grimaces slightly. “Now, I won’t make you wait any longer, girls, if you could stand at the front.”

All the runners walk over to the front of the crowd, it was a little weird at first… but I grew used to it. We stand in our teams and await the results.

“Ok… so, in third place, Crystal Wood School!” There was a small applause for this… There weren’t many students from that school after all.

“Second place goes to Misthaven Academy!” There was a bit more applause for this.

“And then… first place, by only 1 point, is my Hazelwood Team!” There was a lot more applause for this. We had actually done it…

We had won! Evie said “Well done, everyone!” and we all hugged, it felt a little weird to be hugged by someone who used to be my bully… I still wasn’t really over that…

But there was still one question which needed answering… who came fourth in the race, me or Penelope?

I saw Penelope already talking with one of her teachers, so I break away from our group as Amber, Willow and Phoebe join and walk over to Miss Crossley.

“Miss Crossley?”

“Yes, Iris, well done on the running today!”

“Thanks… but I’m not too sure on the positioning.”

“Well… I have to agree with you, because we weren’t too sure either. It was quite close between you and one of the Misthaven students I believe.”

“That’s what I was wondering… I think one of us was fourth, but I wasn’t sure who.”

“Are they discussing it over there” I wonder aloud.

“I’ll go and check.” My teacher says, but I decide to walk along anyway.

They were in the middle of discussing it, it turned out, and turns out, since the teachers couldn’t decide between then, they asked the crowd, and the crowd said that I was first out of the two of us… But I felt that was probably heavily biased… so, there wasn’t really a definitive answer… which was a bit annoying.

I was about to return to where our team was, when Penelope said “Wait up!” I turn back “It’s Iris, right?”

“Yes?” I say.

“I don’t think any of us knew what happened to our positions during the race… But, next time, I’m going to try to win for my school next time…” which I would have to see if that was going ot happen next term “But good race today anyway?”

“Yep… we had a good race… maybe next time we’ll get a definitive result?”

“Hopefully!” Penelope says as we shake hands.

As I return to the group, I still realise that we hadn’t really got a definitive answer as to whether the curse had been lifted… maybe this race was destined to be the same way.

I wonder whether the results of the race would have been different if the curse hadn’t been lifted.

Who knows?
