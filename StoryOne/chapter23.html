One Eye, One Girl

Currently reading: Chapter 23

Previous Happenings:

Was Evie really dead..? and if she was, was there anything our protagonists could do to save her?

I had thought about it all night. Though, that hadn’t worked.

Having thought about the life I would now live, it felt rather odd. I mean, having been bullied my entire life and now… well, now there was nothing.

The mornings were much quieter, when it was time for breakfast, I headed down to the main hall and where I would expect to find a few people talking. There was nothing. No one was talking and it didn’t feel right. Obviously it wasn’t too busy in here first thing in the morning, but when the school bell rang, you would expect to hear the chatter of the students as they headed down the stairs to receive breakfast. Now? There was nothing.

Not too much was going to happen today, unlike all of the other days. It wasn’t right. It shouldn’t be like this.

Even when my friends arrived, there still wasn’t anything we could find to talk about. Which was quite unusual, even if it was just about the school day which lay ahead. School had just changed completely now. I was now hoping that there was at least something we could do about it.

I wanted to say something to my friends… but after a few minutes, I still couldn’t think of anything to mention, it couldn’t have effected our memories could it? I hope not…

All I was hoping to do was remove the bullying, not the soul of the school…

I checked my timetable, but it was the same lessons as usual, so at least some things don’t change.

======

Due to how quiet it was the morning, I wasn’t really expecting anything to happen today, other than the regular lessons we had. However, the day took an interesting turn when Phoebe walked over to our table in English (the second lesson of the day) and said “I have something I need to tell you”

Now, normally since she was one of the bullies, this would have probably been the part where she would make some kind of horrid remark, but instead, after I asked what she had to say, she continued with “Ok, I don’t think I’m supposed to say anything, but I don’t really care what the school wants me to do. Anyway, in contrast to what the school is leading you all to believe, Evie isn’t actually dead.”

I certainly wasn’t expecting to hear that today!

Although, it was quite confusing to hear for two reasons especially, one being that she said that she thought she wasn’t supposed to say anything, I suppose I could ask her about that at some point. And the other reason being that the teachers weren’t the sort of people you would expect to lie.

I was speechless for a few moments, trying to understand the news, I didn’t think of the possibility that this could be one irritating trick, just to be sure, I ask

“How do I know if you’re lying?”

“I share a dormitory with her, that’s how I know.”

“Ok… But how do you know she’s still alive?” Phoebe stays silent, and I’m almost certain she isn’t going to give me an answer, but she eventually replies.

“During the night we have conversations. In fact, she seems rather ill, her voice doesn’t sound like it once did.”

Beatrice joins in with the conversation “When did you notice this change?”

“In her voice?”

“Yes…”

“Well… I mainly noticed it on the evening of the day before the assembly, announcing she was dead”

Me and Beatrice exchange glances, I was quite confused because how would school think she was dead

In the end, because of my silence, Beatrice asks “But how would they know so soon? Surely it would take a few days before they realise, right?”

“Well, I don’t actually know… I didn’t say anything to the school, so I can either imagine they just assumed she was dead, or Maisie said something, she can be quite dramatic sometimes.“ We must still look quite confused because Phoebe tells us to come and see for ourselves, at least it was break time now.

I wasn’t sure where Willow was, she must have headed down to the field already, or she was waiting at the main hall. But we had some investigating to do first before we could join her.

=====

When we arrive at Evie and Phoebe’s dormitory, Maisie must share her dormitory with someone, I wonder who that unlucky individual is… Phoebe opens the door to reveal practically the same dormitory as everyone else, although there were a few changes, like the different things on the table near the window, which was also in the same place, and unlike mine and Willow’s, there seemed to be a few things scattered across the floor, I suppose I could imagine Evie being unorganised. We look to the bed where Evie is lying down,

The former school bully says “She must have dozed off again…” Beatrice asks Phoebe whether this is normal, to which she responds with “Yeah, it’s kind of become a common occurrence now…”

Phoebe walks over to Evie’s bed, almost as if she’s about to wake her, but at that moment, the dormitory door opens and the school nurse walks into the dormitory, she says hi to us, so we say “hi” back, She then asks Phoebe something quietly, What could that be about? I would have to try and find out later. Phoebe nods and whispers something back, and then I hear a whisper in my ear, “What do you think that could be about?” It was Beatrice, so I just tell her that I didn’t know.

The school nurse walks over to where Evie is lying and checks if she is ok, which I think is quite weird to check if a dead person is ok. Unless, what Phoebe is saying is true and she is still alive. When the school nurse eventually leaves the dormitory, Evie wakes up, although her eyes barely open. Phoebe asks how she’s doing, Evie quietly manages to say “I… don’t think it’s… much better…”

The room goes silent for a few moments before Evie quietly grumbles “You… were probably the… cause of… this…” She must have caught sight of us. Now, I had to pick one of two options, either acknowledge that we knew that we were at fault, or pretend we weren’t… respond or stay silent… what to do? In the end, the answer is fairly obvious, I stay silent. Not daring to tell her of all people, she could still be a bully, albeit powerless.

Beatrice whispers “Do you have anything to say?”

“No… No. I do not.” I whisper back.

After that conversation, I immediately feel shame wash over me. I probably should have told the truth… I should’ve told the truth.

=====

A few hours had passed, now I was sat in a science lesson, far less interesting than the lesson about the eye colours which initially started this whole thing. I wasn’t thinking about science though, instead, I was still thinking about how I should have told the truth to Evie... I should tell her… I will tell her… Just after the final lesson, which this was. All I had to do was wait until this lesson was over, then I could tell her… Just 20 minutes left…

When those 20 minutes had ended and some more science stuff had been written down. It was time to confess what I knew. So I began to head to Evie and Phoebe’s dormitory. To tell the truth.

I began to slowly head down the corridor, wandering what was happening to Evie, what had we done to make her that way? But that wasn’t the only question on my mind as I sauntered down the corridor, I was also thinking about what I would actually say to her. If she was still alive. Obviously something which would explain how I thought we had somehow caused her illness, whatever that illness may be.

When I arrived at the dormitory, my hand reached up ready to knock on the door, but then I suddenly stopped. Hesitating. Hesitating about what though? I wasn’t nervous, was I? I would say that it wasn’t easy knocking on what I always knew as the bully’s dormitory door, even though I knew she wouldn’t answer and I knew she wasn’t much of bully anymore, because kind of couldn’t be.

In the end, I eventually build up the courage to knock on the door. So that’s what I do.

A few moments later reveals that Phoebe is the one to answer the door.

“Oh, Iris… I wasn’t expecting to see you here again today?” Then in a quieter voice, she adds “I though it would be Maisie, but truth be told, I’m kind of glad it isn’t.”

“Yeah… I now realise that I should have told Evie something” and I guess also to tell Phoebe too…

When I walked into the dormitory, nothing had changed, not that I was expecting it to. I look over to where Evie is. She must have fallen asleep again. Phoebe asks what it was I wanted. So, I told her that I wanted to speak with Evie.

She then tries to wake her, which is something the school nurse made look much easier, I can tell. Eventually, Evie begins to say “stop it”, so we now know she’s awake. Phoebe then tells her that I have something to say. She reluctantly says “What… is it… then?” I am about to say what I had been thinking about these past few hours, but I began to get nervous… I’m not too sure why, because it didn’t seem like she was a bully anymore, or maybe that’s what she wanted me to think.

Even so, I knew if I didn’t get the truth out now, I never would. So, I say “You know what? You were right, I do think we are to blame for what has happened…”

“Essentially killing me”

“Yes…” At least she isn’t dead, which is what we initially thought, “But don’t worry, because we are going to try and resolve it.” I attempt to reassure her.

“If you caused this… Then there isn’t much hope… Is there?” Ok, maybe still a bit of a bully, maybe she’s the only one who hasn’t changed much?

=====

After leaving the dormitory, I headed back to my dormitory. I needed a rest after all we had discovered today.

I mean, Evie being alive after we had been convinced she was dead was something I wasn’t expecting to believe. But, as much as I don’t want to admit it, I’m kind of glad she’s ok, even if she isn’t feeling her best at the minute, but hopefully we rectify that issue soon… All I needed to do now was formulate a plan… However, there was one small problem… After realising what Evie had said, I wasn’t sure we could do it… Thinking about it, we had managed to make quite a big mess of it all. Evie may have still been alive, but she wasn’t the best at the minute…

=====

When I arrive at my dormitory, I sit down on my bed and wonder what to do about it all. What could I do, even Evie thought there wasn’t much hope.

What was there to do though? I mean, I was quite sure that there wasn’t anything written telling us how to do undo this spell or reverse the curse, I suppose I still need to figure out a name for it. And that was just one thing on my mind, there were still thousands of thoughts floating around my head. I needed to concentrate on one thought… But with school going on at the same time, I couldn’t really mainly focus on the job at hand.

I stood up and teared out a blank piece of paper from my science book, another piece of homework I needed to worry about, and knelt down next to the table by the window. I decided to write down the main things I needed to focus on right now.

In the first few minutes, the list had about 2 things on it, but now it had 5 things on it. The 5 things I can actually pull from the sea of thoughts in my head…

  • Somehow we have to try to make Evie better, but how do we do that?
  • We’ll probably need to somehow find out how the reverse the spell… Or curse… Which One?!
  • I’ll have to find out what’s actually wrong with Evie, she might know something… I should ask her.
  • English quotation sheet homework
  • A single Geography question

After making that list, I lie back on the bed and my eyes slowly begin to close… I’ll only rest my eyes for 2 minutes maximum… Only two minutes...

=====

I woke up and checked the time, it was definitely much later than I initially thought it was, I thought I would have fallen asleep for about 5 to 10 minutes, but somehow around 45 minutes had passed. Upon realising that it was now dinner time, I headed down to the main hall.

Heading down to the main hall, I noticed that Beatrice and Willow were already there, and checking the time made me realise that I had been in my dormitory

“Beatrice? Willow?” I ask “I don’t think we can do this...”

“What do you mean?” Beatrice asks “Think about it, we were the ones who managed to remove the curse and get you a second eye”

“Yeah… Barely though…”

“It may have been… But we managed to do all of that, so all we have to do now is find out how they actually managed to create the spell…”

“I don’t think we’ll find anything in the library relating to setting the spell up again” At this point I was beginning to think how I felt that we had initially rushed the plan to remove the curse or spell, but Beatrice clearly wasn’t thinking about that. I wasn’t really sure what she was thinking about.

I was quite sure that we couldn’t do it, especially after what Evie had said… there isn’t much hope… if you caused this… Ever since she had told me that, I told myself that I couldn’t do it. I wouldn’t be able to save Evie from whatever she was ill of.

“We’ve somehow managed to remove the spell…” Willow recaps, “and if there was something in the library you found relating to that. Then surely there will be something relating to ” Seeing as Willow used to be the quiet one, I was quite surprised she was trying to get us to do this, and considering she initially thought that this was all a bad idea. If she thought there was hope, then I should’ve thought there was hope.

But I didn’t because Beatrice, who initially seemed confident about it all, was thinking that is wasn’t a great idea to pursue this quest. So, I decide to say to Beatrice “I’m still unsure, but if Willow thinks it’s a good idea, we might have to end up doing it…”

After a few moments of deliberation, Beatrice replies “You know what… it feels weird that Hazelwood is like this, with a completely different soul. I suppose it’s worth a try…” and then she quietly says “Unless it all goes wrong, then it will get worse… and I don’t know what that’ll be like…”

I didn’t really want to imagine what it would be like either, all we needed was for this to go well. Otherwise Hazelwood school would be a completely different place, and considering that it is quite different now, I dread to think what it may be like if we mess this up.
