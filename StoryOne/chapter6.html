One Eye, One Girl

Currently reading: Chapter 6

Previous Happenings:

Iris retrieved the history group project from her history class and continued work on it during lights out to get all the things from the science lesson written down.

The next morning, I was awoken by the deafening sound of the school bell. I go out of bed and changed into my school uniform, which consisted of a white shirt, a pale orange blazer and a dark grey skirt. I walked over to the window to take a look at what was going on, and it turned out, not much.

All you could see was part of the paving leading to the entrance of Hazelwood and the dark green fencing which surrounds it. Behind the fence there are a number of trees, which are seemingly prohibited from passing into Hazelwood – even though they can’t move. There were also all sorts of different kinds of flowers most likely planted by the gardener, some tulips, some roses, some lilies, and a lot more. There were also some hills in the distance which you could barely see because the trees shielded that part of the view. However, the one other thing that wasn’t shielded from view was the sky, I could see the sun peeking out from the distant hills and the sky seemed to be made up of all sorts of mesmerising colours this morning, there were different hues of red, orange and purple.

Once I had looked out of the window, I sat back on my bed. I noticed Willow was still asleep, I was hoping she would wake up of her own accord, but I’d probably have to wake her up at some point, surely she’ll wake up this time. I checked my blazer pockets and found the piece of paper which I had written the categories of the books in the library on previously. I needed to get more pens though… I look around the dormitory for a while, before stumbling upon a box of pens in the cupboard, I didn’t even know they were there, so I took two.

Finding the piece of paper had made me remember that I told myself I would head to the library. So, I eventually settled on the idea that I should head to the library to continue the search for information, because it should be open in just less than an hour.

Once this plan had been devised, I heard the school bell ring again, signalling that we should head down to the main hall to get some breakfast. As it was only quarter to seven, I decided to head down to the main hall, and since lessons started at eight, I should hopefully be able to check at least one of the bookcases.

I looked back into the dormitory before I left and found Willow had somehow managed to sleep through the school bell, I always thought it was so loud (in fact, I think you can hear it from outside the school). But, Willow manages to sleep through it nearly every day. I didn’t really want to wake her, so I just left her, hoping she would wake up later.

I then traipsed through the corridor, looking into the library, but the lights were turned off, so nothing could be seen, except the silhouettes of the bookcases and the shadows of the tables. After that, I walk down the stairs and arrive at the main hall.

It wasn’t very busy this morning, although I guess it wasn’t all too busy in the mornings the majority of the time, everyone probably still wanted to be asleep, but I didn’t. I had a plan. A plan which needed to be completed in order to add more to the history project, in fact, it made me wonder, the history project… I hadn’t actually written anything on it, even though I had taken it out of the lesson.

I wandered across the, pretty much empty, main hall to the canteen. When I did, the cook gave me some porridge. The same old porridge we had every morning. If I had to be honest, the porridge tasted vile, I asked my friends about what we thought the porridge tasted like, Beatrice, for some reason said it tasted what she imagined cardboard would taste like. So, cardboard is what it tasted like. Essentially, it wasn’t the nicest.

The empty hall, especially when you were close to the canteen, seemed to smell of the disgusting food though. I decided to sit at the usual table, which thankfully was further away from the canteen. As I was forcing myself to eat the revolting porridge, I looked around the quiet main hall, other than the few pots and pans clattering in the kitchen, and the odd student talking, it was basically silent. it was kind of nice to see the school so quiet. However, it’s probably not going to stay this way.

It didn’t take too long before I pushed away the bowl of porridge in front of me, I didn’t really want to eat it right now. Truth be told, I just wanted to head to the library so I could start my plan, but I knew I wouldn’t be able to, well… not for another hour or so.

=====

My mind was instantly distanced from the thought of going back to the library when I felt a voice behind me, I turned back to find Beatrice walking over to the table.

“Good morning!” she says which just a little too much enthusiasm for a morning in Hazelwood School.

“Morning…” I say back, slightly less enthusiastically. I then look around the hall once more, it appeared to have filled up with quite a few students than I was expecting in the past five minutes, Beatrice sits down and begins to eat her porridge, as she does this, she asks a question,

“Do you think Willow is still asleep?” I had completely forgotten about that, especially when thinking about the library. I could imagine Willow was still asleep, and, even if I didn’t want to, I would have to be the one to wake her.

I wasn’t too surprised that Beatrice remembered because, like I had said before, it nearly always happened. If anything, I was quite surprised to have forgotten.

“She probably still is; I’ll go and see.” So that’s what I do. I tell Beatrice to keep an eye on my bowl of porridge so it doesn’t get stolen, not that it mattered anyway, I probably wasn’t even going to eat anymore of it.

Walking down the corridor, I see some students walking down the corridor to the main hall. I walk against this crowd of people until I arrive at the door to my dormitory. When I enter the room, I see Willow still lying in bed. Still asleep. I walk over to her bed and whisper her name, which she doesn’t seem to respond to. I say “Willow…” Slightly louder, she flinches, but doesn’t seem to wake up. Ultimately, I just end up shouting her name instead, which manages to wake her up, I tell her “Come on, you have t get up now… You’re going to miss breakfast.”

“Ok… alright… give me a couple seconds.” She gets out of bed, and I leave her to put on her uniform.

I head back down to the main hall and when I enter through the doors, it seems quite full of students now. Everyone sat down with most having conversations with one another. The smell of the porridge is quite strong, much to my annoyance.

I look over to the table I was sat at and find that Beatrice is still sat there. I was glad to see she hadn’t been sitting in silence the entire time, because Amber must have sat at the table. As I begin to wander over to the table, I notice that they are talking about something, I wasn’t sure what.

Amber and Beatrice finish their conversation just as I arrive, Beatrice asks me whether or not Willow was awake now. I told her that it took a few attempts, but I managed to wake her.

We talk about random school things for a few minutes and by the end of it, Willow arrives at the table. I give a small wave and say “Good morning, sorry for shouting at you…”

Willow sits down and says “I should be the one who’s sorry…”

Before she can continue, Beatrice cuts in and says “There’s no need to be” then she leans in, as if someone’s listening and adds “Just between us lot, I wanted to stay in bed, as well.”

I find myself agreeing with Beatrice, I feel as though the school day starts far earlier than it actually should. Getting up at six every morning, except for the weekends when we can wake up at seven, is something I don’t particularly want to do. After three months of doing it though, I’m getting quite used to it. Although, I do still think I have to agree with Beatrice because I would have like to have stayed asleep, for at least another half an hour.

I looked around the main hall once more and found that the majority of the tables were taken. As I was looking, however, I locked eye with Evie. She then stands up and starts to stride over to whether me and my friends are sitting. I tense myself for whatever is about to happen next.

She stops beside me. Looks down and asks “What are you looking at me for?”, which I decide to tell the truth for.

“I wasn’t staring at you, I was just… just… surveying the surroundings…” Although, for some reason, the red – haired bully didn’t seem to like this truth.

She looks around the table and picks up my bowl of not even half eaten porridge and says “You see this…” She says, whilst pointing to the bowl of porridge “This is going to be spilt all down you if you don’t start moving in five seconds”

I hear Beatrice say “Is that really necessary?” but it’s no use because Evie begins to count down.

“Five…” So, I quickly leap up from my chair and “four…” begin to run away from her “three…”. As I run away, I see her shadow chasing me, with the bowl in her hands, “two…” I don’t look, but I have the feeling that the eyes of most of the students are one me and her, as she gets closer “one…” her footsteps grow louder and louder as I begin to run out of breath and slow down.

I try to keep running, but it’s no use running forever… “Zero…”

I slow down and feel everything turn silent. As much as I want to continue running, I know it’s no use. She’s going to catch up with me anyway. So, I reluctantly stop.

Evie’s breathing seems to be even louder than mine, as she stops behind me. Then the porridge is dumped onto my head. I can feel the porridge slowly descend down my hair, there are many conversations which have begun now, but I can hear two voices laughing and cheering. Two voices which I easily recognise. Phoebe’s and Maisie’s. Cheering at me. The me, which has porridge down her hair and school uniform, I remove the bowl from my head and I begin to walk away, in the direction of the lavatories. I was quite irritated that even staring at the school bullies would make them annoyed.

Whilst I was walking to the lavatories, I saw a teacher walk into the hall, I stopped moving as they walked past me, I heard the footsteps stop, and the teacher spoke “What on earth… What is going on?”

I just stood there. Looking around the crowds, hoping that someone else would speak. I had no idea which teacher was standing there, shouting across the hall. I could tell by the voice that I didn’t have them for any of my lessons. I waited until I heard a chair scrape on the floor as someone stands up, I look around and saw one figure standing up, I recognised the light blonde hair instantly.

“I think you want to be looking over there” Beatrice says, diverting the teacher’s attention over to Evie, who was walking back to her seat “Because Evie was the one to dump the bowl of porridge all over Iris”

I see the teacher look over to where the red – haired bully is standing and all she asks is “Is this true?”

I look over to Evie, who seemingly refuses to look at me, and starts to say “Of course, I didn’t do that, that student, whatever her name is, is lying…” She points over to Beatrice for added effect, which doesn’t seem to work.

I then decide that I have to try and get the bullies in trouble for once. So I add “Obviously it was Evie, because shouldn’t she have been sat down? And not chasing me around with my bowl of porridge”

I look over in Evie’s direction, hoping she would just give up, but she kept trying to defend herself “That freak is lying, you could ask my friends, they all saw what happened” When she had said this, the table where both Phoebe and Maisie were sat, they nodded their heads almost instantly after she had said this.

I decide to stop arguing and speak “There are countless witnesses in this room, and your friends just so happen to be the only ones who would lie” I say to Evie, who looks taken aback (again, probably for some sort of dramatic effect, which doesn’t work)

“No… No… No… They wouldn’t lie, they would obviously tell the truth” I knew once Evie had said ‘obviously’ that she was lying.

The teacher looked around and said “I don’t have time for this, assembly is in five minutes, so first, you” She says as she points at me “should get the porridge off of your school uniform and your hair” Then she turns to Evie and says “And you need to sit down and stop messing around” I could see that Evie was about to disagree, but then she thought against it as she went to sit down at the table where her two friends were.

Meanwhile, I headed off in the direction of the lavatories to remove the spilt porridge from my pale orange blazer and my hair.

=====

It took a good few minutes before I checked myself in the mirror and saw no trace of porridge.

So, I walked out of the lavatories and saw that everyone was silent, except for deep voice, which could be heard echoing across the hall, (in fact, even if you were in the library, you would probably still be able to hear it). The deep voice belonged to the head master, presumably doing the assembly.

As I walked over to the table I had been sat at before I had had porridge thrown down my clothes, I could hear the head master talk about bullying and how it will be punished if you do it, even if it was a bit late to say that now. I knew I had been punished, although I had no idea how, and somehow the school bullies hadn’t. Even now, I don’t think Evie had been punished for throwing the porridge all down my hair and school uniform.

As I sat down at the table, the had master had moved onto talking about how we shouldn’t litter in an attempt to keep the ‘school environment’ tidy. I wasn’t really listening, truth be told, instead, I was thinking how annoying it was that Evie and her two friends (more like fiends) seemed to always get away wit hall the trouble they cause. For instance, take what happened a few minutes ago, surely she should have been punished.

Beatrice must have seen me thinking, because she quietly says “Don’t start to think anymore of it, we’ll find a way to get her punished”

“Yeah… Good” I snap in response “She always manages to get away with the bullying every single time. It’s ridiculous”

“I know, I know…” Beatrice says “We’ll find a way to make sure she is punished.”

=====

The annoying thing about this morning was that it had made me forget about the original plan of heading to the library.

After a few lessons, much to my relief, the memory of the incident that had happened earlier had completely disappeared. Almost like it had just been removed from my brain completely. It’s quite incredible what two hours of lessons can either teach you, or make you forget. Although, I have a feeling it was trying to wrap my head around one of the complicated math problems we had to do which made me forget about it.

Right now, it was nearing the end of break time, about five minutes left before we had to head to lessons and continue the learning. I checked my timetable and realised that I had science next and it made me remember that I was supposed to be in the library, checking to see if I could find anything. In the end, I decide to do this after science, as it is lunch time, which means I’ll have enough time to at least check some of the bookcases, because not much can be achieved in two minutes.

All I could do was await the end of the science lesson and wait until lunch time to be able to put my plan into action.
