One Eye, One Girl

Currently reading: Chapter 15

Previous Happenings:

It's finally time for the beginning of the (kinda not so heavily investigated) adventure into the night begin...

I cautiously pulled the door opened and scanned the surroundings, as best I could in the dark. Once I had made sure that no teachers were roaming the corridors, I and my two friends paced down the corridor as quickly as possible.

I couldn’t believe we were actually going to try and escape school. That’s if we could execute the plan perfectly.

Down the stairs and then continuing along the corridor, Beatrice eventually whispers “Here it is.”

When I saw Beatrice’s shadow walk over to the door, it was kind of inevitable that, when she opened the door, it didn’t open. At that moment, a sinking feeling hit me. If the cleaning cupboard was locked, then we wouldn’t be able to leave the school to continue the plan.

Willow then whispered “Let me try…” Beatrice moved out of the way and Willow attempted to open the door, but it still wasn’t opening.

I then decided that I should give it a go. So when Willow moves away from the door, I give it a go. So I pull and push on the door, but it doesn’t budge. I wonder whether the sound of the door hitting the door frame would have attracted the attention of the any teachers, our greatest enemies at the moment.

I dismissed this thought as I tried one more time; it still didn’t move.

Beatrice then turns her torch on and this reveals a padlock on the lock underneath the door handle. Which would explain why it wasn’t opening. Then Beatrice pulls a hairclip out of her hair and asks “does anyone know how to pick locks with one of these? Because I don’t think I do” Me and Willow both shook our heads, probably not the answer she was looking for. Beatrice asks Willow to hold the torch as she attempts to slot the hair clip into the key hole and says “Ok, I’ll see if I can do it.”

I was beginning to lose hope now. If Beatrice couldn’t pick this lock, which by the looks of it, didn’t look too promising, then we would be out of options.

However, as I was about to voice my thoughts and tell my friends that we might have to give up on this, Beatrice manages to successfully pick the lock. The lock clatters on the floor, not too loudly which was a bonus.

Beatrice then tries to open the door, however it doesn’t open. Again.

Whether there was another padlock or not wasn’t clear, but one thing which became clear was the fact that this door wasn’t moving, even after we had unlocked it. Willow and Beatrice had both tried again to see if they could open it, but I hadn’t. I didn’t see the point in trying to open the door after they had both tried and failed.

Since we were out of options, I was about to tell both my friends that we should head back to our dormitories, but then Beatrice says “I don’t know what we’re supposed to do now” Exactly what I was thinking “Why don’t you give it a go, Iris?” Despite not seeing the point, I decide to give a it a go anyway.

I pulled on the door as hard as I could and just when I was about to give up, it opened quite suddenly and as it opened, I almost fell to the floor, but managed to regain my footing before doing so.

Beatrice, in slight disbelief, whispered “How did you manage to open it and we couldn’t?” It made me think because I was quite confused because firstly, the door didn’t open for either of my two friends and I especially wasn’t expecting it to open when I tried it. I began to think that it might have been something to do with the strength of being bullied all these years for only having one eye. Looking different from everyone else. Maybe the strength came from all those thoughts. After all, I was getting fairly tired of all the bullying.

Willow then pointed the torch into the small room, so the contents of the cupboard was actually visible. It was quite a small space, there were shelves which lined the walls with different cleaning supplies and gardening tools. Anyway, we were here for one thing: the keys, all we had to do was try and find them.

Looking on the different shelves didn’t seem to reveal much, but then the torch lights begins to move as Willow takes something off of a hook on the door and whispers “I’ve found the keys,” she was holding a ring of keys, there was at least 10 keys.

Willow keeps the torch on, so we could see where we were going. We left the cleaning cupboard, I shut the door of the cupboard seeing as I was the only one who had managed to open it, I did try to lock it again, but it didn’t seem to work, so I just left it.

We then carried on down the corridor on our midnight adventure, even if it wasn’t really midnight, it just felt like it.

When we were in the library, we had planned on heading to the school gardens. Beatrice supposedly knew how to get there, she was leading the way after all. Me and Willow followed Beatrice until we eventually arrived at a door, which I didn’t even know existed, and Beatrice says “I believe this is the door we are looking for.”

“I suppose there was one way to find out…” I whisper back in response, Willow shines the bright light of the torch onto the lock of the door. Beatrice quickly finds the key on the key ring and puts the key into the lock, and when the key is turned, the door just opens, like someone had just pushed it open.

Willow turns the torch to check the corridors, however, from what is lit up, we can’t see anyone. Beatrice then takes the torch from Willow’s hand and is the first one to venture out into the dark outdoors.

Me and Willow followed her and I looked up at the sky. It was dark. The darkness kind of reminded me of all of the things that had gone wrong in school. All the times this week, that time I had been knocked out by playing football, and all of the fights I had been a part of over all this time. Hopefully the bullying would all be over soon.

Hopefully.

The reason we were in the school gardens at all was to face the final obstacle: the gate out of the school. This however, was no problem for us because we had the key to unlock it. We were walking along the path, so hopefully that would lead to the gate. Looking at what was in view. It seemed the garden was kept in pristine condition, it must have been the gardeners pride and joy. I was sure that the gardener would have focussed on other parts of the school, but this was probably where they spent most of their time. There was a large variety of flowers here, it was quite mesmerising to watch them blow in the midnight breeze, but there was more important things to do than to stare at the flowers.

We followed the path until we reached the end, at which we found was the gate we were looking for. This was no problem for us as we had the key to unlock it, so after we shined the torch’s light onto the gate, we found a padlock securing the two gates together. Beatrice then takes the ring of keys out of her pocket and eventually finds the correct key to place in the lock, it turned and the padlock was released. Beatrice took it and placed it on the grass, and so the journey continued along the same path, which Beatrice was sure would take us to the front of the school.

When we arrive at the entrance to Hazelwood, we all look at each other, “Looks like this is it, are we ready to escape school for a bit?” Beatrice asks.

“Yes, we probably should, before we get caught.” Willow replies, slightly louder than her usual whisper.

And so, this was where the adventure would actually begin, hopefully it wouldn’t be too long before we returned to Hazelwood.

In all honesty, now that the school was behind us, it didn’t feel right to be leaving school; we would have the occasional holiday, but other than that we were stuck at school. It made me realise how trapped it all felt. Three months of being stuck in school.

The first big decision in our adventure didn’t take too long to encounter, being just outside the school. Beatrice was the one to bring it up when we reached the path alongside the road “Which way do we go now?” Willow said that we should go right, whereas I, remembering the map we had looked at, said we should go left. On the basis that I had done more research, Beatrice decided that we should go with what I said. So, left we went.

I could tell it was the right option because it wasn’t long before we were walking alongside a giant group of trees. Looking back a few times, I saw Hazelwood School slowly disappearing into the horizon, we may have finally escaped school, but we would be returning soon, hopefully.

Thankfully, the path wasn’t completely dark as it was lit up with the faint orange glow of the street lamp, and the occasional headlights of any car which passed infrequently. Although, other than the few cards which passed us, it was eerily quiet. Just the three of us walking along the pavement, whilst the light stars in the dark sky analysed our every move.

Ultimately, it didn’t take long before one of us broke the silence.

“How long do you think it will be before we get to wherever we need to be?” Beatrice asked, I looked over at the trees and then looked back to her, I could tell she was growing more and more impatient with every step she took.

“I don’t know… But remember the map? It didn’t have any scale on it, so we could be walking for quite some time, or barely any” I say. It was quite annoying that the map we were basing all of this on didn’t have a scale, it would have been nice to have a rough estimate of how long this would take., although we had been walking for about 5 minutes so far.

So, we could be walking for miles and not reach the village before daylight rises. We didn’t (and couldn’t really) stop walking though. We had a quest, and we were going to complete it, no matter how long it took.

=====

We had been walking for what felt like forever, even if it has probably been close to 15 minutes, I was starting to lose hope. That was until I saw a junction in the road we were alongside, looking down it revealed a cul – de – sac, where there were a few buildings which looked to be houses.

“Look over there!” I almost shouted with excitement, “That must be it!” I pointed at the lights which seemed to be slightly fading with every second that passed.

“Finally, this path has better lead us to the village we’re looking for” Beatrice says and I agreed with her. I quickened my pace towards a sign which said: ‘Welcome to Hazelford’. We had made it! Part of our quest was complete; this was the village I assumed we needed to see.

Now, all we had to hope was that there would be a path into this neighbouring forest.

The houses we were met with were relatively small, although they stood tall with two floors. The only way I was able to tell they were made of wood was the faint light emitted from the street lamps. It also highlighted the flowers around the sides and the ivy climbing up the walls of the houses.

“So, where to now?” Beatrice asks.

“That’s a good question…” I reply, not quite knowing myself, I think back to the plan I had attempted to form in my head, the original plan was to try and find the ancient stone building, where the sorcerers and sorceresses could have potentially written on a piece of paper, then enchanted and then stored it. I was hoping that the stone building we were looking for would hold all the answers we needed.

“So… somewhere in the forest I’m guessing” Beatrice says, I must have been mumbling my plan to myself “because I’m not seeing any abandoned buildings here.” She had a point and there was a rectangle in the middle of nowhere (which I guess meant hidden within the forest) if I remember the layout of the map correctly.

We had to see if we could find a path into the forest, because from what we could currently see, there was no way in. Which meant that we were stuck, slightly confused and potentially lost. If this was all we could discover, and the path ended here. This meant we were stuck with two options, either we would have to head back with either the quest unsuccessful or search the area to find a way into the forest.

Since none of us wanted the quest to end. Especially, after all the trouble we had been through to actually get to this point, we decided to search around the cul – de – sac. So, that’s what we spent the next few minutes doing.

It felt like quite a while before Willow pointed over to a gap between tow houses and said “I think I might have found it.”

“Well, thank goodness for that!” I say, “the stone building must be through here,” I say as I begin to head into the forest. The level of darkness because of the trees made me wonder whether or not embarking on this quest was a good idea.

=====

It eventually got to the point where it was so dark that Beatrice said “Wait a minute,” and got the torch out of her bag, when she turned it on, the surroundings were illuminated. We had somehow managed to stray away from the path only slightly. Much to my relief, it was still in sight, so we headed back on the path and continued onwards.

The path we were taking was completely surrounded by trees and there were loose branches which lay unforgiving on the floor. We were probably deep in the forest now. So far, none of the branches had tripped me up, but I didn’t know how long that would last. I decided to hold back and let Beatrice lead the way. Beatrice seemed to be quite confident, however Willow seemed to be taking the walk slowly and steadily, I decided to take the same approach as Willow; I didn’t really want to trip over any of the branches.

I looked down and part of the light from Beatrice’s torch lit up the various greenery scattered across the floor. I was surprised that none of us had tripped over any loose greenery yet. I kept my head down to avoid tripping over, we walked on for quite some time until I could see a folded piece of paper, unlike the rest of the scenery, this wasn’t covered in greenery, which it probably would have if it had been there for quite a while. I decided to pick it up and once I unfolded it, I saw that this piece of paper was quite small, in fact, from what I could see, it was quite obvious that it was hastily cut by someone. The piece of paper had a few handwritten words written on it, once I had read them, I was quite shocked.

Turn Back Now Beatrice.
