One Eye, One Girl

Currently reading: Chapter 24

Previous Happenings:

Currently our protagonists are looking for a way to cure Evie's illness, whatever it may be, especially as Iris assumes that the three of them are to blame for it.

I had formed a plan last night before I went to bed. And I wanted to carry it out as soon as possible.

So, as soon as the school bell rang, I began by getting out of bed and changing into my school uniform. Once all of that was done, I immediately headed to the library and tried to open the doors. They were locked…

That was annoying.

So, in a change of plan, rather annoyingly, I headed back to the main hall to get some breakfast, not really what I wanted to do, but I was quite hungry.

=====

Arriving at the main hall, it was quiet, almost too quiet, I walk over to the canteen and get some plain old porridge. I walk around the empty hall for a minute before taking a sit at one of the many empty table.

I begin mindlessly eating my porridge, wondering what to do for the remainder of the time until the library opens. I think about what day it is... Friday, so the library should be open.

So after I finish my porridge, I head to the library once more to see if the library is open again.

I try to open the doors again and thankfully they open this time! It would have been annoying to find that they were closed again. I walk inside and am met with the familiar sight of hundreds of books laid out on the different bookcases. Where to begin? I decide to start on the mysteries of history section, because if that’s where we found the book which told us all of that, then surely it would be in the bookcase, … Looking at the bookcase made me remember the cover of the book, I wondered whether it would be… Wait… That book, I didn’t return it to the library! Oh well… I’ll have to worry about that later… That would maybe make things a little bit tougher, I was hoping that I might be able to find it near the book, especially since I knew what the cover looked like now. At least I now knew that this bookcase was barely organised, I think the books were just placed on the bookshelf where ever they fit.

I stared mindlessly at the books on the shelves for quite a while before actually beginning to search along the bookcase’s shelves.

Was I expecting to find anything? No… But I was still hopeful, I guess some of Willow’s hope had rubbed off on me.

=====

Final shelf! What was there to find? So far, there had been all sorts of historical books, some on the things we had learned in various history lessons, and others, were just there for further learning, I suppose.

Looking at the few final books now…

Is there something?

No… Nothing. No books there… Maybe there would be one on another bookshelf but I didn’t really want to check right now.

I walk over to the door, but Willow enters “What are you doing here?” I ask, quite surprised.

“Well… You weren’t in the dormitory, so I thought I would check here…”

“Surely this wasn’t the first place you would check?” I ask, wondering how on earth she found me in here without looking anywhere else.

“Well… I looked through the window and saw you in here” fair enough then…

I still wanted to leave the library, so I told Willow about how I had searched the mysteries of history shelf but hadn’t found anything, I then inform her that I was about to leave, and she says, “Alright, let’s go get some breakfast” So, we head out of the library and begin walking to where the stairs are, leading down to the main hall.

As we left the library, she reminded me that I still had to return my library book, so I turned around and headed back to my dormitory, although, maybe if they put it back on the shelf, they may put it back where it was before, it may lead me to somewhere I hadn’t looked before… That’s what I had to hope!

=====

Thankfully, returning the library book was a fairly simple process, now all I had to do was hope that the librarian put the book back in it’s original position. Although, when I arrived at the main hall, I noticed that Willow was sat at a table with Beatrice and Amber. I begin to walk over to where they are sat, but then I hear a voice behind me calling my name, “Iris… Iris!” I look behind me and find Phoebe running after me, normally this would be a bad sign, but I wasn’t too bothered by it this time.

“What is it?” I ask

“I’ve been reading a book and I think I may have found something out!”

“Really? Can I see it?”

“Yeah… Come up to my dormitory and I’ll show you” Now breakfast would have to wait, because hopefully this was something worth seeing.

When Phoebe opened the door to her and Evie’s dormitory, I saw that Evie was still asleep, even though the school bell had rang! But I also saw that there was a book placed on a square wooden table by the window (quite like our dormitory), surely that wasn’t the book I had returned to the library… Surely not… Phoebe walked over to the table and picked up the boo, it had a different title to the one I had just returned, so it must have been different, but the cover did look quite familiar.

“I found this in the library yesterday” (TO DO: CHECK TO SEE IF RIGHT DAY) Phoebe said “and I realised that it might help in fixing whatever’s wrong with Evie”

That’s probably why I couldn’t find anything in the library, because Phoebe had already found it! I was quite glad she found it because if she hadn’t then we would have been in a rather tough spot.

She then turns to a page marked by a bookmark and says “I found this, read it and see what you think”

After reading a few lines, I realise that this is some sort of retelling of the event that took place all those years ago, it must have been written by someone who did it all, around 70 years ago. And funnily enough, when I checked the name of the author of the book: Alicia Leywood, it seemed familiar, because I had seen the name in the other book I had read.  So, all in all, maybe it was different to the one I had read before.

After realising this, I continued to read it and found something rather interesting.

Here’s an excerpt of what I read:

So, after we had all joined together, we had to figure out how to come together in an attempt to change everyone’s personality traits, the reason why I shall not write.

We somehow had to decide on how we were going to actually enact this spell, I had thought of the idea of defining personalities based on a characteristic. After communicating this idea with the rest of the group, when eventually Zachary eventually decided, for he was the one who formed the group, that we should attempt to somehow link it to the people’s eye colours.

That was when we realised that we could assign different personalities to the different eye colours. The initial idea (thought up by Enoch) was to try basic imagery, blue for sadness and green for jealousy just to name a few.

So we were right about that part! At least we had got something right, even if we did get most things wrong. I continued reading regardless.

A few more lines, I read:

But, how were we going to do it? That was the question we had. I have a feeling Zachary had read quite a few fantasy takes because he suggested we do some kind of ritual.

A ritual? was that what we had to do to remove the curse initially.

I was beginning to wonder what this actually had to do with what we were trying to achieve, which was bringing the soul of Hazelwood back to… Well… Hazelwood. Until I read this line:

So, to give this book a purpose and to those who have a distaste for change, I shall inform you of what we did to change everyone's personality traits.

This was exactly what I was looking for, I said to Phoebe that we may be able to recreate this, because looking at the instructions, it didn't look too complicated. Although, I was sure putting it into practise would be much harder than it initially seemed.

“Yeah, I think this is what we’ve been looking for” I say to Phoebe, who looks up from another books she’s reading and says

“Glad to hear it!” Even if she did say it fairly loud, Evie didn’t wake up… I wonder whether she was getting worse, I hoped not.

Some of the things we needed on the list I was quite sure we may not be able to find in the school, but there might be alternatives. One of the things we needed were 5 candles, but I was sure we weren’t going to find 5 candles, but Bunsen burners might work… So, all we had to do was hope that it would still work anyway.

“But one quick thing…” Phoebe suddenly says

“Yes?” I ask

“Well… I read a bit of the book, there were 5 people in the group, that’s why they use 5 candles in a circle formation,” she points to the instruction where it says ‘we then set up 5 candles in a circular formation’

Where would we find 5 people?

“So, it looks like we need 5 people” Phoebe continues.

“I figured… But who?” I ask

“Well… I’d be willing to help out” So that’s at least two people. Although we now needed three more people…

Thankfully I knew exactly where those three people where.

=====

“What are you talking about?” Beatrice asks, clearly confused

“No… It’s quite straight forward” I say, I had headed back to the table where I knew Amber, Beatrice and Willow were sat, Amber must have left at some point because only Beatrice and willow remained “We need three people to help with this ritual… Which should hopefully help with curing Evie of whatever she is ill of at the minute”

“Ok… But how are we supposed to help?” Willow begins to ask

“So, you’re going to help?” I ask, rather eagerly

“I didn’t say that”

“Fine…” I reluctantly say, as I place the book on the table and point to an instruction which says ‘The 5 of us in the group then chanted a spell (see next chapter for details)’ “See here, this is why we need five people, I was wondering whether you two would be willing to help” I hint and then In a quieter voice I add “Especially after what you told me, surely you must feel some of the guilt”

“Ok… I guess we’ll help” Beatrice eventually says “But, how are we going to get two more people?”

“Phoebe has already said that she would help, and I was kind of hoping Amber was still here, so I could have told her as well”

“I suppose I could ask her. After all, I do share a dormitory with her” So we may be forming a plan. A plan to hopefully save the soul of Hazelwood school!

=====

Before going to bed, I decide to read some more of the book, Phoebe had let me keep it after all, so I thought I may as well. Despite there being a wealth of information in this book, I decide to read over the instructions one more time, just in case I missed anything.

=====

First, a list of what we needed:

  • An enclosed, dark space.
  • Chalk (or something else to draw with).
  • Five candles.

Secondly, a list of what we did in order to complete the ritual:

  • First we had to find a dark enclosed space, which was fairly easy, we chose a small abandoned building in a local village.
  • Once the dark space had been found, Zachary was the one to draw a rough sketch of a circle, using some chalk.
  • We then set up 5 candles in a circular formation (this was done by placing each candle at different points on the circle Zachary had drawn).
  • The group then all had to sit down behind the five candles we had placed in the previous step.
  • The 5 of us in the group then chanted a spell (see next chapter for details).

And that’s it! That was all we had to do, although the origin of the spell can get a bit complicated… But other than that, it was fairly straight forward to change everyone’s personality traits, of course, the effect didn’t take place immediately, instead it took until the next day and that was when we all noticed a change.
