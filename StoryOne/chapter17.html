One Eye, One Girl

It's time to change everything... or at least... just the personality traits if they've got it right! which hopefully they do... but time will tell.

As soon as we had all agreed to reverse the spell, Beatrice checked the time, five seconds remaining. In those five seconds, we formed a circle and joined our hands together and we as soon as we heard the bell (we waited until it rang three times) and then we were about to start chanting the words we had found on the wall.

However, just as we were about to start to chant the words, the words will would potentially change the world. We were stopped. Stopped by the sound of footsteps entering the room and the sound of a voice, a voice which I didn’t recognise.

“What on earth… What is going on here?”

We all turned at once and found a shadowed figure stood in the doorway, Beatrice picked up the torch and pointed it towards the person (not in their eyes, thankfully).

We stared at the person, until they eventually spoke, they only said two words

“Hello Beatrice…”

“A… Archie?” Beatrice says, a stutter in her voice, she was clearly quite shocked “What on earth are you doing here?”

“I mean… I could be asking you the exact same thing… What are you doing in this stone building at the middle of the night?” Archie asks her.

All four of us stood in silence, I was just quite surprised to find that someone had actually found us, I had no idea who they were, who Archie even was. Beatrice seemed to know who he was. The plan was certainly not to be found. However somehow, someone had managed to find us. I have no idea how. I felt my heart beat quicken slightly. The plan wasn’t supposed to go like this.

After a few moments of standing still and in silence, I eventually decided to break the silence by asking one question. “Who is this?” Beatrice looks over to me and tells me and Willow

“That’s my older brother, Archie…” After she had said that, I noticed that I could kind of see the resemblance, all I could see though was that they both seemed to have light coloured hair. In a quieter voice Beatrice told us “I have no idea what on earth he is doing here though…” I had known Beatrice (pretty much) since I had joined Hazelwood, and I had never known, because she had never told us that she had an older brother.

“What’s even going on…” Willow says

“I have no idea…” I reply, the focus turns back to Beatrice’s brother, who told us

“I also had no idea what was going on, until I saw three people wandering through the forest, I thought to myself, there is only really one place in the forest where anyone could be heading, and I guessed it would be the stone building, and I was correct”

“I don’t really think anyone cares about why you were probably following us through the forest…” Beatrice tells her brother “but what I want to know is, why are you even here in the first place?”

“You did already ask that” Archie tells her

“Yes… But I didn’t get an answer the first time”

“Ok… Well, if you must know…” Archie starts off by saying

“Yes… We do want to know…” Beatrice interrupts

“I had planned that tonight would be the night that I would put my research to the test, I would venture out to the forest, where this very building is located, and try to put an end to this mystery of history” After he had said this, it didn’t take me long to realise that we were all here because we all wanted to try and fix everyone’s personality traits. “I have no idea why you three are here though… Like why did my little sister have to be here, of all the people...”

“I’m not that little…” Beatrice says “I’m only 2 years younger than you are”

“Can you two… perhaps stop arguing…” I say “To answer your question” I say whilst looking over at Beatrice’s older brother “We’re also here to try to put an end to the mystery of history”

“Well… if you were going to put a stop to it, then you’re doing it all wrong…” Archie tells us all “If you had actually tried to reverse it then…”

“Before you tried to stop us” Beatrice interrupts

“Let me talk!” Archie almost yells, then he returns to his normal voice as he continues to say “You could have completely ruined it all.”

“I’m sorry” I interrupt “But how could we have almost ruined it, what could we have possibly ruined?”

“Well… It’s quite simple really”

“Yeah… Because you have been researching it for a while” Beatrice interrupts Archie again.

“Stop arguing…” Willow mutters underneath her breath, although I don’t think Archie or Beatrice heard her. However, Archie seems to ignore Beatrice and continues to answer my question

“That spell, the one which has changed everyone’s personality traits, has been held for around 70 years now, on the piece of paper, it tells you that it was originally made to stop all of the chaos that was happening in the village. The trouble continued to spread, and that was when the group of sorcerers and sorceresses was formed and they made this spell to try and put an end to the trouble by giving each eye colour different personality traits, it seemed to work as the trouble slowly came to an end” Archie explains. Of course, something would have stood in our way of completing this mission, in this case, it seemed as though trouble would continue, whatever trouble seemed to be happening at the time, it must have been terrible. It seemed that the bad luck just seemed to follow me around, maybe what the teacher said was true.

“Well, what are we supposed to do then? I assume you are also here because you wanted to try and change everyone’s personality traits overnight, how were you going to do it?” Beatrice asks

“Well… Unlike you lot, who probably did all your research overnight” Archie starts to say

“No, we didn’t” Beatrice interrupts “We have been researching this for about 2 weeks now”

“Well…” Archie continues “I have been researching this for around 3 months, and I have a slight feeling that there is a way to try and change everyone’s personality traits without allowing the trouble to continue, however, you have actually figured out the final part of it, all we now have to do is try to complete the rest of the plan”

“Well… then aren’t you going to tell us how we are supposed to do it?” Beatrice rudely asks

“If you carry on talking like that, I may as well not show you”

“Alright then…” Beatrice says “Could you show us how we are supposed to change everyone’s personality traits?”

“I guess I will…” Archie says
