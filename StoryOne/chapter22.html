One Eye, One Girl

Currently reading: Chapter 22

Previous Happenings:

After hoping what they had done had been successful... it turned out that might not be the case, especially as one of the students at Hazelwood wasn't very well at the moment... but who it was, was a mystery, a mystery which Iris wants to solve.

It was now nearing the end of the day (the day after the emergency assembly we had) and me, Beatrice and Willow had decided to meet up at the main hall, I had decided that I would tell both of my friends about how I thought Evie was dead. Once again, the school hadn’t told us who had died. It could be someone else, but I was quite certain that she was the one who was dead. I wasn’t sure what they would think of it though, would they be happy or miserable? I suppose there was one way to find out, and it would happen after this boring maths lesson. Hearing the teacher saying math equations much quicker than he probably should be, because this teaching method doesn’t seem to work. Not on me at least.

Normally before this lesson, everyone would seem to have small conversations before the teacher arrived, however this time, when I arrived, everyone seemed to be sat in their seats, silently awaiting the arrival of the teacher, because he tended to arrive a few minutes later than he was supposed to. I look around for an empty seat and find one towards the back to the classroom next to Willow. She doesn’t say anything, which isn’t very surprising, although after the adventure we had been on, I noticed that she was beginning to speak a little more than before.

However, there was silence in the classroom.

And the silence continued for the entire lesson.

=====

The silence came to an end when the bell rang signalling the end of the day. Most people began their conversations as we left the classroom. I wasn’t really too sure how to feel about what was going on at the minute, thinking about it, I think I had gone slightly quieter than usual, and I was sure Beatrice had as well. I guess I was just shocked by it, but in a way, I was glad the bullying had come to an end, but I wasn’t expecting it to be because someone had died.

After I had left the classroom, I headed to the main hall and hoped my friends would follow me (if they remembered). It was strange because usually when I would walk down these corridors, I would be worried that I would bump into Evie or her friends, and whilst there would still be the risk of running into Maisie or Phoebe. Although that didn’t seem to bother me anymore, in fact, they had gone their separate ways, and it didn’t really feel right, they had turned silent. Evie must have been quite a big influence on their behaviour.

There weren’t many people in the main hall when I arrived. It was much quieter than it usually was. Whether that was because it was the end of the day and some people may have gone back to their dormitories. I took a seat at one of the many empty tables and looked through my blazer pockets, I took out a few pieces of paper, one of the pieces of paper was a piece of English homework I had got in the lesson we had earlier today, the other two pieces of paper which I had found were the notes I had made relating to the small adventure we had gone on a few days prior.

I looked at the English homework and it didn’t seem too bad, just writing about some quotations from Alice’s Adventures in Wonderland, that being the text we were studying (I had always thought that it was quite an intriguing piece of literature). I read over the quotations, all of which we had encountered in the text and heard many times, so, I knew them fairly well now. It didn’t take me long to read over the quotations and when I did, I put the sheet of paper back in my blazer pocket, I would have to remember to complete that assignment later. Because, right now, there were more important things to think about, for example, someone had died and school were still giving out homework (some things just don’t change). Another thing which I was thinking about was the fact that my friends hadn’t come to the main hall yet, even though I had told them. At least, I thought I had, but maybe I had told them in a dream… I hoped that I had actually told them and that it wasn’t a dream… I suppose time would tell.

=====

A short while later, I saw Willow arrive at the entrance to the main hall. She walked over to the table, looking down at the floor as she did so. When she arrived at the table, she took a seat and moved her hair out of her eyes (before we had been on our adventure her hair didn’t really cover her eyes, but was very close to. And now, it was almost covering her eyes).

She asked me “W… What’s going on?” I was wondering whether I should wait for Beatrice or not, but I had no idea where she was. Maybe she had forgotten or I potentially hadn’t told her? I wasn’t too sure… I had waited around 5 minutes just for Willow to arrive, so it could very well be another 5 before Beatrice arrived. In the end, I just decide to tell my quiet friend what’s going on in my mind.

“So, I was thinking about how someone had died. Firstly, I think it might be Evie, and secondly… I think we were the ones who caused it…”

“How can you say that!” Willow exclaims, and then returns to a quieter voice as she asks “What do you mean?”

I suppose it’s also worth mentioning that during the evening of the day we had had the assembly, just before we were supposed to return to our dormitories, we had all agreed that something didn’t seem right, I wasn’t too sure whether both my friends shared my guilt or not… I was guessing not, but still wondered nonetheless. However, I now somehow had to attempt to explain my thinking.

“Well… You know how we managed to sort out the whole situation relating to the eye colours and all of that” I suppose, every time I would look in the mirror, I would notice that I had two eyes, which was something I wasn’t used to seeing, especially only having one eye for my entire life. “I think something happened to Evie after we managed to remove the curse or spell” (I’m still unsure what to call it) “and rather unfortunately, it resulted in her death” Willow pondered upon this for a moment before saying

“I have a feeling you’re right, because the whole point of that was to stop the bullying which always seemed to happen, and it worked, but it looks to have completely removed the main bully… And I’m guessing that wasn’t the intention, was it?”

“No… Not really….” I admit, sure, the whole plan was to try to stop the bullying from happen, but, as Willow just explained, we had managed to do that, but by somehow giving me a second eye and killing Evie, something wasn’t really right about that, but I’m still trying to make sense of it all.

I think we were both trying to somehow piece together how we could’ve potentially caused Evie’s death. When Beatrice eventually arrived and told us that she was sorry she was late and that she completely forgot about it, I tell her not to worry. As she takes a seat, she asks “So… What did you want to talk about?” I look to Willow, who shrugs, before essentially telling her the exact same thing I had told Willow earlier.

After I had told her about it, she says “You think we did it?”

To which I respond with “Yes… I think we did…”

“How, though… How could we have done it?” Again, I told her about how it could be related to the adventure we had went on the day prior to the assembly, and how I thought it was Evie who had died. After she had listened, her response wasn’t much different to Willow’s

“I suppose you’ve got a point… Maybe it was something to do with the spell reversal we did, or something along those lines… and even, if it isn’t related to the adventure we went on, it could be something else like a murder… Can you imagine that, at boring old Hazelwood School, a murder mystery, waiting to be solved, I read those kinds of books all the time, it would be cool if there was actually a mystery like that to solve”

“I doubt it’s anything to do with a murder, because if it was, then wouldn’t school be more cautious about what was going on, so far, we’ve just been going on with our lives normally, wouldn’t there be something that has changed?”

“Maybe… Or maybe it’s so they don’t want us to think it’s a murder, but secretly they’re trying to solve it themselves”

“It could be, but let’s try not to think of it as a murder mystery, because I am quite sure that somehow the adventure we went on, on the day prior to the assembly, somehow had something to do with the death of Evie”

Looking at the bigger picture, I doubted there was anyway she could have been murdered, considering the school was practically locked up during the night, (or at least, I think it was). Still, I think it was ‘right’ that the teachers hadn’t told us that Evie may have been murdered because that would just make us all worried (although it was quite concerning, just how little school was actually telling us). Maybe it was a murder… But I was kind of wishing it wasn’t. As much as I wanted Evie gone, I certainly didn’t want her dead.

=====

The day was soon over and as we were getting ready for bed, me and Willow were talking about what would be happening tomorrow, the sort of lessons we had, and what sort of things we might try to accomplish tomorrow, so far, we had agreed on heading to the library to do some homework which we needed to complete for geography, I also remembered that I would have to complete the English quotation sheet I found in my blazer pocket (now that we had returned to school, it was just the sort of boring thing we had to get used to again). I suppose the good thing about doing our small adventure after lights out was the fact that we hadn’t had to catch up with any missing assignments for our schoolwork, because there were no lessons to worry about at that time (because we were all supposed to be asleep).

Time continued to pass and we were in our beds, I was about to turn the bedside lamp off when Willow asked “What are we going to do?”

“What do you mean?”

“Well… You said we were the ones who caused Evie’s death, right?”

“Yeah…” I say, unsure where this conversation is going “or at least I think I do…”

“I thought so, anyway, we can’t just leave it, can we? Surely we have to do something, right?”

“I’ve been thinking about it for almost the entire day, but I’m not actually too sure what we can do about it…” Somehow we had managed to change it so there wasn’t any bullying, which was quite a surprise, I thought it wouldn’t do anything. But now I was wondering whether we had made a mistake in doing that, in fact, we most certainly had, I was sure (and I was sure Willow was too, as for Beatrice, I wasn’t too sure) that we had caused someone death…

But what could we do? That was the question on my lip that night. We had to do something, because we were the ones who had caused it, so it was only right, right? Although, what was there to do?

I suppose there was every little chance we wouldn’t be able to bring back Evie, but there was also the chance that we could. The one thing which I could think of was that we would have to re – enact the removed spell, how could we do that though? It taken quite a long time to actually remove the spell, so I didn’t think I wanted to know how long it would take to bring back the spell, and besides, would there even be any information on how to bring it back?

I suppose wondering about it wouldn’t really get us anywhere. Although thinking would be the first step to formulating a plan to bring Evie back to life. If we can actually manage to do that…
