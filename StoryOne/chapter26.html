One Eye, One Girl

Currently reading: Chapter 26

Previous Happenings:

After realising that the cross country race was cancelled due to Evie's illness, Iris realises that she kinda has to complete this ritual thing tonight.

At dinner time, we were all sat at the table. Me, Amber, Beatrice, Phoebe and Willow. It felt quite strange to have this many people to be able to talk to without being bullied, it was weird, but great. I had told everyone in maths that we should meet up to try and complete the ritual tonight. But first, we had to discuss what we were actually doing.

“Well… This is it,” I tell everyone, “we’re going to try and perform the ritual tonight.”

“What do we need to do?” Beatrice asks “I’m sure we’re all listening.”

“I was think that… Firstly we should do it at night, because then people can’t tell that we’re doing the ritual. Then for the question of where, I was thinking that we should do it in one of the science classrooms…”

“What about when? When are we going to be doing it?”

“Well… At night, but I guess sometime around midnight would be good, so we would meet up in one of the science classrooms and get everything ready there and then.”

“It sounds good so far, but do we have everything we need?”

“Well… That’s partially why we are doing it in the science classroom, because there will be Bunsen burners and gas taps we can set up to act as candles, and as for the… The chalk, I was hoping there would be some by the chalkboard for the classroom anyway, so that shouldn’t be too hard to find”

“Wait a minute…” Amber suddenly brings my explanation to an end, as she says “Do you have the book with you, Phoebe told me that she found a book containing all of this information after I asked her how you know all of this”

“I don’t… But I could go and get it, if you want me to.” I don’t know why I ask that, because I decide to go and get it anyway “One second, I’ll be back”

=====

After I arrive at the dormitory, I pick up the book and realise that I hadn’t really researched this as thoroughly as I initially wanted to. I had only really read the instructions page and the introduction page and that was it.

I told myself I should’ve aimed to research it more, but I didn’t… Although, we didn’t really have the time to research it more, because I wanted to try and solve this problem, which we had caused, all by myself, and obviously with the help of a few friends, who, if we were going to be changing it back to how it was before, wouldn’t stay friends for very long… Which made me quite miserable thinking about it… I would have to return to a life of being bullied…

I stood in silence for a few minutes, contemplating how, even though it had all gone wrong, some parts of it all felt right… it felt good to finally be rid of all the bullying, the main cause of which was also gone now…. I wonder whether my second eye would disappear? Although, it was finally time to get things right. The world isn’t meant to be this way. There would be some injustice within it, and that’s to be expected. Whilst we may want to fight against the injustice… We find that we can’t… And I’ve now come to realise that we aren’t supposed to. The bad things will continue to happen, and some good things will happen.

Life is a combination of these good and bad things, we just have to accept the bad things are going to happen and we appreciate it when the good things happen.

Anyway… where was I? Oh yes, bringing the book back to the table.

=====

“Here it is,” I say, as I place the book down on the table, and flick to the page with the bookmark in it.

“Where is it?” Amber mutters and then exclaims “Ah yes, this is what I was thinking about”

“W… What is it?” Willow asks

“Well… here it says that the candles need to be around 5 different points of the circle, which I’m guessing is what the chalk is for, and we can’t necessarily move the Bunsen burners very far, because they have to be connected to the gas taps.”

I hadn’t thought of that, it was quite an interesting point, the gas taps would be connected to something underneath the tables which can’t be moved anyway, unless there was someway to create a circular formation by lighting certain Bunsen burners, we were out of luck.

“I don’t know if we would have enough, but I have two torches we could use” Beatrice suggests, it was a good suggestion, but I wasn’t sure it would work.

Although, to my surprise, Phoebe says “I suppose it’s worth a try. I’m sure if we used the torches, I think I may have one in my dormitory we can use, and combined them with the Bunsen burners, we would be able to create the circular formation required to complete the ritual.”

It was a slightly better idea, because then it only relied on two Bunsen burners rather than the initial five. Which, I hoped, was going to make this easier to set up.

Although… Now it would be lights out. In a few hours, the ritual would be performed. “See you tonight,” I say as we leave the table and head to our dormitory

=====

After what Beatrice had said about the torches, I decided to use one to shine a dim light on the torch, she had given me one that was almost out of power, but it did the trick. It also didn’t seem to wake Willow, who had decided, unlike me, to go asleep until it was time to head out to the science classroom.

The clock then showed 11 o’clock so, since that was the time we had agreed on, I stood up and quietly managed to wake Willow up, I guessed she wasn’t actually asleep, or somehow the spell had changed that, because normally she most certainly wouldn’t have woken up.

“Is it time?” She asks.

“Yeah… Let’s go” I whisper back.

When I open the dormitory door, I see Amber and Beatrice walking past, Beatrice waves but doesn’t say anything. I wave back and also don’t say anything, because I knew if I did, I may wake someone up, and that was the last thing we wanted to happen.

Silently, we all headed for the science classroom we had all agreed on, the experiment room was what I called it, because that was where all the experiments we have to do happen, that’s probably the best part about science, being able to do the experiments, I suppose learning about the different personalities was also relatively interesting.

Since there were no dormitories in the science area, it meant that we were able to speak, but we only spoke quietly.

“Here we are.” Beatrice says as we arrive

“This is it.” I whisper in response.

When Beatrice opens the door, she gasps, but then whispers “Oh, it’s only you” I look through the door and find that somehow Phoebe was already there, which I was quite thankful for, at least it wasn’t anyone else.

We all walked into the room and I decide to turn the light on, making sure the light switch didn’t click too loudly.

“Do the lights really have to be on?” Amber asks, shielding her eyes, I guess we were all still used to the dark.

“I’ll admit it’s a bit bright, but give me a minute to draw the circle and light the Bunsen burners,” With that said, I take the chalk from the side of the chalkboard and after a bit of deliberating, and a little bit of help from Phoebe, we eventually had a fairly large circle drawn on the floor.

Admittedly, it wasn’t very visible because we were using white chalk to draw on light grey tiles, but it was all we had, so it would have to do. We had drawn the circle so that it went underneath two of the workbenches, but we had left room so we would be able to place the three torches around the remaining points of the circle.

Much to Amber’s relief, once we had placed all three torches upright at the remaining points of the circle, the lights were able to be turned off, and we were all back in darkness again. Except for the dancing orange flames of the Bunsen burners and the vibrant pale yellow glow of the torches. Although, it was now the time, to perform the ritual.

“Wait… Wait a minute!”

“What is it?”

“Well… What are we supposed to say?”

“Uh…” I didn’t have the book with me, so I wasn’t too sure what to say. And, if I didn’t then no one else would. “I’ll go and get the book, give me a minute”

And with that, I silently sprint down the isolated hallway to my dormitory.

=====

We now knew what we had to say, so now there was nothing standing in the way of us completing the ritual.

Then one side of the room became slightly darker, one of the torches had died…

“Quick! Light a Bunsen burner” Phoebe says, thankfully there was a workbench with a gas tap nearby, so Willow turned the light on as me and Beatrice sorted out the Bunsen burner.

Once it was lit, the light was turned off and now it would be time for the ritual to take place.

We all sat down behind the candles (and Bunsen burners) and held hands, It didn’t say in the instructions, but it felt right, then we all closed our eyes, because, again, it felt right to do so, and began to chant the spell.

I opened my eyes slightly once we had done so and saw a purple sphere forming in the centre of the circle. Much like it had before. I closed my eyes. Please don’t explode… please don’t explode…

Seconds pass and nothing has happened. Then the eruption occurs, I open my eyes as I hear it and then see the purple shockwave building up in size, then it erupted. Rapidly spreading around the room. We should’ve seen it coming, considering it happened last time. Although, I wasn’t expecting it to happen this time, considering we were enacting the spell again. It may have mentioned it in the book, but I probably didn’t read the part about that… I should’ve definitely done more research. Although, this time it was different. I was expecting it to send us flying to the walls of the classroom, but this time was different. Nothing like that happened… When I opened my eye, I was still in the same spot.

I could already tell something has happened, because the right side of my vision has disappeared, my second eye must have been taken away from me. Maybe that was how it was always supposed to be. I look around the circle, the Bunsen burners are still lit up, but the torches have lost their light.

“Open your eyes everyone” I say quietly, remembering that it was nearly midnight.

“What was that!” Phoebe asks, I had forgotten that neither Phoebe nor Amber had seen it happen before, it may take some explaining, but I still wasn’t sure why there was a huge shockwave, but explaining it would be hard work because I had no idea what was causing it either.

I look around the circle, Beatrice and Willow had now stood up, but Amber was still sat on the floor facing down to the floor with her eyes tight shut.

“Amber…” Beatrice whispers, “Amber, you can open your eyes now… It’s over now” Amber opens her eyes and slowly looks up.

“W… Wh… What just happened?” She stutters, she must have seen me looking at her because she says “Iris! Your eye, what happened?”

“We did it,” I quietly say in response, thinking about how I would now have to live life with one eye again, but I was used to that by now, “I think we managed to perform the ritual successfully and bring back the spell!”

“You really think so?”

“Yes… Hopefully”

“Right, should we sort all of this out and then go to sleep” Phoebe suggests.

“Yeah, we probably should” Me and Beatrice say, obviously we don’t say it at the same time, nor do we say the same thing, but we said similar things at similar times.

So we spend the next few minutes placing the Bunsen burners back in the cupboards and attempt to erase the circle, which is a little bit harder than I thought it would be, but we eventually got it to a point where it was barely noticeable.

As we were all heading out, I looked back into the room, who would’ve thought that this would be the place that we would have performed the ritual, let’s just hope that no one else needs to reverse the spell again, otherwise they may have to do it here.

I then turned off the light and quietly closed the door behind me and caught up with my friends, I hoped they were still my friends, after re-enacting the spell.

I was quite tired after all of that. I definitely needed to get some sleep. Hopefully everything would be resolved. I suppose we’ll find out in the morning.
