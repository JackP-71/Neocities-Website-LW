One Eye, One Girl

Currently reading: Chapter 27

Previous Happenings:

With the ritual complete, now our protagonists had to discover whether it had actually had the desired effect.

When the morning rolled around, I immediately began to wonder whether change had happened. I was also thinking about how we would be able to tell the difference, but then I remembered the main reason as to why we were doing this. Evie’s illness. Hopefully she had been cured now. Hopefully.

We had been woken up, once again by the school bell, Willow hadn’t thought, so I had to wake her up. Maybe that was the first sign of change. After I had changed into my school uniform, I was looking out of the window, when all of a sudden I heard a knock at the door. I walked over to the door and on my way asked Willow “Who do you think it could be?”

“I… I don’t know” She responds, which was the answer I, sort of, expected.

I open the door and find Phoebe standing there. Now, in my head, I knew this could go either one of two ways, either her personality hadn’t changed and we were still friends, or she was back to being one of the three bullies.

“Morning?” I say tentatively “What are you doing here?”

Please don’t be the second way… “Morning to you too” She says, thank goodness for that, “You know how we performed the ritual yesterday… or today, maybe…. Anyway, I believe it’s worked and done its job”

“What do you mean?” I ask, wondering how she could have noticed the change that had occurred, and as a direct result of the ritual.

“Well… I won’t tell you… I’ll show you” She moves away to reveal Evie standing behind her. I certainly wasn’t expecting that. She was dressed in her school uniform, which made me wonder how the teachers would react to seeing her back in lessons.

I was expecting her to say something nasty to me, but instead she quietly says “Hello” and waves nervously, what had happened?

I didn’t realise I had said that out loud until Phoebe says “Well… I don’t know either, but I’m guessing she’s cured of her illness and I have the feeling she has learnt from the error of her ways, but I actually have no idea what has happened.” Then she summed up what I was thinking by concluding her sentence with “It’s like she’s a completely different person” It didn’t appear that way because she looked identical, so she couldn’t be anyone else.

She seemed quieter than usual as well, I was sure she was going to say something else, but she just headed off down the corridor. It was almost as if she had completely forgotten about the fact that she was a bully. It was quite strange to see. Maybe all would be revealed later.

=====

It wasn’t until lunch time when I decided that I wanted to see if I could find out anything… Because surely, something was wrong.

In the main hall, I saw Evie sat with Phoebe, with Maisie being nowhere to be seen, I sit down, it seemed the tension between me and Phoebe had settled. Although, I was still unsure about my rivalry with Evie, and I guess Maisie as well. Did it still exist? I walked over to the table they were sat at and asked if I could sit down, Phoebe said that I could and so I did. For a few moments, we sat there in silence, until I eventually decided to speak up and ask “Evie? Are you feeling alright?” To which Evie responds with

“Yeah… What would make you think I wasn’t?”

I looked to Phoebe for some kind of reassurance that I wasn’t being completely out of my mind, but she shrugged, so I continued “Well… I was thinking that you didn’t really seem yourself… Normally you wouldn’t be the nicest of people and now you seem much nicer than before”

“Well… I don’t know what to say to that. You were the one who managed to save me from whatever happened to me before, I don’t really know how else I’m supposed to act. Phoebe said that you were the one who organised the whole ritual thing or whatever. Was she right?”

“Yes… she was” I said, rather cautiously, because this all seemed rather weird.

“You’ve kind of earned my respect, is there anything wrong with that?”

“No… Of course there isn’t” I say, still unsure what to think of the whole ordeal.

“Alright then… I guess I’ll see you later, One Eye” Upon hearing that I had some confidence that Evie was back to her former self, maybe it just took a bit of conversation.

I suppose the way I should think of it is the fact that sure, there will be bad and good things, much like I said earlier, but sometimes the bad things can also turn out to work in your favour. Maybe confronting the bad thing will transform it into a good thing. Although, we just have to accept that these bad things are going to inevitably happen and that the good things, whilst they may seem rare, should be appreciated.

Thinking of a list of good things should be a lot easier:

- All my friends (more than before now!),
- Bullying seems to be put to a stop (for now at least),
- School - Now I realise that this is actually a good thing because whilst it may be annoying at times, I should probably appreciate the education… (Although I’ll still probably never understand what my maths teacher is talking about half the time).

And I have a feeling the list of bad things contains near to no items!

Life is a combination of these good and bad things. Thankfully, the good parts of life make life worth living.
