One Eye, One Girl

Currently reading: Chapter 11

Previous Happenings:

Our protagonist thinks about the plan once more... however, these also included second thoughts about the plan after a rather terrible nightmare last night...

I must’ve gone back to sleep at some point, because I wake up for what’s probably the second time today. And instead, I am woken up by the school bell, which is slightly better than being woken up by a nightmare, or is it? I around and thankfully see no blood splatters lining the outskirts of the dormitory, which is good to see. I get out of bed and see Willow had yet again managed to sleep past the school bell, I honestly don’t know how she manages it because the school bell is so loud.

I quickly change into my school uniform and head down to the main hall. I was hoping that no trouble would await me down the stairs, even though there was a big potential for it to. Looking out of the window, not only could I see it was raining, but I could also hear the rain drops hitting the roof. Apparently, according to a few of the books I’ve read, it’s never really a good sign (hopefully the change in weather doesn’t mean much here though).

When I walk into the main hall, it isn’t too busy because it’s much earlier in the morning than I realised. I get some breakfast from the canteen and begin eating as soon as I sit down at one of the many empty tables. I look down at the bowl of porridge and, remembering the chaos which ensued yesterday, hoped that the events of yesterday morning wouldn’t repeat themselves.

I sit in silence, wondering what to do, but then I remember that I had managed to get the book from the library, so I tell myself to try to read some of that today. After all, the book isn’t going to read itself.

I am distracted from my train of thought when I hear Beatrice’s voice behind me, “Morning, Iris!” She then sit down and Amber sits next to her, Beatrice did have a few other friends now that I thought about it, so I’ve found it quite hard to keep track of them all. Giving it some thought, I think she may be friends with the majority of people in our year, apart from the school bullies.

“Good morning” I reply, now looking at the window, all I could see was the raindrops hitting the ground, hundreds of them each second, constantly doing the same over and over.

“The weather doesn’t seem too great today, does it?” I hear Amber say. I couldn’t really disagree because it seemed to be raining quite heavily.

“Yes… it is, but surely it can’t get worse…” I say, I was supposed to say “Yes… it is, but surely it can’t get worse, can it?” However, a sudden sound stops whatever I am saying, it sounded like some sort of rumbling sound.

All students in the main hall go silent, until one student shouts “Thunder!” across the main hall, and instantly I hear everyone’s conversations start up again.

I found this quite annoying considering the good weather yesterday, because now it meant that we wouldn’t be allowed to go outside on the field, or anywhere in general. All this made me realise was that it may be easier to complete the plan. In my mind, because if everyone is staying inside then it could mean that I could sneak off to my dormitory, which was something I wasn’t sure if you could do without being told off, and catch up on some reading.

Looking at the table, I notice that there is no sign of Willow, I guessed she was still asleep. So I told Beatrice that I was going to head back and wake up Willow.

Walking up the stairs, I thought about whether Willow would have woken up or not. Every time I think that she should have been woken by the school bell, or at least the loud rumble of the thunder, In fact, maybe we needed an alarm clock.

On the way, I see some students waling down the corridor, they might end up missing breakfast if they don’t hurry. The rumble of the thunder happens again. It’s not as loud as before, but it still makes some people talk about it.

I push the dormitory door open and find that Willow is still (somehow) asleep. I honestly don’t know how. Because the school bell in my eye seemed to be one of the loudest things on the entire planet, and today there was the added noise of the thunder. Both of these combined didn’t seem to wake her.

I do what I did yesterday and repeatedly say her name, increasing in volume each time.

It doesn’t take too long before she does wake up, and unlike last time, I didn’t need to shout to get her to do so. “You need to get ready…” I say “quite quickly, because assembly starts in..” I check the time on the clock above the door “five minutes” I don’t give her time to say anything, as I leave as soon as I’ve finished.

When I arrive back at the main hall, it is quite busier than before. Admittedly I did quite like the quietness of the main hall before, but everyone needed to be here for assembly. I find Beatrice and Amber sat at what I could have sworn was a different table, but maybe I had forgotten which table I was sat at before.

=====

Once Willow arrives at the table, it isn’t long before the clock indicates it’s half past seven, which was when assembly was due to start. Since Willow had arrived only a few minutes prior, I considered that to be quite lucky, because if you were late, you would be told off.

it doesn’t take long before the head master enters the main hall and stands towards the centre of the large room, the place near the wall, where he usually stood when he does assembly. Everyone turned their heads to see and hear what he was about to say.

“What do you think the assembly will be about today?” I quietly ask everyone who was sat at our table.

=====

It turned out the assembly was, quite predictably, about the poor weather that had hit Hazelwood this morning, we were told the usual things, that physical education would be cancelled, checking my timetable revealed I didn’t have that lesson today, so I would have missed it anyway.

None of what we were told actually mattered to me, because it wasn’t really anything new. Besides, all I wanted to do was to try and do some more reading of the book which I had found earlier this week. While the head master was saying the usual news, I decided to try and figure out when I would be able to read and do my research. Since I had all five lessons today, I guessed I could work on it during break time and lunch time, but I felt as though that wouldn’t be sufficient if I wanted to finish reading it before the end of the project. Obviously, I would be able to work on it during free time, so that would give a little more time.

So that probably gave me around three or four hours to get as much research done as possible, (and that isn’t even counting lunch time or break time). Irritatingly, it didn’t seem like quite as much time as I would have wanted.

After the assembly, had ended, everyone began to head to their first lesson, mine seemed to be art, which I didn’t think would be the worst lesson. The art classroom is past all the dormitories, so as I walked past my dormitory, I slowed my pace and contemplated getting the book from there and continuing with the research, but I didn’t, because I didn’t really want to be late to the lesson. I picked up my pace as I continued along the corridor, as I do this, Evie passes me and says “I should’ve known it was you blocking the corridor” Even though I wasn’t blocking the corridor, I decide to ignore her.

=====

The lessons weren’t too eventful today, I don’t know whether this was a good thing. The first lesson we had, which was art, was probably the most uneventful as we had a supply teacher, this would usually mean that most people would mess around, but not this time. Instead, the teacher we had just told us to illustrate whatever was on our minds. Since it was on my mind at the time, I decided to just draw something relating to the mystery of history we were researching.

By the end of the lesson, on the sheet of paper I had been given I had drawn an eye in the centre of the page and around it, the three different eye colours, blue, green and brown were also around the design, as well as symbols which depicted the sort of personality traits associated with that eye colour.

Science wasn't as interesting as the previous lesson was... we didn't seem to touch upon any of the topics learnt last lesson... which made we wonder if it was actually relevant after all...

After we were dismissed from the science classroom, it was break time, and I decided that the time would be better spent in my dormitory reading that book. So that’s what I do. My goal, at the end of the day, was to try and fill the sheet of paper (which I was using as a bookmark) with as many notes as possible. So I made an effort to quickly jog to my dormitory.

After science, it was break time, and I decided not to attend it because I wanted to try and find as much information as possible before the end of the day, my goal was to try and fill the sheet of paper, which I was currently using as a bookmark, with as many notes as possible. After we were dismissed from the science classroom, I made an effort to quickly jog over to my dormitory, as soon as I arrived and shut the door behind me, I snatched the book off the wooden table and sat down by the side of my bed and opened to page seventy two, which made me realise how much of the book there was still left to read.

I read the book for the majority of break time, when I arrive at the next heading in the book, I decide to stop reading, I had made it to page eighty six which could have been better. At least now the piece of paper is almost half full with notes.

As I go to place the book back on the wooden table, I hear the school bell and then the sound of the door opening. I was hoping it wasn’t one of the teachers, because I knew I would be in some deep water. Thankfully, it wasn’t one of the teachers, instead it was Beatrice.

Since she seems out of breath, I ask her why she is here. She replies with “I’m looking for my geography homework, you haven’t seen it, have you?” I tell her that I hadn’t, which made me remember about my own, so I checked under my bed and, much to my relief, it’s still there. I snatch it up and show it to Beatrice.

“Did it look like this?” I ask my friend

She thinks for a few moments before responding with “Yes… If I remember correctly” My friend then begins to search my dormitory.

I had the feeling that she wouldn’t find it here, because she would have most likely kept it in her dormitory, “I’m guessing you’ve checked your dormitory already?”

“Yes… but it wasn’t there...” my friend tells me “The only places I can think of where I may have left the homework are the library and your dormitory”

“Well… I doubt it will be here” I say, beginning to lose hope slightly quicker than I wanted to, “But did you check the library?”

“Yeah… I guess, but I’m still checking here first” Beatrice continues to search the dormitory and she then begins to try to make conversation as she does so, “Anyway… What were you doing before? Because you shouldn’t really be in your dormitory at break time…”

“I figured, but you shouldn’t be in your dormitory at this time either”

“Alright, I know…” Beatrice says “But I was trying to find my homework and I can only assume you were doing more research”

“That’s why I’m here” I agree, “But I was about to head to next lesson” which makes me remember that the bell has already rung. I think it’s geography next which is why Beatrice probably came to search for her geography homework.

“Oh… I might make us both late, huh… we should probably head there now and I’ll face the consequences”

Even with this being said, Beatrice continues to search for the missing assignment for a few more minutes. It isn’t long before she admits defeat and says “You know what… I don’t think it’s going to turn up, so let’s go to geography”

On the way to our geography lesson, Beatrice comments “You know… you’re just like my brother” I don’t actually remember Beatrice saying she had a brother before.

“Why’s that?” I ask.

In fact, she was bringing about the question of siblings, which I hadn’t actually considered, but I was quite glad I didn’t have any brothers and sisters. They’d probably just bully me for having one eye. My parents told me that I would fit in, but they were clearly wrong, otherwise I wouldn’t be so interested in all of this, in fact… that’s given me an idea… (which probably won’t work).

Beatrice continues and distracts my train of thought, “Well… if I remember correctly, I think he’s actually researching the exact same thing” I was a tiny bit shocked to hear this because, if Beatrice was correct, then I wasn’t the only one researching this mystery of history 70 years later. Initially, I thought I would be the only one, turns out I was wrong.

“Do you think he’s found any info that could be of use?” I ask, checking my pockets and finding the piece of paper which I had been writing on before and then realising how it was supposed to be the bookmark “That’s annoying…” I mumble to myself, but I decide to show it to Beatrice and then explain how it’s what I had found out so far.

She reads through my quite scruffy handwriting and eventually draws to a conclusion “He doesn’t really tell me any of the information he researches because he might be afraid I misuse it. To be honest, he hasn’t actually told me the reason though…”

I found this a bit strange because I don’t understand why Beatrice’s brother would hide this sort of information from her. I didn’t understand because this mystery was over 70 years old for goodness sake!

“Fair enough, I guess… But It is quite annoying,” I say as I’m handed the piece of paper back, “especially as now we’re researching that exact thing.”

“Yeah, I guess it is” Beatrice says.
