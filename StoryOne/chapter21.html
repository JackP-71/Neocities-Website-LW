When we arrived at the dormitory, I opened the door, making sure it wouldn’t creak, and when we walked in, I shut the door behind me and we all dumped our belongings on the floor. My two friends sat on Willow’s bed, I sat on mine and whispered “Well… It actually worked… I can’t believe it”

“I also can’t believe it” Willow says “I was almost dead”

“Well… apart from that horrifying moment, everything else went to plan”

“Alright then…”

“Anyway, I don’t know about you two, but I’m really tired…” I say

“Same…” Willow says

“I guess we could try to get some rest” Beatrice says, then she shines her torch on the clock “We have about 2 hours before school starts”

“Yes… we should at least try to go to sleep” I say

=====

When I awoke the next morning, waking up with two eyes instead of one isn’t too different, I was still quite tired, we did manage to get around half an hour’s worth of sleep, but I didn’t think it was enough to power through the entire school day. I sat up from my sleeping position on the floor, I see that Beatrice wasn’t here, she must have returned to her own dormitory. I stand up and start to get ready for the day ahead, no doubt a boring and tiring one, However, as I start to get ready, I hear a loud voice start to echo down the corridor, it was one of the teachers. I opened the dormitory door slightly and listened into what the teacher had to say so early in the morning “There will be an emergency assembly in a few minutes, please make your way to the main hall as soon as possible!” As soon as I had heard this, I was instantly intrigued, I quickly changed into my school uniform and hurried down the stairs to the main hall.

When I arrived at the main hall, a few students were already here, they were mainly younger years though. I took a seat at one of the many tables and awaited the arrival of my two friends, I wondered who would arrive first, I was still quite tired and I could imagine my friends would be the same, however, it didn’t take long before one of them eventually arrived, however, it was who had arrived that had surprised me, Willow had wandered into the main hall, and this surprised me because she would normally be one of the last people to enter the main hall, however, today she was one of the first.

“Morning!” She says, slightly more enthusiastic than usual, I guess the spell had worked after all, because she normally stays quiet, but today seemed to be different.

“Morning…” I say, feeling slightly, no, very tired after yesterday’s adventure.

It didn’t actually take that long (when comparing today to all the other school days) for everyone to arrive at the main hall. We had never had an emergency assembly before, or at least, I hadn’t, although I wasn’t too sure whether or not Beatrice and Willow might have had this type of assembly before. I don’t think they would have, I was slightly, scared, what on earth had happened whilst we were gone? When roughly everyone had arrived, the head teacher walked up to where he usually stands when he does an assembly, I notice that he has a slightly flustered expression on her face, after telling everyone to quiet down, he begins to tell us why we were called down here so early and at such short notice.

“Today I have called you here because… Because someone in our school has been found dead”

As soon as he had said this, a hundred questions entered my mind, who had died? How did they die? And why weren’t they telling us who? Were just a few of the hundred, however, there was one thought which overpowered all of these questions, I had a terrible feeling that we may have caused it.

I looked around the hall, everyone seemed to be there, even the teachers. One thing was different though, it was much quieter than usual, maybe it was the news, or maybe one of the people who seem to speak a lot had died.

=====

It didn’t take too long before I was able to ultimately confirm that someone had died. I think school were attempting to cover up this fact, and also who had died, however, I’d say I have a fairly good idea on who had died. I suppose there were the obvious rumours spreading around like wildfire and there was also the quieter classrooms (mainly due to the few people missing in our classes). But apart from all of this, school hadn’t actually told us much, well… explicitly at least.

Although they had also made it fairly obvious, I mean, in the classes we had, there was one name which all teachers failed to call when doing the registers, I should have probably noticed this earlier, especially since it had been two days since we had had that emergency assembly.

And speaking of that assembly, it seemed to have made school a completely different place. It seemed much quieter now, everyone seemed on edge, like something wasn’t right, which it wasn’t. It didn’t seem right. It was also hard to tell whether or not it was the death or what we had done to change everyone’s personality traits two days ago that had made the school seem different.

 

but I don’t really pay much attention to them anymore, I just listen out for my name and say “here”.

And, Evie wouldn’t be able to say “here” anymore… and, I was actually quite surprised at this.

I suppose the main goal was to mitigate the bullying which kept happening constantly, and when we arrived back at school, the atmosphere at Hazelwood had changed, this was probably because we had changed everyone’s personality traits, by removing the curse (which I’m still not sure what to call it, a spell or a curse?) two days prior. It might also have something to do with how the bullying had finally come to an end. I had two eyes now, so there was no reason for it, or at least I don’t think there was…

However, the main goal to mitigate all of the bullying was successful, but I wasn’t expecting to kill the root cause of all the bullying.

So, I might as well also point out that Evie’s two friends were seemingly no longer in any of our classes right now. Although, unlike Evie I had actually seen Phoebe and Maisie, so I knew they were still alive.

To be honest, more research probably could have told us someone was going to die, and how we may be able to prevent her death. But, now I reflect back on the last few weeks, the plan was, in all honesty, rushed. We probably could have spent a few more days investigating, however, I was determined to put an end to all of this bullying as quickly as possible. However, what was in the past now remained in the past and wouldn’t return to the present.
