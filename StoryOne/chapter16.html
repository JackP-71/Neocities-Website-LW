One Eye, One Girl

Currently reading: Chapter 16

Previous Happenings:

The three protagonists set off on their quest and entered the forest near Hazelford. Along the forested path, a peculiar note was found... was someone onto them and their adventure..?

The note may not have been addressed to me, but I was still quite shocked. I read it again, just to make sure I wasn’t imagining what it said. Nope. The letters remained on the page. Even with one eye, I could tell it was real.

When I had made sure I hadn’t envisioned what I had read on the piece of paper, I sprinted as fast as I could to catch up with my two friends, making sure to no trip over any loose branches

“Look what I just found…” I say and I shove the small piece of paper into Willow’s hand, Beatrice turns back and shines the torch into Willow’s hand, the piece of paper was now visible, and I could definitely see what was written on the piece of paper. Willow and Beatrice read the piece of paper

“Do any of you recognise the handwriting on the piece of paper?” I ask, Willow shrugs, but Beatrice looks at me with raised eyebrows, she looked worried, I could understand why, the note had been written to her after all. She doesn’t say anything though.

I turn back to see if anyone is there, hiding in the shadows, but I find no one. As we continue walking, I almost trip over a tree branch

“Watch out” Beatrice says and after I recover from almost falling over, she asks me “are you alright?” I nod my head and we carry on walking; Beatrice seems to have quickened her pace after seeing that letter. I had so many questions in my head after seeing that letter, how did the person who wrote the letter know my friend’s name? Why did I and Beatrice seem worried by it and why was Willow seemingly unbothered by it? Even though I wanted to try and find out the answers to the questions inside my brain, I couldn’t be distracted from the quest at hand. I had to continue onwards.

So that was what we did for a few minutes, so far, I was the only one who had managed to nearly fall over, but we still ventured through the unforgiving plants.

After a few minutes I decide to ask my friends “How long do you think it will take?” As I ask this, Willow nearly trips over a loose root in the soil.

“I’m not sure… we could be walking through this forest for ages” Willow says

“I don’t think so…” says Beatrice as she shines her torch over to where a small structure was located, the structure, from what I could see, was covered in a grey stone exterior and the roof looked to be made of slate, some of the slate tiles were missing and some of the stones were covered in moss “Do you think that could be it?” she asked

“There’s only one way to find out…” I say and Willow lets out a quiet sigh.

 

It takes a few moments to navigate through all of the wild flowers and lost branches, but we eventually make it to the abandoned stone structure. Beatrice then looks behind us, probably to make sure that no one is there. We eventually come to the quiet conclusion that I should be the one to head into the building first, so I grab the torch from Beatrice and open the wooden door, which opens with a creak, the structure must have been left lying here without a purpose for a good few years. But I eventually managed to open the door and I then decided to head into the small building.

If it weren’t for the torch, I wouldn’t have been able to see anything, however thankfully the torch managed to illuminate the surroundings with ease. The torch manages to light up a weak wooden table, which looks so fragile that any pressure that is placed on it could cause it to break under its own weight. However, there was only one thing placed on this wooden table, a piece of paper, another folded piece of paper, this one was roughly the same size as the other piece of paper I had found a few minutes earlier. I picked it up and unfolded it, on the piece of paper. As my friends stepped into the building, I shone the torch onto the piece of paper and read the note.

This ends now Beatrice.

 

“Who on earth is writing these pieces of paper?” I say, slightly louder than necessary. I show it to Beatrice, who reads it and then whispers to me

“I have a terrible idea on who is writing these notes…” Beatrice hands the piece of paper back to me, I place it in my blazer pocket and continue to search the building. As I walked around the building, looking for potential clues, I realise that the exterior of the building certainly seemed smaller than the interior of the building was.

“Should we see what is in that room?” Willow asks, me and Beatrice both agree

I started to wander over to the doorway and I pointed the torch into the room, I looked to see what the torch had lit up, there was a strange glow being emitted from one of the tables on the far side of the room, it was a purple glow, quite like the one I had seen on the book cover of the book I had read to gain all of the information about this mystery of history. I was about to walk over the piece of paper, when Willow walked into the room and said “Wait… do you think it’s a good idea to go anywhere near that glow?”

“I was just going to see what it was. What’s the worst that could happen?” Willow just sighed and said

“Fine… but if it does anything, then don’t say I didn’t warn you.” I decided to ignore Willow’s warning as I continued to step closer and closer towards the mysterious purple glow.

I decided to test how bright the glow actually was, so I turned the torch off, Willow gave a gasp, as we were all plunged into darkness, the purple glow was quite bright, it appeared to illuminate the entire room. I decided to keep the torch turned off as I continued my way towards the glow.

When I had made it to the desk, I saw that the glow consisted of light purple stars, they were all bundled together to make it seem darker than it actually was, I turned the torch back on, and noticed how there was an inkwell, and a fountain pen by the side of it. I had also discovered that the glow belonged to a piece of A4 paper, I felt like I knew exactly what this was, I was about to tell my friends of my discovery. But then I heard a strange creaking sound, I think it probably came from the front door.

“Did anyone hear that?” Willow said with a shudder in her voice.

“Yes… But it was probably nothing…” I say, trying to avoid talking about the huge possibility of being killed in a strange stone building in the middle of nowhere, just like I had had that nightmare about a few days ago. Instead, I decided to tell my Beatrice and Willow about what I had just found. “I think I have just found some vital evidence” I say, trying to block out the noise of the creaking floorboards.

“What have you found?” Beatrice asks, turning to the doorframe in an attempt to see if anyone was there, I was about to point to the desk, but since I wasn’t sure how to describe the enchanted piece of paper, I just tell my friends

“I’ve found a piece of paper and a fountain pen, take a look for yourselves…” I faced the torch towards the desk and Willow and Beatrice wandered over to the desk and they both looked at the piece of paper. I told them both

“I think this is what we have been looking for this whole time…”

I looked over to the wall closest to the desk, there looked to be something slightly strange carved into the stones that had probably been placed many years ago. I asked Beatrice if she could hold the torch for a moment, she took it and I told her to point it at the stones and when she did, I noticed that some words had been carved into the stones. I placed my bag down on the floor and got my notepad out, I then proceeded to attempt to decipher what words were written in the stones

Here is what I had written in my notepad:

When the bell rings once. Three people will chant these words with great energy and all will be undone.

I then looked around the room to see if any other walls appeared to have any words carved into them, I looked around and eventually found that one of the walls, with no furniture blocking it seemed to have quite a lot of words, well… what I presume are words, carved into it. I told Beatrice to bring the light of the torch over to the wall I had just turned to, when she does, I notice a few more words, I then started once again to decode the stones.

In the end, this is what I had written in my notepad:

When the bell rings once. Three people will chant these words with great energy and all will be undone.

The sorcerers who made this spell and the sorceresses who made it happen. All work shall be wasted once all is said and done. Remove the curse. Revert everything back to normal. This ends everything right now.

 

It had taken more than a few days to get to this point, but the end seemed to be in sight now. I said thanks to Beatrice as I looked down at my notepad, I then look back at my friends and tell them both

“I have just found something else” I say “There are some words which have been carved into the stone”

“You may have found that…” Willow interrupts me “But that still doesn’t mean we are any closer to finding out how to fix this…”

“I haven’t finished what I was saying…” I tell her

“Well then… Carry on…” Beatrice says

“Ok… So, I have found some words carved into the stones and this is what they say” I show Willow and Beatrice my notepad, Beatrice shines the torch onto the paper with the words that I had found from the carvings in the stones.

“Yeah… But those words could change anything… They might not just change the personality traits of everyone” Willow says, after reading from my notepad. At this point, I wanted to disagree with her because we were so close to completing this quest, but instead, I found myself agreeing with her, the group of sorcerers and sorceresses could have made and done another curse that we didn’t research.

“Well… we could at least do it and find out…” Is all I tell her

“We could… But then if it is wrong, then there is no way to reverse it” And this is when I stumbled back into silence. I didn’t know how to reverse it, even if it was right, we couldn’t go back once we had done it.

“On your piece of paper” Beatrice says, interrupting the silence “it says that a bell, I’m guessing it will be a bell which tells you the time, will ring three times. So that means that we have to do it at 3 o’ clock”

“I kind of guessed that when I read it” I tell Beatrice “But I have no clue what time it is right now” When I say this, Willow starts to search through the bag she brought and she pulls something which looks like a watch, Beatrice moves the torch light and she tells us both

“It doesn’t look great, if we want to do it, we will probably only have one go at it” I was about to ask her what time it was when she answers the question before I have even asked it “We have about 2 minutes left” We had 2 minutes to decide whether or not we would change everyone’s personality traits, I was considering the two options we had when Beatrice interrupts my thinking “If we don’t do it now, then we’ll have to wait until tomorrow and risk leaving the school again.”

“Yes…” Willow agrees “But what if it is entirely the wrong thing… What if the spell we are about to reverse does something which we do not want it to? Also, how are we supposed to change it if it goes wrong…”

“Well… We could give it a go…” Beatrice says “And even if it does go wrong, then we can just research how to change it back and keep the old curse” I was hoping it would be that easy, but some of me knew that it would not be easy.

I started to weigh up my options. If we were to do it, then it could be wrong or it could be right, if it is wrong, then Beatrice would have been right, we would have to find a way to reverse the spell, bring it back once we had managed to remove it. However, if it is right, then we would have managed to save everyone from the curse, and there would potentially be no more bullies in the school, well… there would probably still be a few, but most certainly not as many as before

“Time is running out…” Beatrice reminds us “We have 20 seconds to make a decision now”

“Ok… One second” I say

“We don’t really have one second…” Beatrice almost shouts, I can tell she’s starting to get slightly annoyed. I sigh as I continue to think of the options we have once more, if we do not do it, then we will miss an opportunity to get it right, however if it was wrong, then at least we won’t have done it, but if it was right all along, then would miss our opportunity to complete this quest and we would have to risk leaving the school again, without getting into trouble. I think about it for a few more seconds

“Ten seconds to make a choice” Beatrice reminds me and Willow.

“I’ll do it” Willow says

I think about it, the two options we had, we could potentially save the world or completely ruin it

I weigh up the options.

Before I come to a conclusion.

“And I will do it too,” I say.
