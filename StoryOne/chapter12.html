One Eye, One Girl

Currently reading: Chapter 12

Previous Happenings:

After Iris discovers that Beatrice's brother was also investigating this mystery, she tries to find some info, but to no avail... she discovered this on the way to their geography lesson, which they were late for...

I was wondering what would happen when we walked into the geography classroom, would we be told off for being late? I viewed that as being most likely. However, when we walked into the classroom I was met with a very different sight.

I looked through the window positioned on the door and saw everyone talking to one another, I saw the two school bullies quickly head to the back of the classroom. I opened the door and stepped into the classroom, when I did I turned and saw the two bullies waiting in anticipation for something. It all made sense when I heard something shuffle behind me and look the table beside me and see Evie standing on the desk. She then releases a pile of leaves, which I was guessing she had taken from the field (even if we weren’t allowed out today), from her grasp. The leaves then land on my head as she says “Welcome to Geography!”

I sigh as I try to remove the different leaves from my hair, some of the leaves were still damp from the rain. Beatrice then walks into the classroom and begins removing the leaves as well. At least she made her small act of bullying relate to the lesson I suppose.

Usually, geography is one of those lessons which wasn’t too tough and not too much that I wasn’t learning something from it. But today’s lesson was different as there was no teacher for the entire lesson. I thought a teacher would have appeared because of all the noise, but no one showed up. It would sometimes be the case where teachers wouldn’t be able to arrive at school due to the weather and I think today was one of those days.

At least Beatrice and I wouldn’t have to hand in our missing or incomplete homework assignments.

=====

The chaos continued for the entire lesson and during the hour, I saw some things which were probably not typical of schoolgirls etiquette. For example, Evie stole all the glue sticks from the font of the classroom and began throwing them across the room, even though I think she was trying to hit me with them, the majority of them hit other students, I think I got lucky there. I then saw many paper aeroplanes fly across the room, some of them travelling from person to person.

Aside from all this, the lesson seemed to be going quite quickly. Although, truth be told, I wasn’t that happy with this lesson, because I could have been in my dormitory doing some more reading; however, I was unsure what would have happened if any of the teachers would have seen me heading to the dormitories. There would have been no point sending me back to my lesson, because there was no teacher to teach anything. Even if I did get caught, Evie would tell me off for bringing a teacher there.

Although whilst we were in the geography lesson, I had started to think about something, about the idea I had on the way here. What if it was possible to change everyone’s personality traits? Willow was the other person sat on my table, she had her geography book open and was checking the notes she had made last time, she must have been one of the only ones to get her book from the front of the classroom.

“I’ve just had a thought” I tell her, slightly louder than I would have liked to be heard over the sounds of the noisy students.

“Oh… Ok… What was your thought?” she quietly asks, so I have to listen closely to what she’s saying.

“I was thinking back to the science lesson we had on Monday…”

“Ok…”

“I was thinking about how the different sorcerers and sorceresses had managed to change everyone’s personality traits to what they are now”

“Ok… So, what were you thinking then?”

“I was thinking about how we could potentially fix everyone’s personality traits…”

I can just about see that she rolls her eyes as she whispers “What on earth…”

“Ok… I know it sounds a bit weird…” And the idea did admittedly sound strange when I had said it out loud “but if the group of sorcerers and sorceresses had managed to change everyone’s personality traits, then surely there is a way to change them back to what they would have been before, right?”

After I had said this, Willow fell silent for a few moments before telling me “I don’t know Iris, I guess we will have to see what Beatrice thinks of that…”

“We shall have to see…”

I then hear Willow say to herself “I mean… How are you even going to accomplish that?”

Shortly after my conversation with Willow, the school bell sounded and everyone practically dashed out of the classroom. I held back a bit to check my timetable and found I had maths next. Since Beatrice was sat at another table in the geography lesson, I would have to see whether she agreed with my idea.

=====

When I walked into the maths classroom, I saw Beatrice sat at her usual seat, so Willow and I decide to sit next to her.

“Hi, you two, did you find the geography lesson interesting?” Beatrice jokingly asks, once she has noticed that we had sat down.

“Well.. there wasn’t much to learn, so at least I didn’t have to concentrate on it that much” I reply.

Willow jumps right to the chase by saying, “Yeah… In fact, Iris wanted to run something by you…”

“Me? Alright, what is it, Iris?”

“Well… I had a thought in geography, which I’m now second guessing… but I was wondering if it were possible to change everyone’s personality traits back to what they were before…”

“And you’re thinking about this, why exactly?”

“I was thinking about it because of… well… I’m always bullied, even today I had a pile of soggy leaves thrown on me”

“If you want my honest opinion,” Beatrice says “I don’t think we know enough about this to even consider this even happening.” She did have a point because we had only been researching this for nearly a week, it was just a thought, but I was wondering if it was possible. ”And besides” My friend continues “What do you mean by ‘before’? Were you thinking before they did the spell or something like that?”

“Yes… Because there was a group of people who changed it and I wonder if it would be any different if it was reverted back to what it was before.”

Willow whispers under her breath “When are you going to stop thinking about that science lesson.”

To my surprise Beatrice responds with “I kind of get what you’re saying,” Which kind of gets my hopes up, but then she says “but like I said before, I don’t think we know enough about this to do that”

“I know… but I’ll continue to do my research and one day we might be able to!” I think I might be a bit too hopeful, but that’s better than having zero hope.

Giving it some thought, I suppose it did seem impossible; we couldn’t really try to leave Hazelwood because we were here pretty much all the time. In fact, the only times we were allowed to leave school would be when we go on school trips and when our parents come to school to see us. I think that our parents would be visiting at the end of the month, so it would be quite food to change everyone’s personality traits so they would be able to see our actual personality traits. I guess it would be interesting to see what my parent’s personality traits would be, as well as my two friends.

As the teacher walks into the classroom, I say to Beatrice “I guess you are right…” and during the lesson, I begin to rethink my plan to try and change everyone’s personality traits.

=====

When it’s lunch time, I decide to do the same as break time and rush to my dormitory in an attempt to do some more reading and note making. About half way through the maths lesson, the thought I had in geography had left my mind. However, arriving at the dormitory and seeing the book on the table invited it back.

When I had told them about it, neither of them seemed supportive of the idea of leaving school, which wasn’t really allowed anyway, and trek to a location unknown and reverse the spell which had been cast all those years ago. To be honest, I couldn’t blame them, because even I knew that more research had to be done since we barely knew anything. Only a few notes which I had found so far.

I don’t think one book would be enough to qualify as having enough information, so I would probably have to see if there was another book on the same topic in the library. If I found one, then I could also validate some of the information from the science lesson and from the first book for certain.

Whilst reading the book, I realise that I always sit by the side of my bed instead of sitting on it. I suppose it’s because it feels more productive to be away from bed, if I was sat on my bed, I would probably feel like falling asleep, which wouldn’t help in getting the research done.

=====

After lunch time, the final few lessons seemed to fly by, thankfully we did have a teacher for all three of them. One of the lessons we had was history, so we managed to complete some more of the project. I had managed to add some of the facts I had learnt from reading the book so far. In fact, we had only been working on this project for not even a week and we had already managed to fill half of the A3 paper.

When me and Willow arrive back at our dormitory, I look out of the window and find it is still raining. I then pick up the book from where I left it last and continue reading.

Reading this book just felt like second nature at this point. Although, I think Beatrice had a point because I had read about 100 pages at this point and it was getting rather monotonous and dull. During this period of free time, I was tempted to shut the book and have a break from reading it. I suppose it was 485 pages which was a lot to read in 17 days. In the end, I decided to stop reading when I got to a heading which said:

Discovering where it all happened.

I placed my bookmark, which was covered in quite a few notes now back in the book. I saw that I was up to page 94 which meant there was still a considerable amount of the book left to read.

I place the book back on the table and begin to flick through the pages and seeing a few headings which seemed like they would be the answer to some of my questions. However, towards the back of the book, it looks to be some wild speculations, I viewed that as a success as it may mean I only have to read 200 or even 150 pages instead of the 485 pages I thought I may have to.

With that discovered, I placed the book on the square wooden table and sat on my bed, staring at the clock, waiting for it to be dinner time.
