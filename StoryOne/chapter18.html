One Eye, One Girl

Currently reading: Chapter 18

Previous Happenings:

Ok... maybe they may have done it wrong... if they actually did it, but, Beatrice's brother, Archie turned up and told them what was going on... so hopefully it goes well now.

“So… Are you really sure that this is a good idea?” Willow asks as we trudge back into the village once more.

“Yes… I am absolutely sure.” Archie replies

To be honest, I wasn’t so sure myself. What we had planned to do was head into the village and apparently one of the sorcerer’s houses was left completely abandoned when he passed away. So, what we were going to try to do was sneak into the house and see if there was a vial of liquid stored in the house, according to Archie, what we would have to do is do the spell, however, as soon as we had done the spell, we were supposed to try and reverse it a few moments after. The vial of liquid we would be looking for was apparently some sort of reversing potion. This potion would hopefully reverse the spell we had yet to do. We were told it would not reverse everything, it would only reverse it to the point where everyone’s personality traits would have changed back to what they would have been before, but their eye colours wouldn’t change, although, now that I think about it, surely, they wouldn’t have changed anyone’s eye colour, surely, they would have only changed people’s personality traits. Thinking about it some more, it made me realise that maybe the sorcerers and sorceresses had done the spell to find out potentially who had what sort of personality (although, to be honest, I don’t really know how they would have known what sort of personality trait everyone had). I decided not to think about it too much, that was mainly because I couldn’t, the only thing on my mind right now was not being caught.

“Here we are,” Archie told us, as we arrived back at Hazelford, he continued walking until we arrived at a wooden house, this house was the only one in the small village that was made of wood (I have no idea why), the wood had certainly seen better days, it was rotting at the end of each plank and there was ivy crawling up the various different planks. The roof was red (I wasn’t sure if that meant anything, I thought I should just note it down anyway). Once we had all built the courage to walk up to the house, I was the one to open the dark blue gate, when I opened it, it gave a long groan as it opened. When it had opened, we all walked through the open it had left and we walked to the wooden door, it has a small window which seemed to be tinted purple and the door was made of the same wood the entire exterior was made out of, so if it wasn’t for the door handle and the fact that it was indented into the main structure of the house, you probably wouldn’t have been able to see it. It also had a small staircase leading up to it, so it wouldn’t have gone completely unnoticed.

I took a glance back to the gate, no one was here, no one apart from us. Whilst my head was turned, I heard a door creak in agony as it opened, I first thought it might have been someone who had finally figured out what we were doing, sneaking into someone’s house (someone who was very much dead). However, when I turned back, I saw Archie pulling the door open. I sighed in relief when the door’s groaning finally came to a stop. Beatrice pointed the torch to the inside of the building and I saw that the door we were standing by led to a hallway, which then led to a room, which looked to be a kitchen. To the left of the hallway, there was a staircase and there was a room to the right side of the hallway. Archie told us that we should split up and Beatrice asked him why he was bossing us about, he ignored her as he told her and Willow to check upstairs and that I should check the kitchen, so that’s what we all did, Beatrice and Willow headed upstairs and Archie headed into the room to the right side of the hallway, which as I walked past, appeared to be a front room, it looked like quite a normal front room, considering this house had been abandoned for potentially 70 or more years.

When I walked into the kitchen, the faint glow of the street lamps glowed through the windows, so it wasn’t complete darkness. As I walked further into the kitchen, the smell of mouldy food became stronger. I walked towards the many different cupboards, there were quite a few of them, all waiting to be searched, until I eventually found the vial, or anyone else found it before I did. It could be anywhere.

I started off my search by checking on top of the kitchen counters, I first checked a bread bin, when I opened it, I saw a loaf of bread which had a best before date of 190 years ago, I shut the bread bin and started to check the cupboards (there were at least 7 different cupboards to check).

I was starting to lose hope, thinking that I wouldn’t ever find what we were looking for, no one had said anything about finding it yet, it had definitely been a few minutes.

However, it just so happened that checking the kitchen seemed to be beneficial, because as I opened the fifth cupboard, I was greeted with nothing, there was nothing in the cupboard, that was until I looked down at the bottom shelf and found a small cylindrical vial of liquid with a lid on it, it was sort of like a test tube you would find in a science lab. I picked it up and held it up to the light, the vial seemed to be filled with a strange purple liquid. Was this it? Had I found it? I called to anyone who could hear me “I think I’ve found something!”

I could hear the creaking of the floorboards above me, as Beatrice and Willow hurried down the stairs, Archie had also walked into the kitchen. I show Archie the vial as both of my friends rush into the kitchen and as they come to Beatrice asks,

“What have you found?”

“I think I’ve found the reversing potion your brother had mentioned” I tell her. However, this vial wasn’t the only thing which I had noticed, I had also seen a note written on lined paper, however, something to note, the hand writing didn’t align with the lines already drawn on the page, I picked up the note and muttered what it had said

Need to throw out at some point towards the future.

“So is this it?” I ask Archie “Is this what you said we had to find?”

“I believe it is” Is all he says. And that was all I needed to hear.

=====

Archie had told us that we needed to get back to the stone building before the sun started to rise. So that’s exactly what we did.

So, you join us as we are all sprinting back into the forest and heading back to the stone building as quickly as we could.

It took a shorter amount of time than I was expecting to arrive at the stone building, (that was probably because we were running rather quickly, obviously quicker than I had thought). When we had arrived, we pushed the door open and found that we were not alone, there were some bats hanging from the ceiling, they must have entered through a gap in the roof, once they had detected our presence, they made a swift exit through the door and I turned to see them fly into the moonlight. We walked into the room where the piece of paper was kept, it was still there, untouched, so I guessed that no one else had entered the building whilst we had been gone.

When we all arrived into the room, Archie started to give us some instructions “Right… So you all need to stand in a circle formation, like you did when I arrived here, and then, if my research is correct, each of you will need to drink 3 drops of the purple liquid and once you have done that, you will need to do what you did, when you hear the bell go, it’s nearly 2 o’ clock now,”

“But did it not have to be midnight?” I ask

“No… I think you must have done something wrong, because when I ventured out here to translate the words written in the stone, it said when the bell rings twice, not once.”

“Oh right…” I say, understanding the mistake I had made, thankfully we hadn’t missed our chance to try and change the school and potentially even the school.

“So you’ll stand in the circle formation and chant the words to reverse the spell” He tells us the words which I had already managed to translate from the stones “and then a few moments later you will drink the purple liquid, if my research is correct, then you will chant the words to make sure the spell still works” Archie then points to the piece of paper and tells us “The words you will need to say to get the spell working are written down on here” I spend a few seconds trying to memorise it, however, then Archie tells us “Time is running out, it’s nearly 2 o’ clock”

When we had heard this, me and my friends all looked away from the piece of paper, we formed a circle and joined our hands together, Archie dashed back to the first room (I’m not entirely sure as to why). And as soon as we heard the bell of the small clock tower ring once and twice, we began to start chanting the words, which would change the world, potentially, forever.

“The sorcerers who made this spell and the sorceresses who made it happen. All work shall be wated once all is said and done. Remove the curse! Revert everything back to normal! This ends everything right now!” I had my doubts when I said “This ends everything right now” I was worried the end of the world might have come, however, we were still alive after we had finished speaking.

“Nothing has happened… My brother lied to us all” Beatrice started to say

I started to think that, we were still alive, nothing had happened at all… Or at least that’s what I thought, because all of a sudden, I heard a rumble and everything in the stone house started to shake. What was happening? Was it the end of the world? Surely not…

“What’s going on?” Asks Beatrice

I was about to respond with “I have no idea” however, I wasn’t able to speak, because a bright purple sphere had started to form between us, I moved away, or at least I tried to. The purple sphere seemingly erupted, sending a huge shockwave across the room, hitting me and both my friends. We were thrown back to the walls. I thought I was dead.
