One Eye, One Girl

Currently reading: Chapter 8

Previous Happenings:

After not really discovering anything the first time... Iris decides to head to the library in the pursuit of information about the science lesson during lunch time.

I quickly walked through the main hall, the lunch queue already building up, but that happened all the time. I see Evie, Maisie and Phoebe near the front of the queue, which I guessed made sense because they had skipped the history lesson.

After walking through the main hall, I walked up the stairs, each stair bringing me closer to potential evidence. I was beginning to wonder what books I would come across. Usually, if I was in the library, I would probably be doing homework, I never really looked at the books they had there.

When I arrive at the door which kept the quiet library separated from the noisy corridors, I push open the door and step past the threshold. The door closes, silencing the noise behind me. I begin to look around, very much amazed at how manty books the school had managed to fit in this one room. Once I arrived at the ‘Mysteries of History’ bookcase, I place my belongings on a nearby table and remember about the geography bookcase. I unfold the piece of spare paper and add ‘Geography’ to the list of categories.

I used my notes to quickly refresh myself with the list of things I had learnt in science.

  • Blue eyes mean you lead a life of sorrow,
  • Green eyes mean you are jealous (and sometimes greedy),
  • Brown eyes mean you are a kind an generous person,
  • Different coloured eyes mean you have good luck – the different eye colour traits may clash and you may end up with two main personality traits,
  • If you only have one eye, then it is seen as a sign of extreme bad luck (not really what I wanted to hear).

Even with the notes from the science lesson, I felt as though there was quite a bit to be discovered.

And then, this is the part where I think more research can be done to find out more, because I think there are some gaps that that can be filled here.

  • A group of sorcerers and sorceresses had joined together around 70 years ago and created some sort of spell to find out what eye colour everyone had,
  • They then created some sort of spell to give everyone different personality traits,
  • The group of sorcerers and sorceresses were either formed in a village called Hazelford or a coastal town called Rose Cove

Especially on that last point, maybe the geography section might be able to help with that.

Anyway, these were the some of the main notes I had written down from the science lesson, it seemed quite a lot when I looked at it in front of me, but I felt as though I could fill some of the missing gaps of knowledge.

I decided to leave the piece of paper and my science book on the table as I stood up to begin the search of the bookcases. I hear the library door shut just as I am about to begin looking through the ‘Mysteries of History’ bookcase. Curiosity gets the better of me, so I turn around and find Beatrice walking over to where I am.

I ask her, “What are you doing here?”

She then replies “I could be asking you the exact same thing, it is lunch time after all.”

I decided to answer her question first, “As you may or may not know, I’m looking for more information to add to our history project” I was hoping that once I had answered her question, she would answer mine.

“You never told me about that,” Beatrice thinks for a moment before unconfidently saying, “or did you?”

I think for a few moments before saying “Yeah, I’m pretty sure I told you yesterday” After I had said this, I started to have doubts, did I tell her yesterday? I was sure I did, but at the same time, I was sure I hadn’t. To remove the doubts from my head.

To prevent myself overthinking it, I decide to change the topic of conversation by stating the obvious “Anyway, there are a lot of books here, aren’t there?”

“I know” Beatrice says “It’s quite incredible how many books they actually managed to fit in here”

“Yeah… I know” I say, and as I turn to look back at the bookcase, I realise that it’s quite taller than I am, so I look around and find a ladder with wheels at the bottom of it, I suppose it’s used for reaching the books at the top of the bookcase, I grab hold of the ladder and wheel it over to the bookcase.

It was all going quite well, well, it was going quite well, until the ladder started to fall, my heart skipped a beat. Thankfully, and quite luckily, Beatrice saw this and quickly ran over to the ladder and steadied it, “Thanks” I say, as we continue to wheel it over to where the bookcase is positioned.

“No problem” she replies as we arrive at the bookcase, we prop the ladder against the bookcase and as I start to climb it, Beatrice asks “What on earth are you doing?”

“Well, it’s like I may have told you yesterday. I’m going to try and see if I can find any books relating to the science lesson we had yesterday” I explain, ever so slightly louder than usual, so my friend could hear me. Even if I wasn’t too high up, it felt like quite a way down.

“Ok… But try not to fall” I hear Beatrice’s voice echo up the ladder.

“I’ll try not to,” I say, mostly to myself, as I begin to scan the titles of the different books. All I see is a range of dull coloured spines with different titles written on them. Some of the titles I can’t read because they’re almost entirely covered in dust. I attempt to sweep some of the dust away on the books, but after about 7 books, my hands become completely covered in dust; I decide to stop.

I check what I can see on the top shelf, although, another annoying thing was because we had placed the ladder on one side of the bookshelf, it was quite hard to check the books which were the furthest away from where we had placed the ladder, I did try to check as many books as I could though, however I couldn’t seem to find anything on this shelf.

=====

I didn’t need the ladder after the top two shelves, so I could actually check all of the books this time. Beatrice helped check half of the bookcase and I did the other half. She would occasionally ask if I had found anything, rather annoyingly, the answer always tended to be no.

I hadn’t found anything so far, the thoughts that this plan would actually work were slowly dying out, similar to a flame in a slow breeze. I wasn’t expecting much for the final few books, but then, I heard Beatrice say “I think I’ve found something!”

I looked over and found she was looking at some a book, which seemed to have a light purple cover, it certainly stood out from the rest of the dull coloured spines of the books surrounding it. My friend then shows me the front cover, it seemed like a peculiar cover. It featured a piece of paper, which was laid on a light wooden desk. I couldn’t exactly what was written on the piece of paper but there were some curly, intricate handwritten letter on the paper. To the side of the piece of paper where was a quill and an inkwell, although it is slightly cut off by the edge of the cover. There was no title on the front page, which was quite strange. Although, looking at the spine, there looked to be a title: "An Eye For History." Beatrice then turned the book over so we could see the blurb.

‘A true mystery of history’

How was it done? People ask, how did they achieve it?

Well, this book intends to find out, historians and scientists across the globe have done research on this one topic throughout countless years and all of the information that has been found out has been considered in this one book, we have summarised all the information that we know so far and it is because of this research, many questions have been narrowed to one, this question is…

Are those people really who they seem?

I look up and Beatrice asks “Do you think this book might have the information you need?”

“It sounds like it should,” I say “Maybe we should check the first few pages just to be sure”

Beatrice passes the book to me and says “You’ll probably have a better understanding of it than I will,” I begin to open the book and read through the first paragraph, as I do this she “Why are you even reading into this?”

“Because I thought it was quite interesting, and besides, it could be something to add to our history project,”

“What do you mean?”

“I suppose it informs us how people gained their unique personality traits” I say, thinking back to the notes I had made in my science book about how the different eye colours could lead to a different trait.

“I guess… And I guess it could be considered history as it did happen about quite some time ago,”

“Exactly what I was thinking” I agree.

=====

Even though the font in the book was quite small, it didn’t actually take that long, about 7 pages later, until we eventually find something. Beatrice and I were now sat around the table where I had left my notes.

I then point to the part I was reading, and told Beatrice to read through it.

Here is what it said

Looking back at what they did, it is truly amazing how they managed to achieve it, so let’s see what historians and scientists across the globe have managed to find out about how a group of mystical beings have managed to change many people’s personalities.

This sentence made me realise that this book may be hiding some vital information we, or at least I, needed. I tell my friend “I think this could be the book which might contain some information about the science lesson”

“That’s good isn’t it, but we got taught that in science, are you sure they will let us add that into the project, if we didn’t learn it in history?”

“I already told you that I would add it to the sheet! Anyway, it doesn’t matter if we can’t because I did it last lesson”

“Oh yeah, of course, I remember now. But… We won’t have time to read all of that,” she flicks all the way to the back page and points to the page number. It has an intricate font, but I can still read that it says 485.

“Hmm, to be honest, I hadn’t thought about how long it would take to read it all, but I have a feeling it might be possible,”

“485 pages in just less than three weeks, considering school work, is that possible?”

“I’m hoping it is. I’ll be the one who reads it, of course, and I’ll make notes on anything I find”

“Ok… But won’t you get bored of it fairly quickly; I remember reading a book that was over 600 pages once and when I was about 400 pages in, I grew bored of it”

“Yeah, I probably will” I say, boredom was something I hadn’t considered, “But if it gets us good grades, then I’ll have to read through it all” I then look over to the clock and check the time, there is still about twenty minutes left of lunch time, so I decide that I should get the book and then try and get some lunch.

I start off by telling my friend “I’m going to see if I can get this book…”

“Ok… But you probably will” Beatrice responds, and, as I begin to walk to the librarian’s desk, she adds, with a small laugh “It is a library after all.”

I stand there for a few seconds, but the librarian doesn’t look up from the book she is reading, so I place the book on the dark wooden surface, it lands on the desk with a thud. That makes the librarian look up, I quickly say “I would like to take this book, please”

The librarian then places her book aside and takes note of the book title and today’s date, she then asks me “What is your name?”

I respond with “Iris Hauxwell."

After she makes a note of this, she tells me “So you can have this book for a week, but after that you will have to return it”

“Ok, thanks” I reply before heading back to the table where Beatrice was sat. When I arrive, I tell her that I had managed to get the book, she replies with “Well… I suppose you already had the book when you first picked it up”

I mockingly tell her to “be quiet” and we leave the library. I then look down the corridor and think to myself that maybe I should put the book in my dormitory. So, I tell Beatrice of my plan and that I would go down to the main hall once I had done that.

The dormitories are relatively close to the library, so, in theory, this shouldn’t take that long. The annoying thing is that there would be teachers patrolling the corridors, since you weren’t really supposed to be walking in the corridors at lunch time. This inevitably meant that if I was caught, I would be in trouble, which was something I wanted to avoid.

It kind of felt like being on some kind of secret mission, even if the objective was to just arrive at my dormitory. However, after scanning the corridor at every moment, listening out for footsteps, I eventually made it. So I placed the book on the small, square wooden table by the window.

After that was done, I quietly walked back down the corridor to the main hall to see if I could get any lunch, although, that part of the plan depended on whether there was actually any lunch left at the canteen. As soon as I arrive, I am greeted to the loud noise of people talking over one another. I looked around and saw that my friends were all sat at the same table we had been sat at for breakfast, I sauntered over to the table.

“Speaking of which, Iris!” Beatrice says, as I arrive and sit down on the empty seat, “I was just telling Amber and Willow how we had potentially found some information which could help with our history project”

“Yes,” I say “We managed to find a book which might tell us more about what we learnt in science yesterday”

“If it helps with your history project, then I could do with some of those notes” Amber jokes.

“Well, first I’ve got to read it, and hope that there’s actually something there”

“I’m sure there will be from the small part of it we read” Beatrice joins in.

“If Beatrice is right, then that would be good… Because I was running out of things to write about using the books in the classroom” Willow explains.

We sit in silence for a few moments, before the smell of school dinners enters my nose, and I remember why I am in the main hall in the first place. I tell my friends that I am going to get something to eat. So, I walk over to the canteen and I see that there is nothing. Nothing left to eat. I decided to leave it for now, I could have asked to see if there was any food left, but I couldn’t really be bothered to at the moment, partially because no one was there, but, even if there was someone there, I imagined they would have tided it away by now, because when I checked the time, there was only ten minutes. The annoying thing was the smell of school dinners in the air made me realise how hungry I actually was, I hadn’t eaten anything since yesterday (pretty much). Maybe I should have eaten my porridge before it was thrown down my hair and blazer.

I slowly walk back to the table my where my friends are.

“I thought you were going to get something to eat” Beatrice says, as I arrive and sit down.

“Yeah… I thought I was as well… But they had ran out of food.”

“That’s happened to me a few times, it is quite annoying” Beatrice says, trying to empathise with me. She also didn’t have any dinner by the looks of it either, maybe she missed out on it too.

=====

In the end, it seemed as though lunch time was going to be uneventful, which I’m unsure whether that is good or not. Even when we walked onto the field after Amber and Willow had finished eating, I was expecting something, yet nothing happened. Although, as I looked around the field, I accidentally locked eye with one of the school bullies, I think it was Maisie, but I wasn’t sure, she alerted Evie to the fact that I had probably stared at them for too long, even if it was just about five seconds. Evie looked over to me and I froze. I felt every muscle in my body tense as I prepared to start running.

Then, unlike all the other times, Evie just shrugged and turned back to talk to the other two school bullies. Since I had stopped walking, I looked back and found my friends were further away than I had thought, so I began to catch up to them. Curiosity did get the better of me because I did look back to check whether or not the red – haired bully had decided to run after me, but it appeared not.

Whether or not that meant that something would happen later as a result of that, I would have to wait and see.
