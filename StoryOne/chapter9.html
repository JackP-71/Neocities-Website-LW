One Eye, One Girl

Currently reading: Chapter 9

Previous Happenings:

After the second visit to the library, Iris found a book which, from a quick skim over, seemed to contain more info about the contents of the science lesson. Although, as it was lunch time, she couldn't really read the book yet...

After seeing Evie, nothing much happened. I didn’t even see her for the rest of the time we were sat on the field.

We spent some time talking about the history project and other subjects also got a mention, but outside of that, it was a fairly boring conversation. In boarding school, you soon realise there’s very few things you can talk about. Before long, the ten minutes was up and the school bell rang, signalling for next lesson, which looked to be English Literature, which was probably one of the better subjects we have.

When we enter the main hall, I realise how quickly the school bell can change how busy it is. One minute it’s full of students and the next, it’s almost completely empty. The corridors seemed to be busier than usual, because everyone was going to their lessons. The crowd of people slowly moves along the corridor and I arrive at the English classroom; I walk into the classroom and see that there are already a few people here. The school bullies weren’t, which I wasn’t too surprised about, they would probably most likely be late, potentially to look ‘cool’

I head over to where I would normally sit in the classroom, not too near the front and not too near the back, I always tended to sit towards the middle of the classroom. I sat towards the end of the middle row of desks. As I sit down, I see that Beatrice and Willow have sat behind me at the back row of desks.

“Why are you sitting behind us?” I ask as I turn to face them. I wasn’t sat by myself on the middle row, because Amber had sat next to me.

“We always sit here…” Willow says, sounding slightly confused

“I thought we always sat on the second row though…” I say, now starting to feel confused.

“I don’t think we usually do…” Beatrice says, as our english teacher Mrs Halstead starts to walk into the classroom, I figure that there isn’t much point in moving seats now the teacher was here, I didn’t really want to risk getting told off.

The teacher reminds us that we will be reading a book which we need to know certain quotes from to pass our final exams. I already knew the book she was talking about, and I think she was going to tell us for the fifth time, but she was interrupted by the school bullies. They pushed the door open and stormed into the classroom, and then sauntered to the back of the classroom, where the each took a seat on the back row.

The teacher asked them why they were late, but the bullies, sat there in silence, when the teacher insisted that they give her, I hear Evie say “We were just late. Ok. What’s wrong with that?”

Mrs Halstead stared at them blankly before she shook her head and said “I’m not getting into an argument with you lot, so just be quiet”

The teacher then moves back to talking about what we are going to be doing this lesson “Ok… so… today, we are going to be reading this book” she then holds a copy of Alice’s adventures in Wonderland in front of us. Some people sigh, probably because she makes us read it aloud. I had always been quite interested in the peculiar world of wonderland. I mean, imagine if another world existed?

Mrs Halstead then reminds us of who would be reading out what part.

There were three people chosen to represent the characters in the book. These people were called to the front of the classroom. Evie had insisted that she should be Alice, which didn’t surprise me much, she probably thought of herself as the main character where ever she goes. Beatrice also walked to the front, the teacher had given her the role of the White Rabbit. I then saw one student stand up and walk to the front, I wasn’t sure of her name, but from what I had heard from other people in our year, I sat next to her in science, I think I remember her name being Sophie, she had gained the role of the mouse.

The teacher then gave three different copies out to the three people standing at the front of the classroom. After she had done this, the teacher then told the class “Ok… If we remember, Evie is going to be Alice, Beatrice is going to be the White Rabbit and Sophie is going to be the mouse and I shall be reading the descriptions in between the speech of the characters.” Everyone standing at the front of the classroom opened their books and they started to read…

The lesson seemed to be going well, no one had significantly messed up their part. I checked the clock which made me realise that I had lost all track of time, which I wasn’t really expecting to happen. Evie, or Alice, says “Curiouser and curiouser”

Just before the teacher says “Oh dear, look at the time, you shall all be terribly late to your next lesson” I was assuming that this was a reference to the book. Nevertheless, we all started to get ready to leave the classroom and head to the next lesson. Physical education.

Me and Willow are walking to our dormitory, I turn around and see Evie walking slightly faster thane me, she tells me “Move it, you freak” I pick up my speed slightly, Willow does the same. I then decide to slow down and that’s when she pushes past me. She continues to stride down the corridor as she says “If you make me late for physical education, you have made one big mistake” which I don’t really think meant anything, because I don’t think she cares if she’s late or not.

Although, I suppose I didn’t really want to have made that one big mistake she had just mentioned, so I was quite glad to have let her past. I wasn’t expecting to see the school bully behind me, instead I thought I would have seen one of my friends.

As we arrive at the dormitory, Willow goes to sit on her bed and I get out my sports kit from the wardrobe.

I take the T – shirt off the coat hanger and am about to get ready, when I hear a knock at the door. I rush over to the door and when I pull it open, I see Beatrice standing there, “Oh, Hello…” I say “I thought you would be in your dormitory…”

“No, I wanted to know what lesson we had next”

“It’s physical education now, I’m not too sure what we have next,” I say, attempting to remember what it was when I looked at my timetable, and that is when I remember what it was “and then it is Home Economics”

“Thanks” Beatrice says.

“I thought you would know it was physical education next though?”

“No… I think I lost my timetable somewhere…” Beatrice says, “It could be anywhere”

“Ok… See you later” I say.

“Alright, see you at the sports hall” My friend replies.

=====

It doesn’t take too long to change into my sports kit, a simple way to describe the Hazelwood sports kit is that it’s a similar colour as the blazer, light orange and it has the school’s name embroidered on the top corner of the T – shirt.

Before I leave, I look over to the wooden square table and see the book placed on it, the one I had gained from the library, I would have to read it at some point! I then also saw Willow lying on her bed reading another book, I couldn’t tell what it was because I couldn’t see the cover.

I tell Willow that I would be leaving and she said “See you in a bit,” Now, I’m not too sure as to why, but she usually skips physical education, the school let her get away with it. After physical education is over, sometimes I’ll see her in our dormitory, so maybe she just stays there for the full hour, it would probably be another Hazelwood mystery, but I think she does some extra revision during this time.

On my way to the sports hall, I passed the library and it made me wonder whether there would be another book written about what we were trying to find out. With so many books in the library, it could be a possibility.

I begin walking along the corridors and every time I head to the sports hall, I’m surprised at how quiet it can be sometimes. The corridors were lined with classrooms, so you could sometimes hear some of the teachers from outside the classroom, it was hard to tell what they were saying, but it was probably safe to assume they were teaching their subject.

When I arrive at the sports hall, I scan the surroundings. Looking around, I see all students stood on one of the lines marked on the floor. I walk to the end of the line and stand there, patiently waiting for the teacher to give some instructions for today’s lesson.

I take a step forward and look down the line, there are only around ten students in our year, so the line isn’t that long. I see Beatrice talking to someone further down the line, I think it may have been Amber, but it was quite hard to tell. As I move back to stand on the line, I hear the teacher’s voice echo around the hall.

“Ok, everyone be quiet please!” She waits for silence, which, surprisingly doesn’t take that long “Today we are going to playing football on the field, so everyone, please make your way to the field please!” I wasn’t the biggest fan of football, and from the collective sigh that happened shortly after, I could tell that the majority of people aren’t either. Even if I didn’t like physical education that much, I think I preferred cross country racing rather than anything else.

We made our way to the field through the school. Whilst we do this, even though the teacher told us to be quiet as lessons continued, I don’t think everyone got the memo, because almost all students seemed to be talking to one another. I had decided to stay silent, there wasn’t anyone to speak to at the minute, and besides, it was probably the easiest way to stay out of trouble. In fact, that’s might be why Willow gets into very little trouble; she doesn’t talk that much.

Eventually we arrive on the field and the teacher tells us to go over to the far-left corner of the field and go and sit on the benches that are located there. I started to traipse over to the other side of the field, I saw some people run over to where the benches were positioned. I didn’t run because I didn’t want to end up running into trouble.

After walking all the way to the opposite side of the field, I arrived at where the benches were. I think they are supposed to be a sort of light grey colour, but it is quite hard to tell with all of the dark green moss covering them. I looked up to find that to find that some of the trees were covering the benches from the sunlight, some students didn’t really seem to care that the benches were almost entirely covered in moss, as most of the students who had run to the benches had sat down and some were already out of breath. Since the majority of students were in the shaded part of the field already, I decided to just stay in the sunlight, even if it did feel as though I was being cooked in an oven.

It didn’t take long before the teacher walked over to where we were standing and she said “Everyone, I need you to get into groups of two people please” I was slightly worried upon hearing this, because I thought Beatrice was going to go with Amber.

A hand on my shoulder sent a shiver down my spine, I thought it may have been one of the school bullies, however, I turned to see Beatrice there, “Hello, again” she says.

“Oh… Hello” I say. Truth be told, I wasn’t in the best mood to do physical education, I already felt tired and bored.

The tiredness and boredom stayed with me as we were told to find a space on the field and kick the football back and forth to each other, Me and Beatrice decided to go quite far away, in the end, we decided to go nearer the other side of the field, away from everyone else, to avoid trouble.

“What’s up?” Beatrice asks, as she kicks the football to me, “You look miserable”

“Nothing much… I just don’t really want to do physical education... not like this anyway” I reply, as I kick it back.

“Yeah, I don’t want to either,” Beatrice says, trying to empathise with me, which doesn’t really work as I know physical education is one of Beatrice’s favourite lessons. It was mine too... but when we're doing this... it just isn't too fun...

=====

Me and Beatrice kick the football back and forth to each other, we both found it quite boring... especially as both of us were bigger fans of cross country, especially with us both being in the team. Although, I think it has to be said that it gets quite boring, especially when you’ve done it about twenty times. Eventually, the teacher calls us back to the benches, but because of the heat, unlike last time, pretty much everyone decides to walk over to the benches this time. When we have all arrived at the benches, the teacher tells us what we will be doing next.

“We will now start a simple game of football” This is then followed by the teacher counting how many of us there are “Ok, so there are eight of you, so we will need two teams of four people, so we need two team captains, does anyone want to be a team captain?” Not many hands go up for this. The teacher then picks a student I don’t know the name of, and one I definitely know the name of, because it was Evie.

The two team captains start choosing players for their team, it takes about a minute but eventually there are only three people left, Me, Beatrice and another student. The first student to get picked is the one I don’t really know the name of, then Beatrice is chosen to be on Evie’s team, Obviously, Evie pretends not to know her name, even though they have been in the same year for about five months, instead she says “Blonde Girl!”. Being the last one left, I am assigned to the other team. I go and stand behind the line of the other students. As I stand there, I think to myself, thankfully, I’m not on Evie’s team.

After we had been sorted into teams, we positioned ourselves on opposite sides of the field. Our team had been placed on the right side. For some reason, I was chosen to be the goalkeeper… We were definitely going to be at a disadvantage now, because, unlike everyone else here, I only had one eye to see which direction the football was coming from.

Thankfully, I didn’t really mind the role of goalkeeper, because there isn’t much to it. All I had to do was make sure the football never passed the threshold of the goal, which would be made easier if I had two eyes, but I still preferred it to actually playing the game.

=====

Nothing much happened during the match… Although, unlike all the other times, our team seemed to be winning. This never happened very often, because, football team I was a part of always seemed to lose.

It wasn’t too long before the football seemed to be rapidly approaching the goal. I stood where I was hopeful I would be able to defend the football from going past the goal post.

One of the people passed the football to Evie. The school bully stopped. I moved in front of her.

I saw Evie kick the football and it barrelling right towards my face.

The last I feel is my body fall to the floor. Everything goes dark and I’m surrounded by silence.

Inescapable silence.
