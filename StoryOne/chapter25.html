One Eye, One Girl

Currently reading: Chapter 25

Previous Happenings:

Iris managed to find a way to potentially remove the curse that has ridiculed Evie. The question is, can she and her friends actually carry it out..?

The next morning, I woke up, thanks to the school bell, as always. And as I woke up, the thoughts from the previous day entered my head. The main one I was thinking about yesterday was where would we actually do the ritual? This was actually quite an interesting question, although, I remembered that I had realised that we may be able to use Bunsen burners, so this would mean that we would need access to gas taps, to be able to turn the Bunsen burners on. And there was only really one type of classroom which had those. The science classroom!

Gathering the resources we needed would be slightly easier than I thought, as it was only a piece of chalk and some candles, or some Bunsen burners in this case. Although, the chalk may be slightly hard to find. Thankfully, it seems we can use anything to draw the circle with.

Perhaps this would all be fairly easy after all... I suppose all we had to do now was wait to see if Amber would do the ritual with us, or whether we would have to find someone else. We would also have to wait until it was night time, not only for the dark space part of it, but it also means that no one will be able to see what’s going on.

Maybe things were finally starting to look up for us! Finally…

=====

I was sat in the main hall eating my breakfast, when I see Beatrice heading down the stairs, she notices me and begins to walk over to the table I’m sat at.

“Bad news Iris…” She says.

“Oh no… What is it?” I ask

“Amber said she didn’t want to help out…” That was probably the one bit of news I didn’t really want to hear, but I suppose it was inevitable, because things has been going so well…

“Well… Who else is there to ask?” I think aloud, although something made me curious “Hold on a minute, what did you say to her?”

“I asked her whether or not she wanted to help out do some sort of ritual. That’s what it is, isn’t it?”

“Yeah… Did you say or ask anything else?”

“No… Why?”

“So you didn’t tell her that we would be helping out the school or any of that?”

“No… Because, think about it Iris, because I was thinking about it, it might not be all that you expect it to be. It might change something else, besides, maybe she was afraid of messing it up for us… I didn’t want to ask the reason why she didn’t want to do it because I didn’t want to seem confrontational” I don’t think she was expecting me to hear the part where she said “A bit like you are at the minute…” I decide not to say anything after that.

Surprisingly, the next person to walk over to the table isn’t Willow, but instead, it’s Phoebe.

“Hello!” She says.

“Hello to you too” I say, “I think we are almost ready to do the ritual!”

“That’s good, because I have a bad feeling that Evie’s illness is getting worse…”

“Although, there is one thing that still needs sorting out…”

=====

I wasn’t sure whether this would work, but Phoebe figured that it might have a chance of working, although she didn’t sound very confident when she said it. We eventually arrived at Maisie’s dormitory, which did take some finding because Phoebe wasn’t too sure which one it was, I wonder whether the spell did something to everyone’s memory? And Phoebe knocked on the door.

“Give me a minute!” I hear someone call from behind the door, I could tell it was Maisie because the voice sounded familiar.

Eventually, Maisie opens the door slightly and immediately says “Oh, it’s you…” I don’t think she looked at me, I think she was looking mainly at Phoebe when she said it, which was a bit strange, was there some kind of rivalry I wasn’t aware of?

“Yes it is… Anyway, I have something to ask you” Phoebe responds.

“Alright, it’d better be quick”

“It will… I need your help with something, do you want to or not?”

“I’m not answering until you tell me what it is”

“Alright, I, more specifically we, need your help with a ritual”

“That’s a bit weird, even for you” Maisie comments, then asks “Wait… what do you mean by we?”

Since the door was only open slightly, Phoebe pushes it open to reveal me standing there next to her

“Oh… That explains why it’s so weird”

“Would you stop saying I’m weird and give me an answer” Phoebe says, which isn’t surprising, if I was in her shoes, I would have said the same thing.

“Well… It was going to be a no anyway, but now you’ve described it all to me and bought her here” she says as she points to me “It’s even more of a no now”

“You could have just said that at the beginning, then” Phoebe quietly says.

“What was that?”

“Nothing…”

“That’s what I thought!” And with that, Maisie violently slams the door shut.

“What was that all about” I ask, as we begin to slowly walk back down the corridor.

“It’s a long story…” Phoebe cryptically replies

“Can I hear it?”

“I suppose so…” Phoebe then tells me that she and Maisie had been friends with Evie for a while, however only recently Maisie had become jealous of Phoebe, for a reason she didn’t know. If she had to guess, it would be that Phoebe feels as though she is better friends with Evie than Maisie is.

So, it seems there was a hidden rivalry, but if Evie and I have given up our rivalry, why hadn’t Phoebe and Maisie? Perhaps this was something worth investigating…

=====

After the fairly horrible encounter with Maisie, I was wondering how further downhill things could escalate. Currently, I was sat in a rather boring geography lesson, one of the lessons I had had today was history, continuing on with the project had been rather… interesting. Having first hand experience with changing history has made the project surprisingly easier.

Anyway, back to the boring geography lesson, which was boring when we were doing the learning of the geological aspects of the planet, we were learning about coasts at the minute, but I didn’t really see the point, seeing as we weren’t near any coasts. It was much better at the end of the lesson, and not only because it was the end of the lesson, because Amber walked over to my desk and said “Iris, I’m so sorry…”

“What? Why?”

“Well… I didn’t realise what was going on, I didn’t realise that we were doing this to save the school.”

“So you’re going to help after all?”

“Well… I’m still not too sure about it, but I’ll try to help out, just tell me what needs to be done”

So maybe things were, once again, beginning to look up.

=====

After having a few lessons, which I may as well have not attended… I wasn’t really paying attention… We had Physical Education, now, it was supposed to be the cross-country race today… but on the way to the lesson, there didn’t seem to be any coaches parked outside. Miss Crossley, our physical education teacher said that the race had been cancelled as a result of Evie’s illness, whatever it was, for we still had no idea what it actually was.

This would explain why we hadn’t began practicing for it yet… Evie was quote unquote dead which basically meant we were a team member down, which wouldn’t have been good, or fair now that I think about it. So, instead of practicing for the cross country race, we instead did some running around the field, which I guess could be considered practice, but it wasn’t labelled as such by the teacher.

I talked to Beatrice about it, the only other person out on the field who was in the cross country team at the minute and she seemed quite annoyed about it all, she must have been practicing a little bit even outside of lessons, which I did a bit of, but not as much as Beatrice must have been. Because of this, I began to wonder whether we should actually attempt to do the ritual tonight or not…

After the physical education lesson, other lessons resumed as normal… if we ignore the fact that Evie wasn’t there. It just didn’t seem right… even if lessons were a bit more peaceful without her. And after all those lessons, it was dinner time and the headmaster informed the school that the cross-country race wasn’t taking place, but he didn’t tell us why, however thankfully I was one of the few people who knew why this was.

So, tonight may have to be the night. The night we complete the ritual.
