One Eye, One Girl

Currently reading: Chapter 14

the more she finds out about the mystery, the stranger it seems to get for Iris... although, what if there was a way to see where it took place in person..?

...and what if they could potentially remove the spell?

Another week at Hazelwood School meant another week to attempt to fill any gaps my research had. I at least tried to answer some of the questions… but it wasn’t much use; I didn’t really get anywhere with that. Maybe some answers are never meant to be found. However, some, such as why would they have changed everyone’s personality traits? And why was Amber shocked at one of the piece of information I had written down? Maybe possible to answer, but didn’t seem all too likely. Maybe I would be able to ask Amber about it, but I think it would be strange to bring that up in conversation, so that would have to wait for another time.

Despite attempting not to think about it, the plan to change everyone’s personality traits had remained in my head, nearly all week. Today was a Thursday and, despite the lack of research, I wanted to try to get it done before the end of the week.

However, this meant that it would be the weekend soon, and it seemed like the better time to do it.

I had kind of already told them about this after the geography lesson where I had originally thought of it, but I was thinking of telling them by the end of the day anyway, even if I had the feeling that they would not want to help with my small quest.

=====

It was towards the end of the day, and despite wanting to do the plan today, I decided to go against this. So at dinner time, I decided not to tell my friends and instead focus on more research. So it was upstairs to my dormitory again to continue reading.

However, upon reaching my dormitory, I couldn’t help but think about it. I couldn’t read the book when this was all I could think about. So it was back down the stairs to get the thoughts out of my head.

When I headed down the stairs for dinner time, I had decided to talk to my two friends about my plan to try and head out of the school to try and fix everyone’s personality traits.

When I arrive at the main hall, I look around and find the table where Beatrice and Willow were sat, Amber must have sat elsewhere, which was good because I don’t think she’d understand what was going on.

Nearly as soon as I sit down, Beatrice tells me “By the look on your face, I can tell you’ve got something to tell us,” well, there was no point in hiding it now.

“You’re right… I do have something to say” I tell her. I think about it… Do I want to tell them? I mean, if I was to do it, it would be better to go as a group of people rather than alone.

“If you’re going to tell us something, then tell us,” Beatrice says “We’re listening.”

“Alright…” I say, just get on with it! “Ok… so, I kind of want to try and see if it is possible to try and see if the mystery of history I am researching at the minute can be solved.”

“You’ve already told us this…” Willow says “Why are you dragging us into it? Can’t you just solve it by yourself?”

“Exactly…” Beatrice joins in “Surely you’ve done enough research, it’s almost every time you’re just in your dormitory reading that book.”

“Well… I have kind of finished researching it, but I don’t think it would be safe to venture out into the forest, which is where they did the spell, alone.”

“So… Let me get this straight…” Beatrice says, trying to make sense of the situation at hand “You want us to try to leave the school… and then head out into some forest place to try and reverse some kind of spell… Is that correct?”

“Yeah… it is” I say “Although, I think out of everything, leaving school unnoticed is probably the hardest part of the plan.”

“Well… I can’t say I’m too sure about this…” Beatrice tells me “Although I suppose I’ll put it to you this way, we’ve been stuck here for about 5 months… and it’s not long before the cross country race, which is here, so that’s pointless. So, that means that it is going to be, at least, another month before we are allowed to leave. I suppose I could do with a change of scenery.”

So, it wasn’t a no, but then again, it wasn’t a yes either.

“I’m not sure about this…” Willow mutters.

“Well, surely there has to be a way, right?” I ask my friends.

Most of that dinner time was spent theorising over how we could possibly leave Hazelwood School. Overall, a much better result than I was expecting, Beatrice was somewhat convinced, the only problem was that Willow wasn’t.

=====

I hadn’t told my friends when we would try this. I kind of wanted to do it at the end of the week. So that gave me some more time to research it. Maybe the weekend might be a good time to try it. Even if she mumbled it, Beatrice mentioned the cross country race which was in the following weeks, I felt as though we should aim to do it before then. So I would have to tell my friends about that.

In fact, the extra time would be good as it would give me more time to find out anything I might need to know before we actually went to do it.

=====

It was Friday, so that meant the time had come to put the long awaited plan into action. A few more notes had been made now and a few loose ends have been tied up, annoyingly not all questions could be answered.

I had told my friends this morning that I wanted to try and do the plan tonight, and their replies when I told them wasn’t exactly what I was hoping for.

“Really?” Beatrice had said, I had a feeling she was expecting me to tell her in advance, and I probably should’ve in hindsight. Willow didn’t reply, which may have been worse depending on howe you looked at it.

It was hard to tell if my friends wanted to join me on my mission before, but now it seemed they didn’t want to.

Then, Beatrice said “What have we got to lose, we may as well get it over and done with,” then she jokingly adds “Even if it’s just to shut you up.” I think she mumbles something else, but it’s hard to tell. I think it was something about her brother, which she seemed to be talking about a bit more about now that I had begun talking about all this mystery.

=====

Yesterday we had come up with the plan to meet in the library during lights out and then we would continue the plan from there, which is basically to say we didn’t actually think of an escape plan yesterday, that was the only thing. However, we would have to be careful when we did this because one wrong move would mean we would be spotted by one of the teachers, if they’re still up, I guess add that to the list of Hazelwood mysteries.

So, when the end of the day arrived, I entered my dormitory after dinner and decided to pack some things that may be useful. I wasn’t actually the one to think of this, Beatrice was and she was probably right to do so. Although I was stuck wondering what to bring, because what were you supposed to bring on an adventure as strange as ours.

I look around the dormitory and firstly find an old backpack I used to use, so I take it from the cupboard, and then look around the dormitory, and eventually settle on taking three empty water bottles, which I would be able to fill up, and partially because I wanted to get rid of them, a pen, some paper and after some deliberation, I decide to take the book with me in case, although if I lost it, I would probably get into quite some trouble because it was the school’s book.

After looking around for a while, I decide that nothing else was worth taking, because we would hopefully return back to school after this small quest anyway.

Not too long after I decide to just sit down on my bed and await lights out, Willow traipsed into the dormitory and notices my bag next to me and quietly mumbles “Oh… I forgot about this”

I mean, I already told her, so I decide that I should probably try to convince her now, because if I left it, it would probably be too late. I simply tell her that I had already told her. And she responds with “Yeah… I know… you’ve already told me this… but I don’t get why you want to try and get us in trouble as well as yourself.”

“I mean, you’ve got a point,” I say, whilst thinking to myself this might take longer than I initially thought. “But… I think It would be better not to go alone, and besides, Beatrice is already going.”

“I understand that, but the rest of it… it doesn’t make sense to me”

“To be honest… It doesn’t really make sense to me either, although I’m sure that this mystery has been researched for quite some time.”

A few minutes later, I felt as though I wasn’t going to get anywhere, so in the end I just tell her, “Me and Beatrice are going, so if you just want to stay here, then that’s fine.”

Willow must have taken this as some kind of foreshadowing that we were about to step into potential danger, because she says “I guess I’ll go, but I’m only going so then you two don’t step into any danger.”

“Do, you’re going to help us?” I double check.

“Yes… I suppose I am” Willow reluctantly says.

I then advise her to take a bag of some stuff that might be needed, but she declines as she says that Beatrice and I will probably have everything that is needed.

Now that I had managed to get both my friends approval on my plan, all we had to do now was wait until lights out, which I didn’t think would take that long. Checking the time, however, showed that the second hand seemed to be moving slower than usual.

======

Eventually, the arrow on the clock moved to 9 PM and that meant it was lights out. I took a quick glance out of the window and saw the dark sky with the scattering of stars, each with a small glow, and then there was the moon, which was overpowering the flow of the many stars in the sky. There was a full moon tonight, I remembered something about the full moon being haunted, but now wasn’t the time to be thinking about fairy tales or myths.

Looking down, I saw the street lamps cast a dark orange glow onto the entrance to the school. If all went to plan, we would be heading up that path shortly.

We now needed to head to the library without being caught. I tried to listen out for footsteps, but couldn’t hear any. I decided to give it a few minutes, just in case one of the teachers was still lurking around the corridors.

When about seven minutes had passed, I had to conclude that the teacher must have gone from the corridors, so I pick up the backpack I had packed earlier and whispered to Willow that we should head to the library now.

She replied with “Wait for me… I don’t want to wander around the school alone.” Which, now that I think about it, would be much better going as a team, I certainly didn’t want to face the inevitable trouble we would get in should we be caught by one of the teachers alone. Although, if we were all caught on the school corridors at this time, we would all be in a considerable amount of trouble.

I slowly opened the dormitory door and looked, as best as I could with one eye (and with it being dark as well, it didn’t make things any easier), left and right before silently heading out onto the corridor, and as soon as Willow had shut the door behind her, we I started to walk along the corridor as quietly as we could.

In a stroke of good luck, it wasn’t a long walk to the library, along the corridor, past the dormitory and the library eventually comes into view. When I try to push the door open, it doesn’t open.

Was it locked? Because if it was, that was that part of the plan ruined.

Willow must have heard the door clatter because she attempts to open the door, and thankfully it opens.

“How did that…” I begin, but since we were supposed to be quiet, I leave it “Never mind…”

When we step into the library, I couldn’t see anything. No lights meant it was very dark, giving the school a different, almost scary atmosphere, it didn’t scare me though. The windows in here showed the same sight as the dormitory, but a different perspective. Due to the orange light outside, I could just about see the shadows of the bookcases, so at least I was able to know where they were because otherwise I would have ended up walked into one of bookcases and made some of the books fall out of it. Even though I can’t really see anything, I look over to the desk and see no silhouetted figure sat there, so I guessed no one was here, apart from us. Which was all part of the plan.

Me and Willow walk past some of the bookcases before arriving at one of the tables, we take a seat and await the arrival of Beatrice.

Whilst we were waiting, I remembered about the geography section and was about to begin searching for the book I had found which mapped out the forest we were about to head to. Due to the darkness of the library, I decide to leave it for now, although it would be helpful, maybe I should have bought a torch or something.

=====

We wait a few moments longer, before I heard the door shut and then heard someone whisper “Hello…” I got up from my seat and walked over to the door to reveal the silhouette of Beatrice, at least it wasn’t one of the teachers.

“We’re over here” I whisper back, which probably wasn’t the most helpful thing to say in the dark. A bright light is then suddenly cast. Beatrice must have bought a torch, which was good because it now meant I would be able to find the book I was looking for. Beatrice then joins us at the table and takes a seat.

“I wasn’t sure what to bring, if I’m being honest.” Beatrice says, then she tells us what she has bought, turns out the list wasn’t too dissimilar to mine, because she had bought, a pen and notebook and then also the torch, obviously, and when she showed us the bag, there looked to be some medical supplies in there (bandages and a few other things…), which would be good if any of us got hurt on the way there, I didn’t even consider that.

“Thank goodness you bought a torch, because there is no hope for finding a book in pitch dark,” Beatrice then passes me the torch and I walk over to the geography section to find the map.

It doesn’t take long to find the same book I had seen earlier in the week ‘The Local Area Examined: Hazelwood’ was what it was called.

I take the book from the bookshelf and head back to the table. I then place the book on the table and give the torch back to Beatrice. She holds it above the book as I search through the pages and eventually find the map.

Navigating past all the geographic features, into the shaded part of the map, signalling the forest which I was sure was referenced in the book I was reading. The map was zoomed in quite bit, so I could see a solitary grey rectangle, showing a solitary building, which must have been the stone building. There didn’t seem to be a path leading to it though.

“I think that’s where we need to go” I point to where the grey rectangle is. I then move my finger over to the road network by the shaded area with a few buildings surrounding the cul – de - sac, “This must be the nearby village: Hazelford… I think I remember reading about that…”

Beatrice then points to a larger darker grey rectangle on the map and says “Well… If that’s Hazelwood, it shouldn’t be too far…” Looking at maps in geography, there was a scale so you were able to see how far the map had been zoomed in, unfortunately, this map lacked that. But it couldn’t be too far if Hazelwood School was that close, right?

“There doesn’t look to be anyway of seeing how far its zoomed in,” Willow realises, she must have noticed too, “however, If you look at the size of the school, it could mean the map is zoomed in…. but we just don’t know how much it is zoomed in by.”

We spend a few minutes predicting how long we all thought it would take to get to the solitary, stone building I had read about. I thought it would take about 10 or 20 minutes, Beatrice thought 5 to 10 and Willow thought it might take half an hour. I was kind of hoping it wouldn’t take 30 minutes or even 20 minutes. In fact, I was secretly hoping that it would take just 5 minutes, but that was most unlikely.

We then agreed that just exiting out through the entrance wasn’t going to work. So, we would have to think of something else. Beatrice had said there was a door which led into the gardens, which seemed like the best bet. It was quite a good plan, however, we knew that the gate leading out of the school would certainly be locked and that we would need a key to open it.

Willow thought that the school gardener would have the keys, but if they were the only keys in the school how were we supposed to take the keys and then leave without being noticed? Supposedly, according to Beatrice, the keys would be kept in the cleaning cupboard, which was downstairs and near to our history classroom (How she knew that, I wasn’t sure).

I was sure that this cupboard would be locked, especially since it was lights out. Although, since it was our only hope, we would have to try it.

“So… is that the plan?” Willow asks “Try to get the key? And then try to escape?”

“Well… All the plan hinges on whether or not we can get into that cleaning cupboard, so I guess we should head there” After I say this, Beatrice turns off her torch and we are plunged into darkness.

Time to begin.
