One Eye, One Girl

Currently reading: Chapter 10

Previous Happenings:

after a frantic PE lesson, Iris ultimately collapsed after being hit in the head by a football kicked by Evie.

My name is called. That’s the first thing I hear. “Iris?” I can’t place a name to the voice. All I hear is my name.

I must have managed to break free from the silence, “Iris?”

My eye still feels heavy, but it reluctantly opens. I wake up from a bed, which I am guessing is mine, my friends are by my side. I turn to see the school nurse standing there, Willow quietly asks, “are you ok, Iris?”

I manage to stutter a reply, “Y-Yes… A-At least I think so…”

“That’s good to hear” I hear the nurse say, although I did have to ask myself was I seriously injured, or just knocked out? I sat up from where I was lying down and I look down at my blood – covered sports kit. I must have had a nose bleed, that’s the second one this week! after Evie hit me with the football. I decided to get out of the bed, but all that did was make me feel dizzy and slightly sick.

I heard the nurse say “You might want to try and not move for a few minutes because you were hit in the head quite hard, from what I’ve been told.” I knew that… But the nurse was probably right, so I just remained lying down on the bed.

I saw the school nurse head towards the door of the dormitory I was in, as she does so, she says “I think you’ll be fine for now, but if you start to feel any pain then you know where to find me,”

Beatrice is the one to reply with “Ok,” the school nurse then leaves and my friend then asks me whether or not I was alright “I saw everything, in fact, you were lucky I was there… because no one else, seemed to help”

“Don’t worry about me… I’m fine… I could have guessed no one else would have helped” I say, “Anyway, what happened?”

“Well… After you got hit with the football, you fell to the ground; I presume you remember that?”

“Yes, It’s the one of the last things I remember” I reply, realising I was probably lucky not to have caught a case of amnesia from it.

Beatrice continues with her answer to my question “Anyway, after you fell to the ground, the teacher blew her whistle and everyone stopped playing football, the teacher ran over and so did me and Amber, we checked to see whether you were still breathing which thankfully you were, then the teacher asked me to go and get the school nurse, which I did and then the school nurse brought you back to your dormitory, which is where you are now. She then tried to stop the nose bleed, which you had when Evie managed to hit your face with the football”

“I’m guessing she did it on purpose…” I say.

“Yeah… I’m guessing she probably did” Beatrice says “You two seem to have some sort of rivalry going on at the moment”

“Yes… I know…” I say wearily “I kind of want it to end, but at the same time, I kind of want to see how far Evie is willing to go with this…”

“I’d say further than she already has because she has already tried to fight you once and you have had porridge thrown down your school uniform this morning and now she has managed to hit you in the face with a football, and that is just this week!” Beatrice says, when she had said it aloud, it made me realise how much Evie hated me and the rivalry we had. I wanted it to end, but I couldn’t help but wonder, how long it would take? I just didn’t know.

=====

It took a while, but eventually I was able to stand without the dizzying feeling overwhelming me. When I stand up, I look over to the clock and see that there is fifty minutes left of free time, which had meant I had been in a deep sleep for almost two hours. Willow finds a pack of playing cards and she and Beatrice begin playing a card game, the name of which I’m unsure. Even though I’m asked if I want to join in, I decide that I should try to make a start on reading the book from the library, so I decline. I stumbled over to the square wooden table, where I had left it last. I pick it up and then sit by the side of my bed. Opening up the first page, I begin reading.

It doesn’t take long before I find the beginning of something, because when I turn to page 3, I am presented with the heading: The brains behind the strange happening

I read on for a bit before I come to the realisation that I should probably attempt to make some notes (to make reading all 485 pages worthwhile), so I stand back up, steadying myself against the bed so the dizzying feeling doesn’t return, and wander over to the wardrobe. I find my blazer and find the spare piece of paper which I had written on previously as a guide for the bookcases to check. I find a pen and then the reading commences.

=====

After about forty minutes, I had managed to read thirty five pages (there was still a considerable amount of the book remaining). Even though I had read quite a few pages, I had only managed to make three notes, I was kind of hoping the have made more, but I guessed all the other information I would be looking for would be scattered throughout the rest of the book.

Upon realising that I had stopped reading, Beatrice asks “Should we head down to the main hall now?”

I look down at my blood – covered sports kit before saying “not yet” and tell my friends that I’ll meet them down there once I had changed back into my school uniform.

Once I put on my school uniform, I hold my sports T – shirt up and see that it is now quite worse for wear. I’ll definitely have to get it cleaned because I don’t think the teacher would want to see me in a sports kit with blood scattered on it.

When I open the dormitory door, I notice Beatrice waiting by the wall, “I thought you were going to the main hall?”

“Well… that was the plan originally, but I decided to wait,” she then adds “because of what happened earlier, after all, the nurse said to me and Willow that we should keep an eye on you”

“I’m guessing Willow didn’t take that advice” I comment.

“To be fair, she got bored of waiting and the bell did go” If it had, I mustn’t have heard it.

We began to walk down the corridor, I could feel the dizzying feeling begin to return, although I did slow my pace and that seemed to stop it for now. I continued the slow pace down the stairs, although things soon took a turn for the worse as we neared the entrance to the main hall. This was because, when Beatrice opened the door, I had expected to hear the usual loud voices of all the students talking one another, instead I heard a loud constant ringing in my ears, it felt as though the volume of everything had just been turned up to be maximum and everything had turned into a high-pitched squeal. I guess my ears hadn’t heard this amount of noise in a while, especially after listening to silence for the entire time I was knocked out.

I put my hands over my ears in an attempt to try and stop the loud noise. It still persisted.

Since I had stopped in my tracks, Beatrice asks “Are you alright?” with a sense of alarm in her voice.

“No!” I hear myself shout, attempting to get rid of the hideous squeal which was still happening.

“What’s the matter?”

“I can barely hear myself think” I say but slightly quieter

Then, the loud noise fades out and is replaced by the sound of various conversations taking place.

It has stopped.

Finally, the loud noise stopped.

I remove my hands from my ears, meaning I can hear the voices much clearer now. It felt so great not to have an ear destroying high pitch ringing constantly in my ears.

When me and Beatrice arrive at the canteen, I can see that the cooks were packing away all the food that was out . I was surprised to see that they were putting all the food away so early, especially considering there was about twenty minutes left of dinner.

I decide to ask one of the dinner staff, “do you have any food left?” I can tell that the cook looks uncertain as she looks around and then spies a tray which looks to be filled with some sort of food, which in all honesty, didn’t look much like food.

“All we have is this” the cook says, pointing to the not so good – looking food, it looked to be some kind of mush, it looked to contain a few different vegetables and it had a light grey colour to it.

I wasn’t tempted, but I wanted to see how badly it tasted, so I say “I’ll have some of that please,” and then in a quieter voice, as the cook scooped some of the grey mush out of the tray, I said “Whatever it is…”

I was handed a bowl of the mixture of vegetables and then I look around the main hall and I see Amber and Willow sat at a table, I walk over to the table and take a seat, as I sit down, I notice that Amber and Willow didn’t appear to have a bowl of revolting grey mush sat in front of them. Beatrice soon sat down at the table and said “My word… this looks absolutely awful, and it probably tastes even worse”

“I know…” I reply, adding that I only got some so I could see how badly it tasted. I take a spoon and attempt to scoop up some of the vegetables hidden within the light grey mixture, I eventually manage to scoop up what I think is a carrot, but because this mush was so poorly prepared, it slid off the spoon, so I tried again and eventually managed to pick up another carrot, I then ate the vegetables and… I don’t think I have had anything so bad before, I have no idea what it even was, but it felt like the cooks had just managed to mush all of the leftovers of previous dishes into this one food, I warn Beatrice “I wouldn’t try it if I was you, it tastes appalling”

“I’ll take your word for it” Beatrice says, as she places the spoon back into the bowl and pushes the bowl away from the edge of the table. I was rather annoyed because I had barely had anything to eat all day and all there was right now was some terrible light grey mush.

After what happened during physical education, I felt like I needed to give my friends an apology, so I decide to say “Beatrice, Willow, and I guess Amber too, I have something to tell you all” I don’t really tend to call my friends by their first names very often “I… I’m sorry…” I stutter and I don’t really mean to.

“What… Why?” Beatrice asks, I wasn’t too sure, but I guess after everything that happened in physical education, and I may have also made them quite a bit worried.

“I’m not too sure, but I guess I must have made you slightly worried after last lesson…”

“Slightly worried?” Beatrice exclaims “I think we were all much more worried than just slightly worried… I don’t know about the others, but I thought you were dead at one point!”

“Ok… So maybe nearly completely worried” I correct “I guess… If I hadn’t been knocked out by a football…” I then add, in an attempt to string some kind of apology together “then I probably would have managed to read more of the book which we had got from the library earlier today”

Beatrice looks at me before saying “Don’t worry about it, what has happened in the past can’t change now… It’s happened and there is nothing you can do about it… I mean we do still have nineteen days left until the project is due in, so none of us should really be in a rush to get it done”

“We should though…” I hear Willow’s voice whisper.

“Well… We don’t have to…” Beatrice says confidently, “At least, not yet, anyway”

Ultimately, Me and Beatrice dump the disgusting food in the bin, and we then decide to head upstairs to the dormitories after a long day. I’m hoping to do some more of the research I could have done earlier, but I guess Beatrice was right, what has happened in the past can’t change now.

=====

Arriving at the dormitory, Willow walks in soon after I do and sees me holding the book and says “You’re going to be reading some more of that book, aren’t you?”

“Yeah… might as well,” I say “I’m already thirty five pages through it already, so may as well carry on”

“Have you managed to find any info in the book?”

“I found about three points before, but I was hoping to find a few more notes”

“Hopefully, you manage to find some more…” Willow says, and then she adds something which I hadn’t actually considered yet, “although, did it actually happen? Think about it, it doesn’t really sound like it would have been something which would have happened in the real world”

I ponder upon this for some time, I suppose I hadn’t considered it because, since we had been taught it, I assumed it had happened, but what if it was some kind of hypothesis or theory which never actually was put in place.

In the end, I respond with “I kind of get what you mean,” I then try to explain, “but I have just been reading about it, and it appears that it had quite a big potential to have taken place” kind of hoping that it happened, but I guess Willow was right, it just didn’t seem like it would be something that would happen in real life. “I guess I’ll carry on reading the book, but you do have a point.”

When I sit by the side of my bed I pull the piece of spare paper out of my blazer pocket, I look through the notes I had written down so far:

The first thing I had written on the piece of paper was all the different categories of books there were in the library, then I had written all of the different things which I had discovered in the previous science lesson:

  • Blue eyes mean that you lead a life of sorrow
  • Green eyes mean that you are jealous and sometimes greedy
  • Brown eyes mean that you are a kind and generous person
  • Different coloured eyes mean that you have good luck and the different eye colours traits may clash and you may end up with two personalities
  • If you only have one eye then it is seen as a sign of extreme bad luck
  • A group of sorcerers and sorceresses had joined together in the late 1800s and created some sort of spell to find out what eye colour everyone had
  • They then created some sort of spell to give everyone different personality traits
  • The group of sorcerers and sorceresses were either formed in a village called Hazelford, or a coastal town called Rose Cove

 

I then looked at the things which I had written after I had read the first thirty five pages of the book:

  • In the book it says that it is widely believed that the sorcerers and sorceresses were formed Hazelford, so this could mean that they did their spell in Hazelford, rather than Rose Cove
  • They had to complete two spells to change everyone’s personality traits, one to find out everyone’s eye colour and then one to change everyone’s personality traits according to what eye colour they had.
  • In the book it says that researchers have found that they have lived in a slightly small stone building, this is slightly confusing because as far as I am aware, there are no stone buildings in Hazelford, so my only guess is that they must have lived somewhere in the forest which is located next to Hazelford.

 

After what Willow had told me about it potentially being some kind of fictional event, I decided I should add that to the piece of paper, so I do. Then I might remember to attempt to verify whether or not this actually happened.

But first, I decide I should probably start to do some research on this peculiar event, so I open the book and start to read it.

=====

I think I must have lost track of the time because it isn’t long before I hear a knock on the door and I hear the voice of a teacher saying “Turn your lights out” I leap up from where I am sitting and place the spare piece of paper into the book to act as a bookmark, I see that I have managed to now read seventy two pages, which isn’t too bad, but I was hoping to have made slightly more progress. I then change into some spare clothes that I found in my wardrobe and turn the lights off before lying on my bed. I had checked the time before I had turned out the lights and saw it was ten minutes past nine o’ clock.

I was trying to get to sleep, but I just couldn’t, no matter how hard I tried, I just couldn’t get to sleep. I had an urge to just get out of bed and continue to read some more of the book because the information we needed wasn’t going to find itself.

=====

I don’t know how long it took, but I eventually fell asleep and found myself having a rather strange dream.

If I remember correctly, the dream went something like this:

It was a dark night in a forest, didn’t seem to be any old forest though, because I was able to see Hazelwood’s shadow in the distance. Now that I think about it, it must have been the one I was thinking of when the stone building was bought up in the book I was reading. Part of the book I had read before this mentioned how the group of sorcerers and sorceresses had conducted their spell in a small stone building.

I walked through the forest and soon the path seemed to disappear, it was replaced with some tree roots. I then observed how some of the trees were craned over the path, as if to say ‘trust me, you don’t want to continue’ however I still persevered onwards.

It didn’t take long before I came across the silhouette of a building now. I could tell from the minute glow of a nearby ornate lamp that it was made of stone. I picked up the lamp and stumble towards the door, I pull on the door handle and, despite there being a key hole, the door creaks open. I stepped into the building and observed what the lantern lit up. There looked to be two rooms and this first room appeared to have a single wooden table in the middle of it. Sort of like the one in my dormitory. The wooden table didn’t seem to possess much, just a few pieces of paper which I didn’t bother reading. Because, on top of these papers, there was a key which I took and placed in the key hole of the door leading to the second room.

Looking down to unlock the door revealed blood stains along the walls.

When I entered the room, I saw two beds, and shadows of two people lying in each bed. I wasn’t sure who these people were. Maybe they were the people who did the spell? I walked closer to see if I could actually recognise any of them. At first I couldn’t but then they appeared to change.

Changed to appear as my two friends. Which was weird… to say the least.

However, the blood stains on the walls of the first room were unexplained. Until I took a step back.

And saw I sight I didn’t want to see.

Walking back revealed blood covered daggers on the beds. I dare not look further into it. Even if the faces had changed back to what they were before. Of the unknown people. I still envisioned my friend’s faces being there.

That’s the last thing I remember seeing before I wake up…

After this dream, which I suppose could be more of a nightmare than I dream. I suddenly awoke and sat bolt upright. I was slightly shook at the horror of my dream that I began to give a second thought on whether or not researching this was actually a good idea. Perhaps it wasn’t really the best idea if it was going to haunt me.

Since it’s so dark, I can’t see the clock, so I have no idea what time it is. I look out the window and see the faint orange glow of the street lamps does keep the surroundings away from the darkness, I can see the trees which are shaded from the light, I don’t see anyone outside though, it must be really late into the night, or really early into the morning.
