Bio:

Iris used to be defined by the fact that she only had one eye.

She was born with it and it caused conflict with every school she went to. Her current school Hazelwood was no different, with Evie being the main bully. Hazelwood was different in one aspect though, she had managed to make two friends there, Beatrice and Willow.

This was the third school she had been to now and she was getting really bored of all of this bullying nonsense. So upon discovering that there was potentially a way to stop the bullying, she decided to try it.

To put a long story short, it initially created more problems than it solved, but eventually everything was resolved and she had a second eye... for a few days before it was eventually taken away from her when resolving the problem the small venture to fix the bullying caused.

Despite this, even with her one eye remaining, there doesn't appear to be much bullying anymore... Maybe Evie gained a newfound respect for the venture Iris and her friends went on to make her better? Who knows...
