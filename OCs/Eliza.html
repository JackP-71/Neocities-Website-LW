Bio:

Eliza is the eldest of the triplets.

with her being the eldest, you would expect her to be a 'role model' to her two sisters... but she doesn't take that role too seriously. Although, Ebony still looks up to her regardless.

she's a lot more social than her two sisters, especially Ellie, in fact, she normally has to talk for Ellie in some cases. Eliza is probably the most talkative out of the her sisters. Out of her two sisters, Ebony seems to love listening to her tales, Maybe it's because Eliza is quite imagintive, so she can basically think of any kinda tale on the spot. Despite this though, she never seems to write down her tales, instead she prefers to draw them.

she was a little tempted to start a diary when both Ebony and Ellie started writing theirs, but she only wrote one entry and gave up. she was determined to try again after Ebony's disappearance, but ultimately never got round to writing anymore entries in the diary she had.
