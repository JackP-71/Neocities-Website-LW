Bio:

so... i'll just say this bio is a disaster, i wrote this like 3 years ago... maybe one day i'll improve on it...

CC is a fox turned human. Based on her accounts, she was found by some good-for-nothing scientists and a few tests were performed on her before she was ultimately held in captivity.

At the time, she did not know they were scientists, having never met any, but it seemed to be right. Attempts were made to escape the invisible cage she found herself in, but at the time, escape was impossible.

Upon feeling really tired, she fell asleep, but upon waking up, things were strange... Something was up.

She was no longer a fox... but instead possessed human features. And, after some further tests, she was allowed to leave the science lab.

CC eventually escaped the science lab... She hopes she'll find the forest she was taken from, and able to see her parents again...
