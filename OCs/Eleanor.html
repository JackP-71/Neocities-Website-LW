Bio:

Eleanor, or Ellie as she is sometimes called by her sisters, is the second youngest of the triplets. I guess for the purposes of this bio, we'll call her Ellie.

Ellie is typically much more melancholic when compared to her sisters. She's a lot more focussed on her schoolwork... Hell, even when Ebony went missing, she was still contemplating whether she should spend her time studying for an end of year exam.

She, like Ebony, keeps a diary, which she doesn't tend to update very often, but when she does, it's normally talking about school work and also tends to talk about two students in her class... maybe it's some kind of competition Ellie thinks is going on between them, supposedly talking about who's getting higher grades by the end of the school year.

Despite all her revision, she seems to be failing this challenge... maybe it's not the right revision method..? or maybe she's just unlucky with what appears in the exams..?

interestingly, there is no more entries in her diary after a certain date, which considering she used to write in it at least every two weeks... is it a sign on something..? if so, what could it be?
