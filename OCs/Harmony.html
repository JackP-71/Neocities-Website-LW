Bio:

Harmony is an aspiring scientist... Even if she's lost in the void, she's still doing research in hopes that one day she'll reach civilisation... Secretly she hopes that she'll never find it due to her anxiety when meeting new people... and civilisation is said to be full of people...

As it stands, she is currently discovering parts of the void, with her twin sister Dissonance, and mapping out what she has discovered. She likes the solitude the void has, but also appreciates spending time with her sister.

when she isn't researching scientific stuff, another hobby Harmony has is singing, typically singing songs with melancholic lyrics... But Dissonance seems to like listening to her songs, and sometimes joins in.
