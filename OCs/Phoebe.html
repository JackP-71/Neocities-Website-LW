Bio:

Phoebe used to be one of the bullies at Hazelwood. However, unlike Maisie and Evie, she ended up changing her ways much earlier.

Phoebe seemed to be a good student at Hazelwood, until she became friends with Evie... how this even occured i don't know... because Evie doesn't seem like a very likeable person.

Ultimately when this happened, her grades seemed to drop and she didn't pay much attention to homework, going from handing it on time to a good standard nearly all the time to no longer doing it at all anymore.

This was one of the signs she had become one of the bullies.

However, this ultimately changed when Evie fell ill and she realised she would have to befriend one of the people who she bullied the most: Iris.

Upon taking time away from Evie, Phoebe realised that maybe things had gone a little bit wrong... looking at if from afar, she weanted those good grades after all. So, she set her mind on rectifying everything that had gone wrong even if that meant become friends with the person she had been bullying for the entire time she had been at Hazelwood for. Of course, this was after they had saved Evie from whatever illness ridiculed her.
