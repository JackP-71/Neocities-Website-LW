Bio:

Raven is Twilight's sister, she doesnt speak often, but tends to speak with her sister quite often. On occasion she'll speak to Ella, her sister's friend.

Because of this, she hasn't made many friends...

Near to her 12th birthday, it was found that the person in charge of the village was placing a supposed time limit on the citizens lives. This caused Raven to isolate herself as it essentially means that she had a few days left to live.

Thankfully with the help of Twilight and Ella, the three were able to stop the time limit before Raven's time limit ran out.
