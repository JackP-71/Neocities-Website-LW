Bio:

Twilight is an aspiring writer, although none of her books were ever published, she had some success uploading to a website where she gained quite a fanbase, eager for her next book or an update on an existing installment.

However this all came crashing down when, somehow, her school managed to come across her writing. At this point, she lost all interest in her writing as she aimed to keep it seperate from her personal life.

She mainly stayed to herself during this time, only really talking to her younger sister Raven and her friend Ella.

She eventually left the school when people started bullying over it and wanting to read it themselves.

However, whilst her new school was without issue, the village at which it was located was definitely an issue. An account she wrote on this said that the village leader (or that was what they called themselves), placed a tine limit upon their lives and when that time reached zero. Well... how can I put this? The end had been reached.

She then became dedicated to putting an end to this upon discovering her sister had a really short time limit, with her birthday coming up soon.
